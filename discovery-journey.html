🧭 The Discovery Journey

This program is built the Steve Blank way — by talking to real people before building for imagined ones. The internal playbook for that process lives in Discovery Process Overview; this page is the plain-language version for anyone invited to actually be part of it.

🚶 What Happens If You Join a Discovery Call

1️⃣ We listen

A short conversation about your actual study habits and pain points — not a sales call. See Interview Guide.

2️⃣ You try it

An invitation to the free Sunday cohort or a preview video, no pressure to pay.

3️⃣ You tell us the truth

What worked, what didn't — logged directly into this site's own tracker.

4️⃣ We change the product

Your feedback directly shapes what gets built next — see Evidence Map.
