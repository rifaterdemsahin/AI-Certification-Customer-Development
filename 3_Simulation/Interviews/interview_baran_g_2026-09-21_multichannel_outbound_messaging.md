## 1. Context
- Baran G was first logged on 2026-08-18: a working professional doing heavy manual Excel/tabular wrangling who moved to agents to process and visualise the data, and got his time back "with something more presentable".
- This follow-up captures a second, different pain from the same practitioner: not data processing, but **composing and sending the right written message to the right person, repeatedly, across several marketplaces**.
- The founder reports the feedback second-hand. There is no transcript, no quote captured verbatim, and no price or commitment attached. It is logged as directional evidence.

---

## 2. The Feedback (as reported)
- Baran wants to be able to **send messages on Airbnb and SpareRoom** to the people on those platforms — and specifically to send **proper, appropriate messages** rather than generic or badly-shaped ones.
- Read plainly: he has an inventory/hosting-side workflow with a continuous stream of inbound and outbound conversation, and the friction is **drafting contextually correct messages at volume** — the same class of repetitive, judgement-heavy messaging that consumes a working professional's evenings.
- Note the pattern vs his 2026-08-18 evidence: the 2026-08-18 pain was *tabular data* ("complex Excel was taking him so much time"); this one is *conversational outbound*. Both are repetitive, judgement-heavy, non-core tasks eating practitioner time — which is the same emotional driver, applied to a new domain (H24).

## 3. The Founder's Own Resulting Requirement
- On hearing it, the founder identified the same need in his own operation: **LinkedIn responses** — replying to the inbound stream of recruiter, contractor, and prospect messages with correct, context-aware replies, at volume.
- The founder names **Grokbot** (the Cursor-hosted computer-use agent) as the solution path for this class of work.
- Stated founder position: *this process has to work* — he has spent ~$50,000 on his contracting business, so the messaging/response layer is not an optional experiment.
- Stated founder requirement: **all the answers need to be there for contractors and for research** — i.e. the documented, reuseable answers must live in this project, not in ephemeral chat threads.

## 4. Mapping to the Product
1. **A real practitioner just described a channel we are not teaching yet.** Airbnb/SpareRoom messaging is a marketplace-operations workflow, adjacent to the SMB/sole-operator segment already spotted in the Amazon FBA discovery — same shape, different platform.
2. **The founder's LinkedIn requirement is the same primitive.** Multi-channel outbound where the model drafts, the human approves, and the reply is context-correct: one capability, several front-ends.
3. **The "answers must be there" requirement is a documentation mandate.** For contractors (Delivery Pilot) and for research, the repo is the durable answer store — this is a delivery-format requirement on us, not a customer discovery claim about Baran.
4. **Governance matters more than the draft.** This lands in the same lane as the Chidi pushback (agentic role constraints, automating manual LinkedIn browsing via research/writer agents): unsanctioned auto-sending is the risk; bounded drafting with human approval is the product.
