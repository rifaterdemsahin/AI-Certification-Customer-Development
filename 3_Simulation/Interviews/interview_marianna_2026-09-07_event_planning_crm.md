## 2. Core Operational & Strategic Syntheses

### A. Shift from Unplanned Frequency to High-Impact Bi-Weekly Cadence (H23 / H5)
- **Finding:** Running frequent, impromptu, or sub-optimally publicized live calls creates friction and attendee drop-off because working professionals cannot plan around ad-hoc calendars.
- **Solution:** Shift to **1 event every 2 weeks (bi-weekly)**, backed by rigorous 14-day advance notice and structured promotional runway.

### B. Standardized 8-Part Pedagogical Event Architecture (H29 / H30 / H8)
- Every cohort workshop/class must follow a standardized curriculum blueprint:
  1. **Name & Session #:** Clear semantic title and position in the transformation roadmap.
  2. **Description:** Concrete summary of the real-world problem being tackled.
  3. **Goal:** Specific outcome targeted during the session.
  4. **Results / Deliverables:** Tangible artifacts produced (e.g., deployed agent, Terraform script, mock exam score).
  5. **Skills Mastered:** Explicit technical capabilities unlocked (e.g., MCP server configuration, hybrid RAG).
  6. **Hands-on Exercises:** Interactive lab steps executed live using the 5-10-5 or 10-10-15 sprint model.
  7. **Workplace Application:** Clear operational guide on how attendees apply the artifact at their paid day-jobs/contracts on Monday morning.
  8. **Post-Session Recording & Assets:** High-value recap shared across LinkedIn and community hubs.
