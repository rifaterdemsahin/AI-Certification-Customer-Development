Discovery Process · Hypothesis Interview Questions

🧪 What to Ask to Test Each Hypothesis

The Interview Guide covers general pains/gains discovery questions. This page maps specific questions directly onto every hypothesis in the Hypothesis Tracker, so an interview can deliberately probe for evidence that would validate or kill a named claim — not just surface general frustration. Log what you hear on Interview Recording & Tracker, then update the matching hypothesis's status on Hypothesis Tracker and, if it changes the evidence picture, Evidence Map.

⚠️

STILL A DISCOVERY INTERVIEW, NOT A SURVEY

These questions are prompts to steer a conversation toward the right topic — ask them open-ended and in your own words, then let the customer talk. Never read a hypothesis statement aloud or ask "would you pay for X?" as a yes/no; per the Interview Guide's Listening Mandate, a leading question gets a polite lie, not evidence.

🔍 Stage 1: Customer Discovery

H1

Rising AI skills expectations

  • "Has your job description or performance review changed in the last year to mention AI tools or skills? How?"
  • "What's the last conversation you had with a manager or recruiter about needing an AI/cloud certification?"
Listen for: unprompted stories of role change or employer pressure — kills if nobody can point to a real conversation or requirement.
H2

Animated content beats standard formats

  • "Tell me about the last time you actually finished a technical video or course start to finish — what kept you watching?"
  • "When did you last stop watching a study video out of boredom or confusion? What was on screen at that moment?"
Listen for: specific visual/format complaints about dry material — kills if format was never the reason they dropped off.
H3

Audience will pay for cert prep

  • "What's the last course, book, or prep tool you paid for out of your own pocket? How much, and why that one?"
  • "Did you sign up for any waitlist or newsletter for something exam-related recently? What made you do it?"
Listen for: a real past purchase or signup — kills if every answer is "I'd probably pay for..." (hypothetical, not past behavior).

📈 Stage 2: Customer Validation

H4

YouTube funnel → ~1% paid membership

  • "Walk me through what you did right after watching a free study video that you liked — did you click anything, save it, come back later?"
  • "What would make you come back to the same channel or community every week instead of watching once and moving on?"
Listen for: a concrete next action taken after free content — kills if free content is consumed and instantly forgotten.
H5

Cohorts sell out organically (no guarantee)

  • "Have you ever joined a live study group or cohort before? How did you hear about it, and did you pay?"
  • "What would a live weekly session need to give you — besides passing the exam — to be worth showing up for every time?"
Listen for: value placed on live problem-solving/community, independent of any pass guarantee — per H8 Premise 3, never imply the exam pass itself is promised.
H6

Market is large enough (TAM/SAM/SOM)

  • "How many people on your team or in your network are also working toward an AI/cloud certification right now?"
  • "Where do people like you usually find out about a new certification or exam prep resource?"
Listen for: named communities/channels with real headcount — a qualitative proxy for SAM/SOM reach, not a substitute for the sizing study H6 still needs.
H7

YouTube funnel converts at stated rates

  • "What made you click through from a video description or comment into a newsletter or sign-up page, the last time you did that?"
  • "How many exam-prep newsletters or channels are you currently subscribed to, and which ones do you actually open?"
Listen for: what specifically triggers a click-through — kills if subscribers can't recall ever clicking through from a video.
H8

Cohort delivers exam-ready PMF

  • "Last time you studied with other people (in person or online), did it actually help you learn faster, or was it mostly social?"
  • "After a past study group ended, did you stay in touch with anyone from it? How?"
Listen for: real peer-accountability behavior (like the free cohort's organic WhatsApp group) — never state or imply a pass guarantee while asking this (H8 Premise 3).

⚡ Stage 3: Customer Creation

H9

$10k / 2-launch exit gate

  • "If you'd already paid for one cohort, what would make you pay again for a second one rather than trying to study solo the second time?"
Listen for: repeat-purchase reasoning — this hypothesis is mostly tested by real revenue across two launches, not a single interview, but this question surfaces retention intent early.
H10

>40% retention = MVP quality floor

  • "Think of the specific point where you stopped watching one of our videos, if you did — what was happening on screen right then?"
Listen for: a concrete drop-off moment (confusing pacing, unclear diagram) — this is qualitative color; the real metric comes from YouTube Studio Analytics, tracked in content-analysis.html.
H13

1,000x subscriber growth

  • "What made you hit subscribe on a channel like this, versus just watching one video and leaving?"
  • "Have you ever shared or recommended a study video to a coworker or classmate? What made you do it?"
Listen for: the specific subscribe/share trigger — that's the lever this metric depends on compounding.
H12

B2B / consulting-firm channel

  • "Does your employer reimburse or budget for certification training? Who approves that, and what do they ask for?"
  • "Has your company ever bought training seats in bulk for a team, rather than individuals expensing it themselves?"
Listen for: a named approver and a real reimbursement/bulk-purchase process — this is the question to bring to the 3–5 Claude Partner Network firms H12 recommends contacting.
H14

Multi-certification expansion

  • "Besides Claude/Anthropic, which other AI or cloud certifications are you currently working toward or considering?"
Listen for: real, current multi-vendor study activity — kills if Claude is the only certification anyone mentions unprompted.
H16

Paid ads, post-validation only

  • "Have you ever clicked a paid/sponsored ad for a certification course? What made you click, and did you buy anything after?"
Listen for: what ad content/placement actually earns a click — informs which channel to capped-test first once H4/H7 produce organic-funnel baselines.
H17

Onsite Cambridge + corporate pilot

  • "Would an in-person version of this session be worth traveling for? What would make that trip worth it versus joining online?"
Listen for: specific in-person value (networking, focus, accountability) beyond the online cohort — ask Marianna-side contacts directly about corporate onsite procurement.
H18

International onsite delivery

  • "If this training existed in your country/office, who would be the person that decides whether to book it?"
Listen for: a real decision-maker and geography — this hypothesis is explicitly downstream of H17's pilot, so treat answers as early signal, not a green light.

🏢 Stage 4: Company Building

H11

$100k ARR → hiring threshold

  • "As a student, would you notice or care if support, editing, or scheduling were handled by someone other than the founder?"
Listen for: whether delegated support would improve or hurt perceived quality — this hypothesis is mainly tested by a cost model (acidity Finding F7), not customer interviews.
H15

Founder goes full-time at Stage 4

  • N/A — this is a founder decision about his own employment, not a customer-facing claim. Nothing to ask customers here.
Not customer-testable. Tracked instead via the founder's own runway/cost-of-living model.
