🤝 Accepted Responsibility

Not every risk gets mitigated. Some are consciously accepted because the cost of mitigating them now exceeds the benefit at this stage. Rifat Erdem Sahin has explicitly chosen to accept the following:

🧑‍💻 Founder-as-single-point-of-failure
All research, scripting, animation, and cohort teaching runs through one person. Accepted rather than mitigated (e.g. by hiring early) because Stage 1–3 needs speed and low overhead more than redundancy; hiring is deferred until the $100k ARR trigger (see Company Building). See Single-Founder Bandwidth for the actual weekly time ledger behind this risk.
📺 YouTube platform dependency
Distribution relies almost entirely on YouTube's organic algorithm rather than paid, owned channels. Accepted because it delivers near-zero-cost distribution ($0 CAC target, see Funnel Spec) that a self-funded solo operation cannot otherwise afford.
💰 Unsourced TAM/SAM/SOM precision
The exact 25M/3M/50k figures are treated as a working estimate, not a validated study (see H6 in Hypothesis Tracker). Accepted because a dedicated sizing study costs more time than the decisions currently riding on the number justify.
🎯 Single-niche exam focus (Claude Certified Architect)
Betting the curriculum on one certification program rather than diversifying across multiple vendors early. Accepted to keep the MVP focused and avoid diluting production time — see Focus for the explicit scope decision.
