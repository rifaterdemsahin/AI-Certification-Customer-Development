📋 Full Detail

  • PMF:Product-Market Fit — the point at which an offer satisfies real, strong market demand well enough that customers seek it out, get value from it, and recommend it on their own, without being sold.
  • P1:The cohort curriculum covers the full scope of the target certification, delivered live.
  • P2:Structured live instruction and peer accountability outperform self-study for exam readiness.
  • P3:The company does not grant, administer, or guarantee the certification itself — Anthropic alone issues it via Pearson VUE.
  • Conclusion:Cohort graduates will pass the target exam at a high rate and recommend the program to peers.
  • Target Metric:>80% internal pass rate; NPS >50; organic LinkedIn badge sharing (all internal, non-promised).
📅 Hypothesized
