# 🚀 AI Certification Helper - Customer Development Portal

This repository contains the interactive Customer Development and business validation blueprints for Rifat Erdem Sahin's **AI Certification Helper**, designed under Steve Blank's customer development framework.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective & Key Results (OKRs)

### 📌 Core Objective
Validate the problem-solution fit and establish a repeatable sales pipeline for highly-animated technical training guides that help developers pass AI certifications efficiently, moving from a solo side-hustle to a scaled, automated operation.

### 📈 Key Results
1. **Interview Base:** Complete **30 high-yield customer interviews** intercepting developers at watering holes (e.g., Triton Square, Venture Coffee, Discord cohorts) without pitching the solution, to map out candidate pains and gains.
2. **Agile Validation Gate:** Reach a exit-gate threshold of **$10,000 (USD)** in gross revenue from a single course launch before leaving Stage 2 (Customer Validation).
3. **Engagement Metric:** Achieve **&ge; 10% Click-Through-Rate (CTR)** from YouTube Courses to newsletter signups, and **1,000x subscriber growth** per published video in Stage 3.
4. **Hiring/Scale Target (Stage 4):** Achieve a **$100,000/year ARR** run-rate triggering delegation of operations to:
   - **Freelance Video Editor:** expected by **2028** to handle animation and vocal leveling.
   - **Cohort Support Coordinator:** proposed to be **Mariana** to manage calendars, calendar invites, and onboarding.
   - **Technical TAs:** recruited directly from the **invested audience** to support students 1-on-1.
