💎 Offer Tiers: Membership vs. Cohort

Low Ticket: YouTube Membership ($10/mo)

Provides general access to practice mock exams, student badges, community channels, and study materials.

*Access style: Regular members just watch the live stream broadcasts.

High Ticket: Paid Cohort ($250 – $500)

Intensive bootcamp style sessions with hands-on architectural reviews, direct mock-exam feedback, and 1-on-1 assistance.

✨ Key distinction: Paid cohort members get to share their screens for active troubleshooting, whereas regular members just watch the live stream.
