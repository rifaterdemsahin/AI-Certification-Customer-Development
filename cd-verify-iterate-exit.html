🔎 What This Is

Steve Blank's Customer Discovery is a loop, not a straight line: if all three checks pass, persevere into Customer Validation. If one fails, iterate — revise the specific hypothesis that broke and re-run the loop from Step 1. If the core premise is fundamentally wrong, exit and stop investing further.

🎯 Applied to AI Certification Helper

Applied, concretely:

  • ✅Persevere: all three checks pass → proceed to Stage 2: Customer Validation.
  • 🔁Iterate: e.g. retention stays below 40% → revisit the Product Hypothesis; conversion stays below 5% → revisit the Distribution & Pricing Hypothesis.
  • 🛑Exit: the problem itself doesn't hold up across multiple independent conversations — stop before committing more time as a second job.
