🌙 New Field Evidence: The Sunday Cohort

The free weekly cohort runs Sundays, 9–11pm, and is doing double duty: it's both the live delivery/feedback loop behind H4, and the venue where the discovery interviews recommended by the acidity check are actually being run.

  • 🕘Attendance itself is evidence: people are giving up a Sunday-night rest window to attend, a costlier signal of real demand than a survey response or a "yes" to a hypothetical price question — direct support for H1 and H3.
  • 🎤The 10–15 discovery interviews the acidity check recommended (Recommended Action #3, Acidity Check v1.1) are being collected inside these same weekly sessions, rather than as a separate outreach effort — see Test Plan for the cadence.
  • 📊8 weeks, ~4 attendees/session is the dataset behind H4's upgrade to "In Testing" — thin, but real and repeated, not a one-off.
  • ⚖️Note: gemini.md's original qualitative exit criterion calls for 30 total customer interviews before finalizing MVP themes; the 10–15 figure here is the near-term batch being collected through this specific Sunday-cohort channel, not a lowered target.

🆕 Founder Field Notes (2026-08-04)

A fresh batch of founder notes, captured via the site's Workspace Notes feature directly against the H1–H9 cards on Hypothesis Tracker. Full writeups are in HYPOTHESIS.md v1.7.0.

  • ⚔️H1: Anthropic, Google, and Microsoft have all independently shipped or expanded AI/LLM certification programs in 2026 (Claude Certified Architect, Gemini certifications, Azure AI credentials). Multiple well-capitalized competitors racing into the same category is corroborating evidence the underlying skills-gap trend is real — see Competitive Analysis for the positioning implications.
  • 🎬H2: First real single-video data point — a produced animated short scored 68.5% average view retention on YouTube Studio Analytics, clearing the >40% target. One video, not yet a catalog-wide average (that's H10's bar), but the first concrete proof the format works. Tracked going forward on the new Content Analysis page.
  • 📣H5: The current (still-free) cohort's actual channel mechanics: live every Sunday at 9pm, marketed at $0 spend via WhatsApp and face-to-face outreach. The founder reports this builds content, collects feedback, and increases his own confidence ahead of a paid launch.
  • 🧑‍💻H6: The "Forward Deployed Engineer" (FDE) role — a concrete, named embodiment of this hypothesis's SAM segment — is growing explosively: job postings up 700–800%+ year-over-year, ~2,100% growth projected by end of 2026, and Google, Deloitte, Salesforce, OpenAI, and Anthropic all named as fast-growing hirers.
  • 🤝H8: The free Sunday cohort audience is already self-organizing on WhatsApp between sessions — a live, tested peer-accountability signal, not just a hoped-for design goal.
  • 🚪H9: The founder independently reconfirmed the double-validation requirement — the model must be shown working twice, not once, before going all-in on Customer Creation — matching the 2-launch gate already recorded in Validation Repeat Gate.

H3, H4, and H7 notes from this same batch were action items rather than evidence — tracked as open tasks on the new Task List page instead.
