🔎 What This Is

The Product Hypothesis states what the product actually is and why its specific form is the thing that solves the customer's problem — not just that a solution exists in general.

🎯 Applied to AI Certification Helper

Hypothesis: Short, visually animated video lessons that distill dense AI/cloud certification syllabi into fast, high-retention content will teach the material better than existing dry manuals or 40-hour slide-deck courses — and a live cohort adds structured practice + community on top of that.

  • 🎬MVP form: animated YouTube lessons (see MVP Spec).
  • 📈Tied directly to H2 in the Hypothesis Tracker: animated content beats standard formats on watch retention.
  • 🏛️Layered product: free videos → $10/mo membership → $250–500 live cohort (see Value Proposition).
🚦 What Would Falsify This
If published lessons can't sustain >40% average watch retention (the MVP quality floor — see Quality Gates), the product form itself is wrong and needs to change before any pricing or funnel work matters.
