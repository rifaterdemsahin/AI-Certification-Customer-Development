## 1. Executive Summary & Core Customer Insight

### "Value to Business Over Paper Credentials"
Apo's customer discovery interview provides decisive validation for the core positioning of AI Certification Helper and Delivery Pilot:
* **The Paper Credential Myth:** Apo explicitly stated he is **not interested in a certification for its own sake**—he is interested exclusively in the **tangible operational and financial uplift** AI tools bring to his existing e-commerce (Amazon FBA) business.
* **The 100-Tab Fragmentation:** Before AI adoption, his workflow suffered from severe fragmentation: over 100 open browser tabs, manual copy-pasting, and information overload. He even purchased physical hardware devices to manage the cognitive clutter.
* **Autonomous Script Uplift:** Using Claude, Apo wrote 5 custom automation programs that directly increased his revenue-generating capacity.
* **Post-Hype Pragmatism:** After 2 years of AI market hype, active practitioners are cutting through the noise to focus on concrete, revenue-generating tools.
