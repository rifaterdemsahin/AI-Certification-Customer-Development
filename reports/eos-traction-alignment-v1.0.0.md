## Verdict in one line

You already have **more written vision than EOS asks for**, and almost **none of the Traction machinery** (3–7 quarterly Rocks with a single owner, a 5–15 number weekly Scorecard, a same-day Level 10 meeting, IDS, an Accountability Chart of seats). *Vision without traction is hallucination.* The 272-page site is the hallucination risk. Three named Rocks plus a 45-minute weekly self Level 10 is the fix.

## What the book actually is

EOS is an operating system for a **10–250 person** entrepreneurial company. Six components must be strong together: **Vision, People, Data, Issues, Process, Traction**. Wickman says five tools produce ~80% of the result, in this order:

1. Accountability Chart  
2. Rocks  
3. Meeting Pulse (Level 10)  
4. Scorecard  
5. V/TO (Vision/Traction Organizer)

Process documentation and “everyone has a number” come after those five.

This business is **one founder + a free Skool room**, not a leadership team. Do **not** hire an EOS Implementer, Integrator, or staff until H9 ($10,000 from two consecutive paid launches) and later H11 ($100k ARR) fire. Adapt the five tools for a solo founder. Do not wait until you have 10 employees to start.
