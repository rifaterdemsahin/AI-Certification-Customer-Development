## Operational Highlights & Strategic Logic

1. **Standard (Free) Includes Full Replays**:
   - Generously unlocking session replays on the Free tier builds massive word-of-mouth trust and frictionless onboarding for community members.
2. **Premium ($1/mo, Late $10) Unlocks Interactive Cohort + AI Certification Classrooms**:
   - Positions the $1 handshake around live 2-hour interactive group discussions, case practice, and dedicated certification classrooms.
3. **VIP ($250/yr, Late $1,000) Unlocks "The Seat" & Saturday 1-1s**:
   - Gives active stage/screen-share rights on Sundays, direct founder review, and dedicated Saturday 1-1 time blocks (Environment setup, AI Security, Human in the Loop agent architectures).
