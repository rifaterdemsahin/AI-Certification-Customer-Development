# TAM/SAM/SOM Verification Assessment (Perplexity Research)

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Date:** 2026-08-05
**Source:** Independent verification research run by the founder in Perplexity, checking the TAM/SAM/SOM figures published in `comp-market.html` (25M / 3M / 50k) and the H6 hypothesis in `HYPOTHESIS.md` against real-world market data.
**Purpose:** Give H6 a formal, layer-by-layer verification verdict — which of TAM, SAM, and SOM are backed by real data vs. which remain working assumptions — rather than treating the whole hypothesis as a single pass/fail claim.

## Hypothesis Verification Assessment

### Verified vs. Unverified Status

The research correctly separates the hypothesis into its three market layers rather than scoring it as one number:

| Market Layer | Stated Claim | Verification Status | Real-World Grounding |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TAM** | ~25M developers must adapt to AI engineering | ⚠️ **Unverified / Directional Proxy** | **Working Assumption.** No single study cites exactly 25M AI-adapting developers, but it aligns with global developer counts (e.g., Evans Data estimates ~26M–28M professional developers worldwide) and macro workforce re-skilling trends. |
| **SAM** | ~3M seeking Cloud/ML/LLM certifications | **Verified Market Signal (via FDE Trend)** | **Strongly Grounded.** The 3M figure itself is a logical proxy, but the underlying market behavior — enterprises creating and funding AI deployment roles at scale — is fully verified by market data (see below). |
| **SOM** | ~50k reachable via channels in 3 years | ⚠️ **Unverified / Internal Projection** | **Operational Estimate.** Depends entirely on channel execution and partner pipeline conversions — no external data can verify a founder-specific reach projection. |

## Key Verified Findings Supporting the SAM Premise

The Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) data points already cited in `reports/ai-adoption-and-skills-gap-v1.0.md` and `HYPOTHESIS.md`'s H6 entry match independently-checked empirical market reports:

- **Posting Growth:** Indeed data confirms US FDE job postings grew **729% year-over-year** (643 in April 2025 → 5,330 in April 2026).
- **Demand Explosion:** Executive search firm Christian & Timbers reports enterprise demand for FDEs is projected to **surge ~2,100% by end of 2026**, with enterprise hiring plans expanding from ~5–10% of companies at the start of the year to **~70% by mid-2026**.
- **Enterprise Commitment:** Frontier labs and cloud hyperscalers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Cloud, Salesforce, AWS) are aggressively building out dedicated FDE and deployment organizations.
- **High-Value Trajectory:** Compensation data confirms mid-to-senior FDE salaries range from **$170k base to $385k–$600k+ total compensation**, evidencing high enterprise budget priority for practitioners with verified AI/cloud integration skills.

## Verdict

> **H6 is a Partially Verified Hypothesis.** The *macro trend* (SAM expansion via FDEs and enterprise AI upskilling) is empirically verified by real market data. The exact numbers (25M / 3M / 50k) remain working assumptions that serve as plausible orders of magnitude rather than audited Census metrics.

This does not upgrade H6's tracked status in `HYPOTHESIS.md` beyond its existing ⚠️ Claimed-unverified-now-partially-grounded tier — the hypothesis's own conclusion is the SOM figure (~50k reachable), and that specific number is explicitly still unverified per this same research. What this assessment adds is a clearer, layer-by-layer account of *which part* of H6 is grounded (SAM, via the FDE trend) and which parts remain founder assumptions (TAM, SOM) — sharper than treating H6 as one undifferentiated claim.

## Recommendation for `comp-market.html`

1. Frame the TAM/SAM/SOM numbers as an **"Order-of-Magnitude Working Model"** anchored by verified market behavior, not as audited figures.
2. Use the FDE data as the primary **"Proof of Intent"** evidence — proof that enterprise budgets are actively flowing into training and hiring engineers with cloud, LLM, and MLOps capabilities, even though the specific 25M/3M/50k figures aren't independently auditable.

See `comp-market.html`'s "Formula Grounding" section for where this is applied, and `HYPOTHESIS.md`'s H6 entry for the tracked hypothesis status.
