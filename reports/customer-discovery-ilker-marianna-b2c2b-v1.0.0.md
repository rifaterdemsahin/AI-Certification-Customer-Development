## 1. Executive Summary

On August 28, 2026, founder Rifat Erdem Sahin held an advisory and customer discovery strategy session with **Ilker and Marianna**. The session crystalized three essential pillars of the AI Certification Helper go-to-market strategy and economic model:

1. **The B2C2B Business Model Engine:** The overall model is structured as **B2C2B (Business-to-Consumer-to-Business)**.
   - Individual practitioners (B2C) enter via free YouTube courses, proctored certification roadmaps, and the Skool community.
   - **Erdem's Insight on Progressive "2B" Realization:** Enterprise transition ("2B") executes gradually:
     1. First, engaging **Small and Mid-Sized Enterprises (SMEs)** as agile pilot clients for rapid AI implementations (£3k–£10k POCs).
     2. Second, pivoting to **Large Enterprises (Fortune 500 / Global Consultancies)** by **sourcing them certified, battle-tested Delivery Pilots (Forward Deployed Engineers)** at £500–£1,000+/day day rates.
2. **The Core Message (Positive AI Skills Elevation):** Rapid AI capability advancement is dramatically raising enterprise skill expectations. The marketing and pedagogical narrative must be **relentlessly positive**—framing AI not as a threat that replaces developers, but as a superhuman leverage multiplier that elevates engineers into high-earning AI Cockpit Pilots.
3. **The Community Value Proposition (Certifications + Custom Apps):** Clarifying what community members truly get out of the ecosystem. While **vendor certifications** (Pearson VUE / Anthropic / Microsoft) act as critical external gatekeeper keys for HR and clients, the true compounding wealth is the **functional custom AI applications members build for themselves** (Obsidian Second Brains, agent workflows, MCP servers, automation tools).
