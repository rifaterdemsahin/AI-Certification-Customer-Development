## Verdict

Keep the three prices. Change the three names. Cap what VIP promises.

The ladder is Freemium with the right shape — magnet / tripwire / offer. The live names (Standard / Premium / VIP) sell status, not proximity to Sunday. The VIP copy now also sells 1:1 founder time at $250/year, which a second-job founder cannot deliver if more than a handful of people buy.

|---|---|---|---|

| Premium | $1 / month | Additional recorded workshops + listen-only Sunday live | **Sit In** |

---

## What is working

1. **Freemium is the right community model for Stage 2.** Strangers can enter without a card. That is how a preview room is supposed to work (see H5 Premise 4 — the free Sunday already proved people will show up).
2. **$1 is a handshake, not a business.** It is a Hormozi tripwire so they raise a hand for this Sunday. It is not revenue. It is not the cohort. Do not dress it up as Premium.
3. **$250/year is the only paid-enrollment SKU on this page.** Only a cleared VIP / Share Screen payment counts toward H5 and H9. $0 and $1 joins stay community signups, same bar as the current members.
4. **The entitlement *intent* is correct.** Free = see the room. $1 = listen. $250 = your screen, reviewed. That is Peek → Sit In → Share Screen, just wearing the wrong labels.
5. **This does not replace the revenue model.** Premise 3 stays $10/mo recorded + $250 (3-month live) / $500 (year live). This ladder is the join-page experiment so the first paid conversion is an easy yes.

## What to change

### 1. Rename Standard / Premium / VIP — do this tonight

Standard / Premium / VIP is a catalog. Peek / Sit In / Share Screen is an offer. A stranger should be able to repeat one sentence: *“Every Sunday at 9pm we solve Claude Architect problems live. Peek free. Sit in for $1. Share your screen for $250/year.”*

Paste-ready names and descriptions already live on `5_Symbols/product/skool-delivery-pilot-offer.html`. The live storefront has not been renamed yet.

Do not let Premium own the word “cohort.” The live page has tagged Premium with “Cohorts.” That trains the market that the gym floor is a dollar.

### 2. Tighten what Standard gives away

“Learning materials” on the free tier is too wide. If Standard already includes the Classroom archive, Premium has nothing left except listen-only Sunday — and Peek is no longer a preview, it is the library.

Gate it:

- **Peek ($0):** welcome + next Sunday on the calendar + **one** sample replay or 10-minute clip + read the board. Live join locked. Screen locked.
- **Sit In ($1/mo):** Sunday listen-only + more recorded workshops + post/reply.
- **Share Screen ($250/year):** screen-share + founder review of *their* setup on Sunday + full Classroom.

If Peek is locked empty, people bounce. If Peek is the whole library, nobody upgrades.

### 3. Cap VIP so it does not sell unbounded founder time

The live VIP line now promises **personal architecture assistance** and **founder setup reviews** at $250/year (~$4.80/week).

Group Sunday screen-share is the scarce, still-scalable thing. Open-ended 1:1 mentorship is not. Rifat runs this as a second job (`single-founder-bandwidth.html`). Ten VIP buyers asking for personal architecture help will eat the week.

Rewrite VIP / Share Screen as:

- Share your screen **on Sunday** (the seat).
- Founder review of **your setup, live, in the room**.
- Not on-demand Slack/WhatsApp architecture consulting.
- Not a private weekly 1:1.

If a buyer wants more 1:1 than Sunday, that is a later SKU — not this $250 experiment.

### 4. Do not let $250/year become the permanent year price

Premise 3’s yearly live seat is **$500**. Live VIP at $250/year is a 50% Stage 2 easy-yes. Fine as the first paid conversion. Fatal if it silently replaces the revenue model on `bmc-revenue-streams.html`.

Keep the experiment labeled as an experiment. When the first Share Screen seats convert, decide whether $250/year stays a founding-member price or steps up to the $500 year / $250 three-month pair.

## What this is not

- Not a Grand Slam Offer. The Hormozi agent still wants a named Architect Exam Sprint once the mock exists (H27).
- Not $10/mo YouTube/exam-prep membership. That SKU is still the recorded-replay tier in Premise 3. It is not on this Skool join page, and should not be — three prices is enough.
- Not the $29 Exam Prep Bundle (H21). Do not add a fourth card here.
- Not paid enrollment. Confirming Freemium entitlements does not move H5 to ✅ or H9 at all.
- Not “get certified.” The About tab still must not promise the badge. Anthropic issues that via Pearson VUE.
