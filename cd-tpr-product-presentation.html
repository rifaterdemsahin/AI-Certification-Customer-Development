🔎 What This Is

Product Presentation is demoing the actual MVP — not a mockup or description of it — to prospects and collecting structured reactions to the real thing, including price.

🎯 Applied to AI Certification Helper

Applied: Showing prospects the real pricing page — $10/mo membership, $250–500 live cohort — alongside a sample video, and measuring email/newsletter signup conversion.

  • 🎯Target: >5% email signup conversion (H3 on the Hypothesis Tracker).
  • 📐Experiment design detail in Test & Metrics.
🚦 What A Pass Looks Like
Signups happen after seeing the real price, not just interest expressed before price was shown — that gap is where most product hypotheses quietly fail.
