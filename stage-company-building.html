🏗️ Scaling Milestones & Operational Delegation

Operational Scaling ($100k ARR Trigger)

Once Rifat Erdem Sahin's AI Certification Helper reaches $100,000 in annual revenue, it transitions from a solo operation into a structured corporate organization. This milestone triggers key hires to maintain student quality and scale curriculum velocity.

🎬 Hire 1: Professional Video Editor
Rifat delegates video rendering, audio tuning, asset organization, and graphic animation. This frees Rifat to focus strictly on researching new AI certification blueprints and writing code examples.
🗂️ Hire 2: Cohort Support Coordinator
Oversees cohort scheduling, handles intake forms, processes refunds, sends homework reminders, and manages member subscriptions to ensure a seamless experience.
🛠️ Hire 3: Technical Support TAs (1-1 Support)
Qualified assistants (often top alumni of previous Claude Architect cohorts) hired part-time to help students 1-on-1 with deployment scripts, API configurations, and curriculum questions.
