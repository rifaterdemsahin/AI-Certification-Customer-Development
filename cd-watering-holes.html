💬 Outreach Scripts (Qualitative Engagement)

Use these structured scripts to initiate conversations. Always customize them with details of the candidate's focus area (e.g. AWS, Kubernetes, Claude).

Template A: Teams / Slack / LinkedIn Outreach (Professional)

Ideal for connecting with colleagues or warm developer contacts.

"Hi [Name], I noticed you have been preparing for the AI Architect exam / technical certifications. I'm currently running a brief qualitative study on what makes study guides dry versus effective for busy developers. I am not selling anything—just doing a 10-minute research project on study timelines and frustration pain points. Would you be open to sharing your experience over a quick virtual coffee?"

Template B: X (Twitter) / Social Media DM (Casual & Direct)

Best for digital cold outreach to candidates sharing exam preparation updates.

"Hey [Name]! Saw your tweet about studying for the Claude AI Architect exam. I'm doing research on developer certification study pains (focusing on visual vs text learning). Not pitching anything, just trying to understand what sucks most about prep. Do you have 5 mins for a quick chat? Cheers!"

Template C: Face-to-Face Intercept Script (Triton Square / Coffee shop)

Designed to approach developers casually holding papers or reading tablets during lunch or coffee meetups.

"Hi! Excuse me, sorry to interrupt. I saw you were looking at cloud architecture diagrams / study guides. I'm Rifat, and I'm currently conducting customer discovery interviews on why technical training material is so hard to digest. Are you currently studying for a certification? I'd love to ask you two quick questions about your study experience—I promise I'm not selling anything!"
