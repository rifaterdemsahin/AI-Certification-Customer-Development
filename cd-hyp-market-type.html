🔎 What This Is

Steve Blank defines four market types — existing, re-segmented, new, or clone — because the type determines strategy: existing markets compete on features, new markets require educating customers from scratch, re-segmented markets carve a niche out of an existing one.

🎯 Applied to AI Certification Helper

Hypothesis: This is a re-segmented niche of the existing cloud/tech certification-prep market — not a brand-new market. Certification prep already exists (AWS, GCP, etc.); the niche is AI-specific certification (Claude Certified Architect – Professional) delivered through an animated-video + live-cohort format.

  • 🎯TAM/SAM/SOM sizing in Market & Audience Spec (H6): ~25M / 3M / 50k.
  • 🏗️Existing-market strategy implication: compete on format/experience (animation + live cohort), not on inventing category awareness from zero.
🚦 What Would Falsify This
If prospects don't already search for or recognize "certification prep" as a category, this is actually a new market requiring category-education spend — a much slower, more expensive strategy than assumed.
