🔎 What This Is

Problem Presentation means describing the problem hypothesis to a prospect in plain terms and watching their reaction — deliberately withholding the product/solution so the response measures problem urgency, not pitch persuasiveness.

🎯 Applied to AI Certification Helper

Applied: Presenting the framing from Problem-Solution Fit Spec — "rising AI skill expectations at work" plus "existing cert-prep material is dry and slow" — and listening for whether the prospect immediately relates or has to be convinced.

  • 🗣️No product mentioned yet — see Focus for the single problem statement being tested.
  • 📝Notes feed directly back into H1/H2 on the Hypothesis Tracker.
🚦 What A Pass Looks Like
Prospects describe their own version of the problem unprompted, rather than agreeing politely once it's suggested to them.
