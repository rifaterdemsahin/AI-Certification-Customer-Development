The YouTube Courses Acquisition Strategy

Why YouTube Courses?

Instead of spending high budgets on advertising, the AI Certification Helper leverages YouTube's dedicated "Courses" functionality.

This specialized tab structures playlists into sequentially organized academic curricula. The algorithm ranks structured playlists higher for educational keywords, driving free, high-intent traffic.

1
AWARENESS: Animated YouTube Lessons

Casual viewers search "Claude AI Architect Prep" and discover our highly polished, animated lesson series.

2
INTEREST: YouTube Course Structured Tab

Viewers click "YouTube Course" to view the structured chapters, downloading free worksheets and exam blueprints in the description.

3
DECISION: YouTube Membership ($10/mo) or Newsletter

Students join YouTube membership for badges and community access, or join Rifat's newsletter for cohort announcements.

4
ACTION: Premium Cohort Enrollment ($250 - $500)

Highly committed students join the 4-8 week cohorts to secure 1-1 assistance and guaranteed cert preparation.
