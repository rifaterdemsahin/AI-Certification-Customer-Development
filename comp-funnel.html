📺 The YouTube Courses Acquisition Strategy

Why YouTube Courses?

Instead of spending high budgets on advertising, the AI Certification Helper leverages YouTube's dedicated "Courses" functionality.

This specialized tab structures playlists into sequentially organized academic curricula. The algorithm ranks structured playlists higher for educational keywords, driving free, high-intent traffic.

Platform Decision: Hosting on platforms like Thinkific has a $50/month cost which cannot be justified for this project, especially since Thinkific does not organically generate traffic or revenue.

1
👀 AWARENESS: Animated YouTube Lessons

Casual viewers search "Claude AI Architect Prep" and discover our highly polished, animated lesson series.

2
🤔 INTEREST: YouTube Course Tab & LinkedIn Organic Engagement

Viewers discover structured chapters and click to download free worksheets. Alongside YouTube, Rifat uses LinkedIn organic posts to generate interest, direct traffic, and build authority. Connect with Rifat on LinkedIn.

3
🤝 DECISION: YouTube Membership ($10/mo) or Newsletter

Students join YouTube membership for badges and community access, or sign up for Rifat's email newsletter (live at rifaterdemsahin.github.io/email-newsletter) for cohort announcements. Two separate capture points feed the same email list — one linked from LinkedIn posts, one linked from YouTube video descriptions — reaching two different audiences (Rifat's professional LinkedIn network vs. organic YouTube search traffic) and acting as backups for each other if either single channel underperforms or a platform link breaks.

4
🎓 ACTION: Premium Cohort Enrollment ($250 - $500)

Highly committed students join live-streamed cohort sessions for real-time problem solving, 1-1 assistance, and a supportive study network — preparation and discipline, not a guaranteed pass.
