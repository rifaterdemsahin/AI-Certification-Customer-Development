The Transition Gateways

Gate 1: $10,000 Validation Revenue (Stage 2 Exit)

Before any customer creation or company-building activity begins, Customer Validation (Stage 2) must independently produce $10,000 (USD) worth of validated, paying customers. This work is done solely by Rifat, with no hires yet, specifically to keep the validation loop agile and cheap to run.

"Revenue is the ultimate customer validation indicator."

We calculate this as gross cohort enrollments within a single launch period. Generating $10k verifies that both the message and product are positioned correctly in the target market, and is the explicit exit gate from Stage 2 into Stage 3 — not a milestone inside Stage 3 itself.

Gate 2: 1,000x Subscriber Growth Per Video (Stage 3 Focus)

Once Stage 2 is validated, the Customer Creation metric we are actually optimizing for is channel growth, not retention: each published video is expected to move the channel toward 1,000x its current subscriber baseline.

"Subscriber growth proves demand creation is working, not just content quality."

Baseline as of this writing: ~30 subscribers on the channel. The >40% average-view-retention figure used during MVP/PMF testing remains a content-quality floor, but it is no longer the primary Stage 3 gate — subscriber growth per video is.
