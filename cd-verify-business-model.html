🔎 What This Is

Verifying the business model means confirming people actually pay, at the hypothesized price and through the hypothesized channel — the hardest of the three checks, because interest and intent are not the same as revenue.

🎯 Applied to AI Certification Helper

Applied: >5% newsletter/pricing-page conversion (H3) actually observed, plus real progress toward the $10,000 Stage 2 revenue gate (H9) — not just projected.

  • ⚠️The Hypothesis Tracker flags that H4 and H7's funnel math don't yet reconcile — this check is also where that gets resolved with real numbers.
  • 💰Gate detail: Quality Gates, Business Model Spec.
🚦 Pass / Fail
Pass: real paying customers at or near the target conversion rate. Fail: interest without payment — revisit the Distribution & Pricing Hypothesis, not the product.
