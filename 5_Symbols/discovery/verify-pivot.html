Making the Decision

At this stage, we compare the measured results against our initial hypotheses. If the retention target (≥40%) and signup conversion (≥5%) are met, we verify our Discovery and transition to Customer Validation. Otherwise, we pivot.

Verify (Proceed)

All target metrics match. We proceed to Stage 2 (Customer Validation) to test the cohort sales structure, price elasticity, and long-term marketing funnel plans.

Pivot (Adapt)

If retention is <40%, we pivot. Pivoting may include: changing the video graphics style, updating curriculum complexity, changing the targeted certification, or adjusting script writing pacing.

⚠️ Sanity Check: Missing Questions & Risks

Missing Questions:

  • What is the exit criteria for calling a pivot a failure?
  • How many pivot iterations are allowed before shutting down the project?

Key Risks:

  • Sunk Cost Fallacy: The founder might refuse to pivot even if metrics are poor, continuing to invest time and energy into boring video styles.
  • Premature Validation: Proceeding to validation too quickly because of a single video outlier with high views, without establishing general curriculum interest.
