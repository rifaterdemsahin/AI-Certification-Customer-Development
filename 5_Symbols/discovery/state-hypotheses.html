Formulating Core Assumptions

Before "getting out of the building," Steve Blank's framework requires that the founder writes down all starting hypotheses on the Business Model Canvas.

Value Proposition Assumptions

We assume that developers need short, visually structured animations rather than standard text guides to pass AI Architect exams. We assume the certification has value in the job market.

Distribution & Pricing Assumptions

We assume organic search on YouTube is enough to reach buyers. We assume students will pay $10/mo for access to mock question explanations.

⚠️ Sanity Check: Missing Questions & Risks

Missing Questions:

  • Has the exact syllabus breakdown for Claude AI Architect Professional been mapped?
  • Are there competitor channels offering similar visual content for free?

Key Risks:

  • Assumption Bias: The founder might assume people love animations when they might actually prefer quick textual code cheatsheets.
  • Market Size Risk: The Claude AI Architect exam is relatively new; the initial addressable market might be too small to sustain a $10k cohort launch.
