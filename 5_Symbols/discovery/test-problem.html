Testing the Problem

This step involves interviewing customers to understand if the rising AI skills expectation is a genuine and painful problem. We must assess if developers are actively seeking certifications to solve it.

Interview Guidelines

Do not pitch the solution yet. Ask questions like: "How are you staying ahead of the current AI shifts?", "Have you attempted any AI certifications?", and "What was the most frustrating part of your prep?"

Success Indicators

The problem is validated if over 70% of interviewees rate "staying ahead of AI architectural shifts" as a top 3 career challenge and complain that existing video materials are too slow.

⚠️ Sanity Check: Missing Questions & Risks

Missing Questions:

  • How many interviews have actually been conducted so far?
  • Do interviewees represent our core demographic (senior engineers vs entry-level)?

Key Risks:

  • False Positives: Interviewees might say they care about certifications because they want to sound proactive, but they might not commit time to study.
  • B2B vs B2C Split: Will the student pay out-of-pocket, or do they expect their employer to sponsor the membership fees?
