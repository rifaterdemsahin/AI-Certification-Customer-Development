✅ The Go/No-Go Checkpoint

Verify is where Customer Discovery closes the loop. Every hypothesis from Step 1 has now been tested against real people twice — once as a problem (Step 2), once as an actual product (Step 3). Step 4 asks three separate yes/no questions — is the product validated, is the problem validated, is the business model validated — and then forces an explicit decision: persevere into Stage 2 (Customer Validation), iterate on a specific hypothesis, or exit the idea entirely.

This is a company-building decision, not a data point — which is why it's tracked separately from the individual metrics in Quality Gates.
