🚪 Get Out Of The Building

Step 2 is where the hypotheses from Step 1 meet real people. The goal is not to sell or pitch — it's to present the problem statement to real prospects and listen for genuine, visceral reactions versus polite agreement. Steve Blank's rule of thumb: if prospects don't lean forward and start describing their own pain unprompted, the problem hypothesis probably isn't strong enough yet.

For AI Certification Helper this means reaching engineers and professionals described in Target Audience before any cohort or paid product exists — through comments, communities, and direct outreach, not ad spend.
