🔎 What This Is

The Competitive Hypothesis names who else is already solving (or trying to solve) this problem for the same customer, and states the specific reason a prospect would choose this product over them.

🎯 Applied to AI Certification Helper

Hypothesis: Competitors are generic cloud-cert bootcamps, dry official manuals, and corporate training programs. Differentiation is the animated-video teaching format plus a live cohort built around real-time community problem-solving — explicitly not a promised exam-pass outcome.

  • ⚖️No "guarantee of passing" language anywhere on the site (see Risk Analysis) — this is a deliberate differentiation and liability decision, not an oversight.
  • 🏢H12 in the Hypothesis Tracker: IT consulting/government-contractor firms as a bulk B2B channel — a competitive angle individual-focused competitors aren't pursuing.
  • ✅Related PMF framing in Product-Market Fit Spec (H8).
🚦 What Would Falsify This
If prospects can't articulate a reason to choose this over a cheaper/established alternative once both are shown side by side, the differentiation isn't real — it's a feature, not a reason to switch.
