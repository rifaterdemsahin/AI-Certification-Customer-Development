This is a different zoom level than the site's other 4-stage framework (Discovery → Validation → Creation → Building). Those are the four company-building stages; the four steps on this page are Steve Blank's internal process for working through Stage 1 specifically — state hypotheses, test them against reality outside the building, and verify before spending real money.

🧭 Step-by-Step

1. Hypothesis

Write down every assumption — product, customer, pricing, demand, market type, competition — before talking to anyone.

View Step 1 →

2. Test Problem Hypothesis

Leave the building. Present the problem (not the solution) to real prospects and listen.

View Step 2 →

3. Test Product Hypothesis

Show the MVP itself — a video, a pricing page — and watch what people actually do.

View Step 3 →

4. Verify

Confirm problem, product, and business model all hold, then persevere, iterate, or exit.

View Step 4 →
