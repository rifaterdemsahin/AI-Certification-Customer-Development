👥 Participant Discovery Signals & Verbatim VoC

Enterprise QA & Research

Mariana

Expressed acute friction regarding cognitive fatigue, late Sunday timing, and unstructured open discussions that intimidate newer learners.

"You are trying to run the cohort where people can discuss items freely... But they are not on a similar level as you, and they feel very unprofessional. You are feeding them with information that makes them feel that they know more... they got scared and didn't come back."
"In terms of education, people need a program, a clear simple agenda. They want to know what they are going to listen to and do step-by-step. They want to feel smart... Also, Sunday evening is very tricky. Make it 30 minutes to one hour."
Validation: Demand for structured, step-by-step roadmaps, Preconditions/Postconditions, and 1-on-1 personalized setup workshops.
Enterprise Systems Architect

Brian

Currently taking TOGAF architecture certification. Highlighted the crucial difference between governance and hands-on delivery, and proposed preset project levels.

"If you want to cover a wide skill level, you should have preset projects: Foundational (clock app), Intermediate (guided execution), Architect (complex infrastructure). That way, people learn the handles and terms before getting hit with architect-level jargon."
"There's a big difference between a service and a learning opportunity: Service is 'Pay me and I will build it for you.' Learning is 'We go through the gears together step-by-step so I learn how to build it myself.'"
Validation: Need for clear project laddering; validates practical co-building ("going through the gears") as the core value prop of Delivery Pilot.
Academic Educator & UWISE Founder

Anna (via Mariana Feedback)

Conveyed feedback from initial session: attendee was enthusiastic about Obsidian and managing scientific data, but suffered from extreme information overload.

"Anna felt like you are very professional—which is great—but what can she get from it? It was difficult for her to understand everything and follow the conversation. It was too complicated, too quickly, too much information at once."
"She suggested trying 5–10 minutes explaining, 10 minutes demo, 15 minutes Q&A, and strictly 3 tasks per session."
Validation: Validates H24 (Cognitive Overload) and informs the new 10-10-15 sprint pacing standard.

🛠️ The 4 Action Pillars Resulting from Session 9

1. 🗓️ Live Session & Schedule Overhaul

  • Cap at 60 Minutes: Reduce standard live sessions from 2 hours to 60 minutes.
  • Weekday Evening Talks: Move general community talks to Tuesday or Thursday at 7:00 PM UK time.
  • 10-10-15 Structure: 5–10 min topic intro → 10 min live demo/lab → 15 min structured Q&A.
  • Cohort Size Cap: Limit interactive cohorts to 4–5 participants so everyone can speak.

2. 🎯 Course & Preset Project Setup

  • Separate Content Streams: Decouple theoretical video courses from interactive labs.
  • 🟢 Foundational Level: Basic starter setups (simple clock & utility apps).
  • 🟡 Intermediate Level: Guided workflow builds (custom LinkedIn automation, Obsidian).
  • 🔴 Architect Level: Enterprise security, multi-agent frameworks, complex custom infra.
  • Pre & Postconditions: Explicitly publish what to install before and what will be built after.

3. ⚙️ VIP 1-on-1 Workshop Setup

  • Monday (19:00–21:00 UK): 🔒 AI Security & Local Admin Protocols
  • Tuesday (19:00–21:00 UK): 🛠 Environment & App Setup (Obsidian, Azure Foundry, RAG)
  • Friday (19:00–21:00 UK): 👤 Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Workflows
  • 10-Minute Waiting Policy: Wait 10 minutes max for booked appointments before going offline.
  • Grant VIP Tier: Upgrade initial 10 core regulars (Brian, Mariana, Sude) to complimentary VIP.

4. 📢 Platform Marketing & Skool Bug Fixes

  • LinkedIn & X.com Promotion: Daily updates to 30,000-person network (Swipe File).
  • Sunday Lunchtime WhatsApp Broadcast: Weekly personal reminder (12:00–14:00 UK) to study groups.
  • Fix Skool Booking Bug: Configure calendar display setting to enable 1-on-1 bookings without the $99/mo webinar upgrade fee.
