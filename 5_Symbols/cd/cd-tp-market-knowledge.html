🔎 What This Is

Market Knowledge is what gets learned about the wider competitive and market landscape simply by talking to prospects — what else they considered, what they searched for, who they already trust — supplementing desk research with field evidence.

🎯 Applied to AI Certification Helper

Applied: Using prospect conversations to sanity-check the TAM/SAM/SOM figures in Market & Audience Spec and surface competitors that desk research alone might miss (internal corporate training programs, Discord-based study groups, etc.).

  • 🔍Cross-checked against H6 and H12 (B2B consulting-firm channel) on the Hypothesis Tracker.
  • 📄Full sourcing behind the market numbers: Full Hypothesis Doc.
🚦 What A Pass Looks Like
Field conversations either corroborate the TAM/SAM/SOM working estimate or surface a concrete reason to revise it — both are useful outcomes.
