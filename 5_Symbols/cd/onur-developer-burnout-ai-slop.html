⚡ Onur: Developer Burnout, Idle AI Guilt & The "AI Slop" Filter Chain

Spoken customer discovery from American Turkish software engineer & tech YouTuber Onur (14+ years experience: 11 years US enterprise software consultant/engineer, 1 year Europe, 2 years Turkey). Heavy daily user of Claude Code, Cursor, and agentic harnesses. Demonstrates the acute AI Acceleration Paradox: tools shrank task duration from 1 week to half a day, but management expanded workloads 10x, leading to extreme cognitive exhaustion, context-switching amnesia, guilt over idle compute, and communication breakdowns caused by multi-stage "AI slop".

🗣️ Spoken Transcript Highlights

The Acceleration Paradox & White Hair (H24)
“Look at me now, I'm in a miserable state, man. My hair is going to turn white... Normally you'd give a task, say, a week. When tools like Claude Code and Cursor came out, it dropped to half a day. Now, if it doesn't finish in half a day, it becomes: 'Hey, why isn't it finished yet?' Instead of doing 1 task, I now have to finish 10 tasks. Once again, the heavy burden fell on us software engineers.”
"God Doesn't Like Idle Hands" & Compute Guilt (H24)
“Brother, we became addicted, man! When my Claude account sits idle, I feel like I'm paying an hourly worker who is getting paid by the hour while resting. I look at AI sitting idle for 45 minutes and I'm like: 'You don't deserve the money you get, you damn dog!' As the saying goes in our culture: 'God doesn't like idle hands.' Before dinner, before walking the dog, before sleeping, I'm queuing prompts... we can't spare any time for ourselves.”
Context Switching & Claude Code "Recap" (H24 / H30)
“Four tabs open, say, four Claude Code sessions open there. Switch to that one, switch to a completely different idea... By the time the initial task finishes, you return to it: 'What the hell were we doing here?' Why do you think they built the 'Recap' feature in Claude Code? Because the guys who built it went through the exact same thing when their brains locked up! Context switching isn't easy.”
The AI Slop Filter Chain & Loss of Meaning (H2 / H28)
“The customer tells the analyst via Claude. The analyst tells me via ChatGPT. I give that idea that passed through AI twice back to Claude. It builds it. The customer says: 'I didn't want this!' Shifts in meaning happen, invention of details happens. I specifically tell people: 'Please send me what you wrote without passing it through AI, no matter how silly it is.' Slop is real. It really, really exists.”
Toy SPAs vs. Real Enterprise Complexity (H1 / H25)
“People build dashboards, brother! A single-page app... if we do this, Cursor does it anyway. But if an actual enterprise solution is needed, something that genuinely solves problems, you're talking about a concept of how to reach the solution, what people need, 20 people coordinating, complex insurance logic that takes 6–9 months to internalize. Someone needs to manage this process.”
95% Discarded vs. Solution-Oriented Pilots (H30 / H35)
“And 95% of what we do, we throw in the trash! In the last 2 months, maybe 60 things... only 2 or 3 gave an output that people can reach. Therefore, I still see people saying: 'Brother, I'm quitting the profession.' No profession is safe anyway. If your brain works and you're solution-oriented, I don't recommend quitting the profession. We need people who manage this process.”
