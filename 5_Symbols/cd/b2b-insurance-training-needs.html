👑 Erdem (SME) • Software & Multi-Agent Delivery

The B-MAD Framework & Multi-Agent Swarms

Single-chat assistants fail at complex enterprise operations due to context overflow. The future is multi-agent architectures with context sharding.

⚡ B-MAD (Breakthrough Method for Agile Delivery):

Evolution of Agile/Scrum tailored for AI engineering. Role-based agentic workflows (analyst, architect, developer, verifier) + certification tracks. Drops hallucination & revision rates from ~75% to enterprise reliability.

Key Concept: Context Sharding splits state across specialized subagents, eliminating context degradation and hallucinations.

Insurance Compliance & Regulatory Silos

The insurance sector operates under stringent privacy and competitive regulations. Unregulated multi-tenant models or cross-org data pooling are strictly disallowed.
