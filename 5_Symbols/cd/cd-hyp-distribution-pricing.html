🔎 What This Is

The Distribution & Pricing Hypothesis states the channel the product will reach customers through, and what price point customers will actually accept at that channel.

🎯 Applied to AI Certification Helper

Hypothesis: YouTube is the primary organic distribution channel; monetization layers as a $10/mo prep membership and $250–500 live cohort bootcamps, not a single one-time purchase.

  • 📺Channel detail in Channels (BMC).
  • 💰Pricing detail in Revenue Streams (BMC).
  • 📉Tied to H4 (~1% free→paid conversion) and H5 (cohorts sell 20–40 seats organically) in the Hypothesis Tracker.
🚦 What Would Falsify This
If >5% newsletter/email conversion (H3) can't be hit even with a clear pricing page, the price or the channel — not the product — is the blocker.
