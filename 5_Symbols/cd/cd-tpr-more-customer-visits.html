🔎 What This Is

More Customer Visits widens the sample beyond the first handful of contacts to check whether the early reaction generalizes across the broader target audience, rather than reflecting one friendly pocket of early adopters.

🎯 Applied to AI Certification Helper

Applied: Repeating the product presentation across additional videos and audience segments described in Target Audience, and feeding results into the Sales Pipeline before committing to a full cohort launch.

  • 🔁This is the loop that turns one lucky video into a repeatable pattern.
  • 👥Checks whether different audience segments respond consistently, or only one narrow segment does.
🚦 What A Pass Looks Like
The retention and conversion pattern holds across multiple videos and segments — not just the first one that happened to land well.
