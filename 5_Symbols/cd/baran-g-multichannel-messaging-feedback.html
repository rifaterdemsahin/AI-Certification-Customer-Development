🧭 One Capability, Three Front-Ends

Airbnb & SpareRoom

Marketplace conversation: continuous, inbound-triggered, judged on tone and appropriateness. A generic message costs a booking — which is why templating alone fails and he wants drafting help.

LinkedIn responses

The founder’s own instance of the same primitive: reply to the inbound stream of recruiter, contractor and prospect messages correctly and at volume. Grokbot named as the solution path.

Draft by agent, send by human

The teaching value sits in the drafting (tone rules, evidence rules, per-channel etiquette); the governance value sits in the approval boundary — consistent with the Chidi role-constraint thread. No unattended sending.

Answers must persist

“For contractors and for research” is a retrieval requirement on us: a documented, checkable multi-channel messaging recipe in the repo, not a chat thread that evaporates.
