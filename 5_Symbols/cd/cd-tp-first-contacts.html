🔎 What This Is

First Contacts is the concrete act of identifying and reaching the first 10–15 real prospects willing to talk about the problem — not a survey blast, but individually chosen people who plausibly fit the customer hypothesis.

🎯 Applied to AI Certification Helper

Applied: Sourcing the first prospect conversations from people already engaging with early content — YouTube commenters, relevant subreddits/Discord communities, and LinkedIn connections working toward cloud/AI certifications — rather than cold outreach to strangers with no signal.

  • 🎯Prospect profile matches Target Audience segments.
  • 📋Feeds the top of the Sales Pipeline.
🚦 Target
10–15 real conversations booked with people who fit the target-audience profile — quality of fit matters more than volume at this stage.
