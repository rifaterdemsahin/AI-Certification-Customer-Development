🔎 What This Is

Customer Understanding goes past the problem statement into how the prospect currently lives with it — what they've already tried, what they spend, and what a 10x-better solution would need to look like for them.

🎯 Applied to AI Certification Helper

Applied: Learning what cert-prep resources prospects already use (official docs, AWS/GCP-style bootcamps, YouTube tutorials), what they've paid for those, and whether $10/mo or $250–500 cohort pricing lands as reasonable or high given that context.

  • 💳Directly informs H3 (>5% email signup conversion) and H5 (cohort willingness-to-pay) on the Hypothesis Tracker.
  • 👤Sharpens the persona detail in Target Audience.
🚦 What A Pass Looks Like
Prospects can name a specific budget or past purchase for cert prep — proving willingness to pay is real, not hypothetical.
