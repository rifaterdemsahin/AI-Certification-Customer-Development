Steve Blank Method

🎤 Customer Discovery Interview Guide

Qualitative research playbook designed to extract authentic developer stories, identify learning pains, and validate cloud architect certification struggles.

⚠️

THE LISTENING MANDATE: DO NOT TALK ABOUT YOUR SOLUTION

Your goal is validation of the problem, not promotion of your product. If you mention highly-animated videos, the customer will switch from sharing their actual frustrations to politely agreeing with your idea. Focus 100% on listening to their past experiences, timelines, and current frustrations.

🛑 Pain Identification

Frustration & Friction

Dry Study Materials

  • "What is the single most frustrating part of preparing for a technical certification?"
  • "When was the last time you closed a study guide in annoyance? What caused that?"
  • "How do you deal with heavy, multi-page AWS or Azure architecture docs when you are short on time?"
Timeline & Inflation

Study Constraints

  • "How do you fit 40+ hours of exam preparation into your work week?"
  • "What happens when you spend hours reading documentation but feel like you retained nothing?"
  • "How much pressure do you feel from your company or the industry to get certified quickly?"

✨ Gain Identification

Ideal Experience

The Magic Wand

  • "If you could wave a magic wand and design the ultimate study guide, what would it look like?"
  • "What does a 'successful' and satisfying 1-hour study block feel like to you?"
  • "How do you know when you have actually mastered a complex system architecture?"
Career Impact

Value & Outcomes

  • "How would passing this exam change your day-to-day work or job confidence?"
  • "What is the biggest benefit of visual diagrams compared to text descriptions for your learning style?"
  • "What are you hoping to achieve once this certification is on your resume/LinkedIn?"
Qualitative Deep-Dive

🔍 Learning From Customer Stories (Past Behavior)

People are poor predictors of their future behavior, but their past actions tell the truth. Always steer the interview towards concrete stories:

How They Acted

Ask: "Tell me about the last certification you prepared for. Walk me through exactly what you bought, clicked, and read first."

How They Solve It Today

Ask: "What workarounds do you currently use when a concept is too complex? How much time or money do you spend doing that?"

The Budget Test

Ask: "What other courses, books, or prep tools have you paid for out of your own pocket in the past 12 months?"
