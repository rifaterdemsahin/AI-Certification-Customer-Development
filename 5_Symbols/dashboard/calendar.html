⚠️ Sanity Check: Missing Questions & Risks

Missing Questions:

  • Who updates this calendar when a milestone actually lands — does the entry move from ⚪ estimated to ✅ done with a real date, in place?
  • Should the four "Date TBD" entries (H12/H14/H16/H17/H18) get real target quarters once the $10k gate clears and Stage 3 planning firms up?

Key Risks:

  • A dated calendar reads as more certain than the underlying estimates actually are — every ⚪ entry inherits all the caveats already listed on Stage Timelines.
