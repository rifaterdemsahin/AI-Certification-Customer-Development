It is not a lecture post and it is not a second live call. YouTube / LinkedIn comments stay on the weekly batch.

1. Hello
Open members. Greet anyone new by name with one unfinished question.
2. Connect
Send the Skool connect request. Introduce them to one other member if a fit is obvious.
3. Notifications
Open the bell. Reply to joins, comments, and DMs. Mark the rest read. Do not leave a red badge overnight.
4. Prune Weeds
Zero Tolerance: Delete spam (ban + delete 7 days), remove negativity/bullying with feedback (Garden Playbook).

Paste-ready notes (do not lecture)

Welcome / hello: “Hey [Name] — welcome into Delivery Pilot. What pulled you in this week: Sunday live, a Classroom replay, or something else you are stuck on?”

Connect request: “Connecting so I can greet you properly, not as a broadcast. If you tell me what you are prepping for (Associate vs Architect, or a work pipeline), I will point you at the right Classroom and one person already in the room.”

Notification reply: Thank them by name, ask one follow-up, and if they posted a win ask them to put it on the board so someone else can answer next time. A private-only thank-you does not build a regular (see H29).
