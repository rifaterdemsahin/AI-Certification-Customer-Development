🎬 Why 1-1 still matters (Hormozi, 2026 start-over)

Either sell extremely expensive stuff to a select few, or sell something super cheap to everyone. The middle is where people die.

His start-over sequence is not “skip 1-1.” It is: sell your time 1-1 even though it does not scale, because that is the fastest way to make an expensive offer real. Then cap it (he says roughly 5–10% of time) so it cash-flows growth without blocking the gym floor. Even if you already run a Skool at a low monthly price, put a $1,000/mo or $10,000 one-time thing on the menu and make it available. Applied here: Peek / Sit In is the cheap door; Sunday Share Screen is the packed class; capped VIP 1-1 is the expensive few. $10/mo in the middle, sold as the hero, is the death zone he names.

1-1 is the classroom for the founder
You can change delivery the same hour without retraining a TA or recoding Classroom. That is the beta for the Architect Exam Sprint. Screen-share with Sude, Brian, Marianna, Tuncer is how this site learned install friction, 5-10-5 pacing, and partner-gated exam access. Killing 1-1 kills H29 and Customer Discovery.
1-1 builds the community, then feeds the room
People who got unstuck privately become the regulars who talk on Sunday. Founding invites and referrals (Bihar → Sude) started as personal time, not a second live night. 1-1 is how you earn the 10 true regulars. The class is how those regulars stop needing you one at a time. See 10 True Regulars.
Fast cash, then scale down-market
He starts high and works down. Tiny volume with lots of zeros still funds the cheap/scalable side. Gym story: one expensive client paid the floor. Here: a few £2.5k–£10k contractor 1-1s (or a real $1k/mo seat) can cash-flow Skool + video evenings. Do not wait for 90 Sit In members to pay the rent. See Revenue Streams.
Cap supply when demand shows
He is explicit: cap 1-1 so it does not get in the way long term. When the calendar fills, cut supply — raise the price, do not add nights. Saturday Environment / AI Security / Human-in-the-Loop blocks already do this. Unlimited WhatsApp 1-1 is the version that dies in the middle.

🧮 Why Hormozi Picks One Full Class

Leverage is one-to-many
Value = (Dream × Likelihood) ÷ (Delay × Effort). 1-1 raises likelihood for one buyer and destroys founder hours. A packed Sunday raises likelihood for every seat at the same cost. Hormozi's Grand Slam is the Architect Exam Sprint — live lab as the core, not a private retainer. See the stack on the agent page.
A catalog of classes is still a catalog
Opening Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday lives multiplies SKUs before one offer converts. The agent already graded the menu a C. Multiple rooms without a named sprint is more hallway, not more value. One CTA: Share Screen Sunday (Delivery Pilot Offer).
Bandwidth is the real constraint
Weekdays are an inside-IR35 contract. There is one unpaid live slot: Sunday 9–11pm. Each extra 1-1 or extra live night comes out of video production that feeds H4. See the weekly ledger.
Unlimited 1-1 trains the market wrong
On-demand 1-1 inside a $250/year VIP is a $2,000/hour giveaway. That is the middle: expensive-feeling, cheap-priced, unscalable. Cap the lab. Price the remaining 1-1 like a 100x SKU. Do not hide it, and do not give it away as “community support.”
