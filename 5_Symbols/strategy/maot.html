✨ MAOT — Minimum Awesome Outcome Threshold

MAOT is the smallest outcome a customer has to experience for the product to feel genuinely awesome to them — not just functional. It's a customer-development companion concept to MVP: the MVP is the bar for "does it work," MAOT is the bar for "would they tell a friend."

📏 MVP vs. MAOT

Everything left of the MVP bar fails outright. Between the two bars, the product functions but doesn't spread — customers use it quietly and churn silently. Crossing the MAOT bar is what starts organic referral, the cheapest possible growth channel for a $0-ad-spend business like this one.
