🛏️ The Bed of Procrustes (Camil Petrescu): Branding is Perception

In Camil Petrescu's modernist novel Patul lui Procust (The Bed of Procrustes), human experience is revealed as radically subjective: there is no single objective reality external to the observer. Each character experiences, distorts, and reconstructs love, status, and truth through their own cognitive lens. In the classical myth, Procrustes stretched travelers who were too short and amputated limbs of those who were too long to force them to fit his rigid iron bed. The Business Law: Branding is perception. Your brand is not what you claim it is on your pitch deck or website; it is exclusively what exists inside the customer's mind. Forcing customers to fit your preconceived product vision is fatal Procrustean engineering. You must mold the product around the customer's perceived reality. See Listen More Than You Speak, Perception of AI, and H29.

🎭 Brand Claim vs. Customer Mind Reality: The 4 Core Disconnects

1. The Certificate Itself

What Founder/Vendor Claims: "A PDF credential proving comprehensive architectural mastery of LLM token economics and prompt APIs."

What Actually Exists in Customer's Mind: "A weapon to pass automated recruiter keyword screening and stop being treated as an entry-level prompt tinkerer" (Audience Transformation).

2. The Sunday Live Cohort

What Conventional Educator Claims: "A 2-hour synchronous masterclass lecture covering module 4 of the official curriculum."

What Actually Exists in Customer's Mind: "A safe peer room where I can share my broken terminal screen at 13:30 UK without judgment and watch an experienced $1.3M contractor debug it live" (Party Format Pivot).

3. YouTube Animation Videos

What Standard Creator Claims: "A complete comprehensive instructional guide explaining how MCP servers work."

What Actually Exists in Customer's Mind: "A 60-second visual spark that challenged the mainstream AI hype, made me feel less crazy about corporate tech debt, and invited me to the Sunday screen-share" (Founder Voice SOP).

4. The Delivery Pilot Membership

What Basic Business Model Claims: "A $1/mo or $250/yr SaaS community access subscription."

What Actually Exists in Customer's Mind: "An alliance of independent contractors co-bidding on enterprise tenders and escaping the £50k PAYE salary ceiling" (H30 Contractor Alliance).

📖 The Three Petrescu Principles for Customer Development

  • 1️⃣ Never argue with a customer's subjective perception: If a candidate believes Claude certs are partner-gated or too theoretical, that belief is 100% of their operational reality. You cannot argue them out of it; you must provide tangible proof, partner enrollment access, and live terminal exercises.
  • 2️⃣ Proof beats claims (The Multi-Perspective Lens): Petrescu shows that letters, diaries, and spoken confessions create reality. In our business, Canva slides, real exam scorecards (Charles's 917/1000), live terminal debugging, and unscripted screen shares provide undeniable proof that transforms perception.
  • 3️⃣ The Listening Posture: A founder who lectures assumes they possess the single objective truth. A founder who listens (H29) understands that each student brings a distinct perceptual world that must shape the curriculum.
