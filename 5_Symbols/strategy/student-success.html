🎓 Prioritize Student Success

Student achievement is the bedrock of platform credibility, community retention, and commercial viability. Rather than measuring vanity views, the platform tracks tangible engineering outcomes: vendor certification pass rates, public GitHub architectures, and successful contractor placements at £500–£1,000+/day (see H8 and H30).

🔄 The Student Success Retention & Growth Flywheel

Phase 1: Rigorous Hands-on Practice Students participate in active Sunday screen-shares and complete real exam scenario labs, eliminating theoretical fluff.
Phase 2: Formal Credentialing & Portfolios Passing the Claude Architect exam establishes unquestioned third-party validation, paired with public GitHub code artifacts.
Phase 3: Proof-of-Concept Testimonials Verified student career wins serve as unassailable marketing proof, dramatically dropping Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).
Phase 4: B2B Enterprise Inbound Corporate leaders and consulting firms observe alumni results, leading to bulk enterprise training contracts and sponsorship deals.
