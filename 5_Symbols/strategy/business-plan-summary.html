Business Plan Summary

One-page view of AI Certification Helper — founder Rifat Erdem Sahin's animated-content-and-cohort business built on Steve Blank's Customer Development framework. 😖 Problem

Generative AI is reshaping technical job requirements faster than professionals can retrain. Existing certification prep material is dry (500-page manuals, 40-hour courses) and has low completion.

See Problem Focus →

💡 Solution

Animated, visually distilled technical training videos on YouTube, funneling into a $10/mo prep membership and $250–$500 live cohort bootcamps for AI/cloud certification exams.

See Value Proposition →

🌐 Market

TAM ~25M developers worldwide adapting to AI-driven engineering; SAM ~3M actively seeking AI/cloud certifications; SOM ~50k reachable within 3 years via Rifat's channels.

See Market & Audience → · See Personas →

💳 Business Model

Free YouTube funnel → $10/mo membership → $250–$500 cohort bootcamps. No paid ad spend; distribution runs on organic YouTube search and course-tab structuring.

See Business Model Canvas →

🚦 Traction Targets

  • 🚪$10,000 cumulative cohort revenue closes Stage 2 (Customer Validation).
  • 📈1,000x subscriber growth per video (~30 → ~30,000) drives Stage 3 (Customer Creation).
  • 🏢$100,000 ARR triggers Stage 4 hiring (editor, coordinator, TAs).
See Quality Gates →

🗺️ Roadmap & Ask

Currently in Stage 1–2 (Discovery/Validation), run solo by Rifat Erdem Sahin as a second job. No external funding sought at this stage — growth is self-funded by cohort revenue, reinvested per the growth flywheel.
