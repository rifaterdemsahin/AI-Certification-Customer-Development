🏢 ➡️ 👥 ➡️ 🏢 The B2C2B Business Model & Sourcing Engine

Our core economic engine operates on a B2C2B (Business-to-Consumer-to-Business) architecture. Rather than burning capital on slow 12-month top-down enterprise software sales, we acquire and upskill individual practitioners (B2C), transition gradually through agile Small & Mid-Sized Enterprise pilots (SME 2B), and ultimately scale into Fortune 500 enterprises by sourcing them verified, battle-tested Delivery Pilots (Enterprise 2B).

🎯 B2C: Practitioner Flywheel ⚡ SME 2B: Agile Turn-Key Pilots 🏢 Enterprise 2B: Talent Sourcing & FDEs 💼 £500–£1,000+/Day Day Rates 📋 H1, H12, H22, H30

🗺️ The 3-Stage B2C2B Progression Architecture

Stage 1 · B2C (Consumer)

1. Individual Upskilling & Proof

Software engineers, DevOps leads, and contractors join via free YouTube courses and Skool community ($0 Peek / $1 Sit In / $250 VIP). They master agentic workflows (Claude Code, MCP, RAG), pass proctored vendor exams, and build real custom AI apps.

Key Metric: 10 True Regulars, Pearson VUE exam passes, functional personal repositories.
Stage 2 · SME 2B (Agile Pilots)

2. Small-Mid Enterprise Pilots

Erdem's Insight: The "2B" pivot begins slowly with Small & Mid-Sized Enterprises. SMEs have fast 1-week procurement, acute AI delivery bottlenecks, and cannot afford £50k consultancy retainers. Delivery Pilots build £3k–£10k turn-key automation pilots.

Key Metric: 2–4 week pilot delivery, working client telemetry, verifiable commercial proof.
Stage 3 · Enterprise 2B (Sourcing)

3. Enterprise Talent Sourcing

Large enterprises (Banks, Healthcare, Energy, Global Consultancies) need vetted AI talent to execute multi-million pound tenders. The ecosystem sources them certified, portfolio-proven Delivery Pilots (£500–£1,000+/day contracts or placement fees).

Key Metric: £500–£1,000+/day day rate contracts, recruiter co-bidding alliances, enterprise retainers.

📊 The B2C2B Flywheel & Value Transmission

⚖️ Strategic Comparison: Direct Top-Down B2B vs. Organic B2C2B

Dimension ❌ Traditional Top-Down B2B ✅ Delivery Pilot B2C2B Engine
Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Huge enterprise sales team, expensive trade booths, 9–12 month sales cycles. Near Zero / Organic: Free YouTube videos and organic LinkedIn reach attract passionate engineers.
Early Validation Speed Paralyzed by security reviews, procurement RFP red tape, and delayed sign-offs. Instant Agile Feedback: Weekly Sunday screen-shares and fast SME pilot turn-key deployments (2–4 weeks).
Talent Quality & Trust Recruitment agencies spam unverified CVs and candidates with surface-level claims. Verifiable Proof: Proctored Credly certifications + public code repos + real custom deployed apps.
Monetization Structure Lumpy, high-risk enterprise deals; high churn risk if sales cycle misses quarter. Multi-Tiered Cashflow: Steady consumer subscriptions ($10/mo, $250 VIP) → SME POCs → Enterprise contractor day-rates.
