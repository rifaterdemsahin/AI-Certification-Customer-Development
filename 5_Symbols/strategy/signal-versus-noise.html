📡 Signal versus Noise

Every page and current report on this site, scored and sorted by how much it would change a founder decision this week versus how much attention it burns without changing one. The filter is H9: until two consecutive paid cohorts combine to $10,000, field evidence and current-stage gates are signal; Stage 3/4 theater, unverified TAM, and duplicate restatements are noise. See Evidence Map for what actually backs each hypothesis, and Single-Founder Bandwidth for why this ranking exists.

🧮 Scoring Rubric

Signal (0–100)

Would this change what Rifat does this week toward interviews, paid conversion, or the $10k gate?

  • 90+First-party field evidence (interviews, Studio numbers, exam scores, live signups).
  • 70–89Current-stage operating decision or evidence tracker (gates, unit economics, hypothesis status).
  • 45–69Working model for Discovery/Validation — useful, not yet proof.
  • 0–44Future-stage plan, duplicate restatement, prospect/investor theater, or nav-only.

Noise (0–100)

How much attention does this burn without changing a decision?

  • +35Future-stage work (Creation, Building, hiring, 1,000x growth, paid ads) presented as current.
  • +25Duplicate BMC/funnel restatement, or unverified TAM treated as settled (see H6).
  • +20Investor/prospect theater with no new evidence.
  • +15Framework overlay (4DX, MAOT, Marxian surplus) that does not change this week's work.
  • +10Meta navigation (sitemap, glossary, latest-pages).

Net = Signal − Noise. Ratio = Signal ÷ max(Noise, 1). Quadrants: Read first Signal ≥ 70 and Noise ≤ 35   Extract Signal ≥ 70 and Noise > 35   Reference everything in between   Defer Signal < 50 and Noise ≥ 50. Scores are founder-attention judgments, not page quality grades — a well-written Stage 4 page is still noise this week.
