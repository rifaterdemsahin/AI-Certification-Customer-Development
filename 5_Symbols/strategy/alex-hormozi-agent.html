📚 The Two Books, Mapped Onto This Business

Hormozi's sequence is not optional. $100M Offers is the product. $100M Leads is the megaphone. This site already agrees with the second half of that sequence on When to Start Paid Ads. The agent is here to force the first half.

$100M Offers

How to make an offer so good people feel stupid saying no

  • 1Grand Slam Offer — one named package that cannot be compared to Udemy by price.
  • 2Value Equation — raise dream and certainty; crush delay and effort.
  • 3Stack — break the offer into priced bonuses, then give the stack.
  • 4Guarantee the process, never a Pearson VUE pass. Site rule still holds.

$100M Leads

How to get strangers to want to buy your stuff

  • 1Core Four — warm outreach, posted content, cold outreach, paid ads.
  • 2Lead magnet — a free thing so good it would sell, traded for contact.
  • 3Rule of 100 — 100 minutes or 100 actions a day on the working channels.
  • 4More / Better / New — volume first, then quality, then a new channel.

🧮 Value Equation · $100M Offers

📣 Core Four · $100M Leads

All advertising is two axes: warm vs cold audience, one-to-one vs one-to-many. That gives four plays. This business is allowed two of them until H9 clears.

Warm · 1-to-1

Work the list you already have

Marianna, Sude, Bayo, Charles, Baris, the WhatsApp room, LinkedIn colleagues. Hormozi starts here because trust is prepaid. Ask: "Who else is sitting the Architect exam this quarter?" That is Watering Holes, not a content calendar.

Warm/public · 1-to-many

Post, then point at one door

YouTube and LinkedIn are the right public play. The miss is the handoff. Every video needs one CTA to one offer, not a sitemap. Titles already map on Titles → Skool. Use them.

Cold · 1-to-1

After the warm list is empty

Cold DMs to strangers are optional volume. A second-job founder should not open this channel until warm outreach and weekly content are at the Rule of 100. Bandwidth is the constraint — see Single-Founder Bandwidth.

Cold · 1-to-many

Do not buy strangers yet

Hormozi and this site agree. Ads are a multiplier on a converting offer. When to Start Paid Ads is the lock. If organic viewers will not buy, paid strangers will not buy either.

🧲 Lead Magnet vs. Free Cohort

What Hormozi means by lead magnet

A free thing so useful it could sell, given in exchange for a way to follow up. Not a 2-hour live workshop that consumes the founder's only Sunday night. Not a planning site. A cheatsheet, a 20-question diagnostic, or a 12-minute "book your exam in 21 days" briefing that ends on an email capture.

What you are doing instead

The Sunday session is discovery gold and delivery-expensive. Keep it for people who already raised their hand. Put a smaller magnet in front of YouTube and LinkedIn so H3's newsletter actually gets a denominator. The $29 bundle is a tripwire, not a magnet — magnets are free.

💯 Rule of 100 · Second-Job Version

Hormozi's volume rule: 100 minutes of outreach, or 100 minutes of content, or 100 paid-ad dollars — every day. You do not have that day. Translate it to a weekly 100 the founder can actually keep.
