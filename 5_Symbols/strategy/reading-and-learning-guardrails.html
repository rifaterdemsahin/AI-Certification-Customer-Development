📖 Reading & Learning Guardrails

Customer discovery starts with listening and learning, not broadcasting on the channel. In Stages 1 and 2, posting content without deep reading is a vanity trap: it burns single-founder energy, generates ungrounded opinions, and creates solutions for problems nobody has. These operational guardrails enforce a 4:1 reading-to-posting ratio, strict throttle limits on broadcasting, and measurable listening metrics across all touchpoints. See H29 and The Listening Mandate.

🛑 The Steve Blank Law: Customer Discovery Begins With Listening

"In Customer Discovery, there are no facts inside the building, and there are no facts inside your own monologue."

When a solo founder spends 10 hours creating high-gloss videos, writing LinkedIn treatises, or filling Skool feeds with top-down announcements, they are broadcasting, not discovering. Broadcasting generates artificial dopamine, burns finite cognitive energy, and traps the builder in an echo chamber of their own hypotheses. Customer discovery begins when you stop speaking, open the primary documentation, read real forum threads, analyze exam blueprints, and listen to what actual practitioners are agonizing over.

⚖️ The Posting Trap vs. The Learning Engine

❌ The Posting Trap (Broadcast Mode)

Compulsive Creation Without Input

  • 🚨The Illusion of Progress: Publishing 3 posts/week feels like "building a business," but validates zero customer pain.
  • 🗣️Lecturing the Void: Pushing answers to questions nobody actually asked in real cohort calls or comments.
  • 📉Founder Burnout: Sucks bandwidth away from reading official Anthropic updates, Pearson VUE blueprints, and member code repos.
  • 🙈Confirmation Bias: Only noticing likes and polite affirmations while missing underlying confusion.
✅ The Learning Engine (Discovery Mode)

Deliberate Ingestion & Evidence Capture

  • 📚Deep Domain Reading: Studying model cards, architecture whitepapers, and exam domain rubrics before drafting anything.
  • 👂Active Listening Log: Capturing verbatim quotes, blockers, and failure stories on Interview Recording.
  • 🎯Targeted Synthesis: Every piece of content is an exact response to a documented, repeated practitioner obstacle.
  • 🤝Audience Cocreation: The community builds the curriculum because the founder listens to their actual friction points.

🛡️ The 5 Operational Guardrails

Guardrail 1: The 4:1 Reading-to-Posting Ratio (80% Input / 20% Output)

Time Ledger Law

For every 1 hour allocated to producing public content (scripting, editing, publishing), the founder must spend at least 4 hours deeply reading technical documentation, exam blueprints, candidate code submissions, community forums, and logged interview notes. If the reading ledger is empty, publishing is locked.

Guardrail 2: The "No Unearned Broadcast" Rule

Falsification Rule

No new video topic, article, or announcement may be created based on founder intuition alone. Every output must cite at least 3 verified, logged candidate friction points (from discovery calls, Sunday live troubleshooting, or Skool questions) in its planning document before production begins.

Guardrail 3: Strict Weekly Posting Throttle

Output Cap

In Stages 1 and 2, public broadcasting is capped: maximum 1 animated video per week and maximum 1 structured LinkedIn synthesis every 3–5 days. YouTube and LinkedIn comments are batch-read and answered strictly in a single weekly batch (Thursday evening), never via continuous reactive notifications.

Guardrail 4: The 2:1 Live Listening Ratio

Live Room Metric

During the Sunday live cohort sessions, the founder's speaking time must not exceed 33% of the total session duration. The remaining 67%+ is reserved for candidate screen-sharing, open problem-solving, and learner discussions. If the session turns into a 60-minute founder lecture, the cohort failed its listening objective (H29).

Guardrail 5: Unfinished Questions Over Polished Monologues

Engagement Rule

Every published asset (video outro, Skool post, LinkedIn share) must close with an open, specific inquiry into a difficult technical problem (e.g., "Where did your evaluation harness fail in production?"), inviting the community to reply and teach the room, rather than delivering a closed authoritative sermon.

🔄 The 3-Phase Weekly Listening Routine

Phase 1: Ingest & Study

Read & Absorb

Mon–Wed: Read Anthropic documentation, inspect Pearson VUE sample items, review Claude Code release diffs, and study candidate code repositories. No posting allowed.

Phase 2: Listen & Log

Observe & Record

Thu–Sat: Batch-read YouTube & LinkedIn comments (Thu PM), run 1:1 discovery intercepts at Venture Coffee / meetups, and log exact problem quotes in the tracker.

Phase 3: Cocreate & Synthesize

Live Build & Ship

Sunday: Run the live cohort room with a 2:1 listening ratio, let candidates screen-share real blocker code, and ship 1 curriculum improvement directly addressing their feedback.
