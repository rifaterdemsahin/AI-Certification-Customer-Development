🧑‍💼 For Stakeholders: How to Read This Register

This page exists so a stakeholder — an investor, a partner, or the founder himself — can evaluate exposure at a glance without reading every other page on the site. Each row below is scored on two axes: Likelihood (how probable is this in the next 6–12 months) and Impact (how much it would set back the stage gates if it happened). Every risk lands in exactly one of three buckets: actively mitigated in the register itself, a mitigation underway as a live pilot, or knowingly accepted because fixing it now costs more than the exposure is worth at this stage.

🚀 Active Mitigations Underway

Unlike the accepted risks below, these are mitigations actually being piloted right now against the founder-single-point-of-failure and platform-dependency risks in the register above — see Single-Founder Bandwidth for the underlying bandwidth ceiling they're responding to.

Piloting an in-person delivery format alongside the online Sunday cohort. Diversifies delivery beyond 100% solo/online, and further validates whether the content and format hold up face-to-face, not just on a screen. Tracked as H17 in the Hypothesis Tracker.

Building an onsite training product for a corporate client together with partner Marianna — the first concrete test of the B2B/consulting-firm channel hypothesis (H12), and a step toward reducing single-founder delivery dependency ahead of the $100k ARR hiring trigger.

🤝 Accepted Responsibility

Not every risk gets mitigated. Some are consciously accepted because the cost of mitigating them now exceeds the benefit at this stage. Rifat Erdem Sahin has explicitly chosen to accept the following:

🧑‍💻 Founder-as-single-point-of-failure
All research, scripting, animation, and cohort teaching runs through one person. Accepted rather than mitigated (e.g. by hiring early) because Stage 1–3 needs speed and low overhead more than redundancy; hiring is deferred until the $100k ARR trigger (see Company Building). See Single-Founder Bandwidth for the actual weekly time ledger behind this risk.
📺 YouTube platform dependency
Distribution relies almost entirely on YouTube's organic algorithm rather than paid, owned channels. Accepted because it delivers near-zero-cost distribution ($0 CAC target, see Funnel Spec) that a self-funded solo operation cannot otherwise afford.
💰 Unsourced TAM/SAM/SOM precision
The exact 25M/3M/50k figures are treated as a working estimate, not a validated study (see H6 in Hypothesis Tracker). Accepted because a dedicated sizing study costs more time than the decisions currently riding on the number justify.
🎯 Single-niche exam focus (Claude Certified Architect)
Betting the curriculum on one certification program rather than diversifying across multiple vendors early. Accepted to keep the MVP focused and avoid diluting production time — see Focus for the explicit scope decision.
