This page maps how unfair advantages are leveraged to drive Stage 1 & 2 validation, reduce customer acquisition cost, and establish an uncopyable business moat.

📌 The Leverage Premise (Hormozi Style)

Leverage is the difference between what you put in and what you get out. If you have zero leverage, you work 100 hours to get 100 units of value. If you have extreme leverage, you work 1 hour to get 10,000 units of value. You don't build a business by working harder. You build it by choosing better levers.

The Law of Leverage · Focus on inputs that produce outsized outputs

As a single founder running this business as a second job while working a contract role (see Single-Founder Bandwidth), time is the ultimate constraint. You cannot afford linear operations. You must use asymmetric advantages that amplify your efforts while keeping operational costs near zero.
