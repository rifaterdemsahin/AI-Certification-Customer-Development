💡 Why Enterprises Demand "At Least 2 Certifications"

The "T-Shaped Practitioner" Screening Filter

Enterprise recruiters receive hundreds of CVs claiming "Copilot and AI experience". However, enterprise delivery fails when a practitioner only knows how to build an agent but cannot secure the tenant, or knows how to configure Purview but cannot speak to C-level stakeholders.

Requiring at least two certifications guarantees that candidates possess both Functional Execution (e.g. AB-100 / MS-102) and Governance & Trust (e.g. SC-401 / AB-730).

Pairing 1: The Builder-Defender
AB-100 + SC-401: Builds high-velocity Copilot Studio agents while guaranteeing that Microsoft Purview sensitivity labels, DLP boundaries, and Entra ID access controls prevent cross-department data leaks.
Pairing 2: The Enterprise Transformation Lead
MS-102 + AB-730: Combines rock-solid Microsoft 365 tenant foundation and hybrid identity with enterprise-grade multi-agent governance and C-level change management architecture.
