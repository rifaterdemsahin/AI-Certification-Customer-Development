📣 Slogan & Elevator Pitch

Canonical public line for YouTube, LinkedIn, Skool, and face-to-face opens. The slogan is the two-beat hook. The elevator pitch is the 20-second follow. Neither is a pass promise — Anthropic issues the exam via Pearson VUE, and this company trains, practices, and places people on the Delivery Pilot path. See H1, H24, H30, and Value Proposition.

📣 Public slogan live ✨ Positive skills framing ⚠️ Not a pass guarantee 🧑‍✈️ Maps to Delivery Pilot (H30)

Slogan

Get Certified in AI.
Shape the Future.

📣 The Slogan

Two beats. Beat one is the verifiable signal (sit a proctored AI exam). Beat two is the career job (become the person who ships, not the person left behind). Use this exact wording across channels so the brand does not splinter into a new tagline every week. See R.A.I.S.E. and Positive AI Skills Elevation. 

Get Certified in AI. Shape the Future.

🛗 The Elevator Pitch

Speak this after the slogan when someone asks “so what do you actually do?” Keep it under 20 seconds. Then stop and listen (see H29 and the face-to-face tactic). 

AI is reshaping every industry, and getting certified is the fastest way to stay ahead. Our program gives you the practical, high-impact skills needed to build cutting-edge solutions, stand out to top employers, and launch a future-proof career at the forefront of technology.

🧭 How the pitch maps to the real offer

Public copy can stay short. Internal translation must stay honest so the slogan never becomes a pass guarantee or a fake job placement. See H8 and F12 (exam access is partner-gated).

“Get Certified in AI”
Means: sit the Claude Certified Architect / Developer exam (Anthropic via Pearson VUE). This company does not grant, administer, or guarantee the badge. Partners seat the exam. Practice lives on YouTube + Sunday 13:30–14:30 UK. See Why Certification.
“Shape the Future”
Means: Delivery Pilot / Forward Deployed Engineer path — hands-on training, then cert, then recruiter promotion, then independent contracting at £500–£1,000+/day. See H30 roadmap.
“Our program”
Means: animated YouTube lessons, Skool Delivery Pilot (Peek $0 / Sit In $1 / Share Screen $250), Sunday live screen-share, and the $10/mo + $250–$500 revenue model. $0 and $1 joins are not paid enrollments.
“Stand out to top employers”
Means: a proctored badge plus a public portfolio of real builds — not a placement contract. B2C→B2B placement stays a slogan until partner payroll exists (Session 10 / Chidi). Monday VIP recruiter cowork is the current lever.

📍 Where to use it

Surface Use Do not

🧪 What this claims (and what would falsify it)

  • 📣Claim (H1 / H24): a positive two-beat slogan (“certify + shape the future”) outperforms doom copy and generic “pass the exam” copy on YouTube, LinkedIn, and Skool About. Experiment: A/B the slogan vs the Video 6 manifesto line on two consecutive Saturday invites and two Shorts CTAs; log click-through to skool.com/delivery-pilot-8938.
  • 🧑‍✈️Claim (H30): “Shape the Future” is read as contractor / FDE transformation, not academic study. Falsify if discovery contacts hear only “badge mill” and never mention Delivery Pilot, day rate, or real builds.
  • ⚠️Guardrail: slogan resonance is not a paid enrollment. $0 Peek and $1 Sit In still do not count toward H5 or H9.
