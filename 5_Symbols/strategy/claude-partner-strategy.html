🗺️ Stage-by-Stage CPN Engagement Playbook

When should a single founder engage? The Steve Blank Customer Development model dictates avoiding heavy administrative and marketing overhead before validation. Below is our staged roadmap:

Stage 1: Customer Discovery Active / Initial Entry

Academic Prep & Subsidized Prototyping

Establish entry-level registered status immediately to access learning resources and reduce build expenses.

  • 🎯 50% Exam Discount: Founder gets certified under Claude Certified Associate/Professional tracks at half price.
  • 🎯 $3K Sandbox Credits: Subsidizes LLM API calls for developing interactive study assistants and course demos (avoiding personal spend).
  • 🎯 Partner Academy: Use official materials as a baseline syllabus.
Stage 2: Customer Validation Current Focus

Credibility Signaling & Cohort Pilot

Leverage partner status to build trust with students paying premium ticket prices ($250–$500) on Skool.

  • 🎯 Claude Partner Badge: Placed on the Skool LMS checkout pages to counter price resistance (trust signal).
  • 🎯 Product Update Calls: Stay synchronized with Anthropic's changes to keep the MVP course materials extremely fresh.
  • 🎯 Repeat Gate Support: Minimize variable costs via Sandbox credits as we target the $10,000 gross sales gate (see Repeat Gate).
Stage 3: Customer Creation Planned

B2B Scaling & Lead Harvesting

Scale channels and expand the business to multi-certification (Nvidia, Microsoft, Google) using corporate CPN hooks.

  • 🎯 Partner Directory Profile: Harvest inbound enterprise/consulting-firm leads looking for Claude integrations.
  • 🎯 Services Registration: Attribute corporate consulting deals (like Marianna/Capgemini) to grow Pexabo's partner rank.
  • 🎯 $15K Story Incentive: Convert cohort success stories into co-branded marketing case studies funded by Anthropic ($5K/story).
  • 🎯 The CPN B2B Hook (Crucial): Selling cohort seats to other CPN partners who need to certify 10/100 staff to maintain their tiers! (See H12).
Stage 4: Company Building Future

TA Training & Organization Building

Delegate operations. TAs, video editors, and cohort support coordinators run the machine independently.

  • 🎯 Partner Academy for Staff: Train newly hired support TAs via official CPN learning tracks.
  • 🎯 Pooled PRM Support: Resolve partner tier escalations as Pexabo scales its structural training pipeline.

⚠️ Strategic Risks & Open Questions

🔑 The B2B Certification Hook (H12):

Anthropic's partner system forces consulting firms to certify 10 (Select), 100 (Preferred), or 1,000 (Global Premier) employees to maintain their tier. Because Rifat's course is one of the few optimized, animated preparation cohort systems, Pexabo LTD can sell bulk seats directly to these partner firms. This turns CPN's rules into a major B2B lead generation driver for us (see H12).

🔴 Open Risk & Pearson VUE Gate (Finding F12):

An active risk is whether Pearson VUE registration is restricted only to employees of registered CPN partner firms. If non-affiliated developers cannot take the exam, our B2C individual-market TAM (H1/H6) shrinks significantly. Confirming Pearson VUE individual access remains our top research priority (see Risk Register).
