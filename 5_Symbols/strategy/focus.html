Strategy · Focus

Problem Focus

Early-stage discipline requires solving exactly one problem well before expanding scope. This page states that problem explicitly, and what is deliberately out of scope for now — it complements Problem-Solution Fit, which covers the hypothesis validation plan.

🎯 The One Problem, Right Now

"Ambitious technical professionals who need to pass the Claude Certified Architect exam find existing prep material too dry to finish, and have no fast, visual way to build genuine understanding before test day."

Every content decision, every cohort feature, and every metric on this site (H1–H3 in the Hypothesis Tracker) exists to test and solve this single problem. Nothing else gets built until this is validated.

🚫 Explicitly Out of Scope (Deferred)

🎓 Multi-vendor certification coverage
AWS, GCP, Azure, or OpenAI certification content is not being produced yet. Adding vendors before validating the Claude-Architect niche would dilute production time without proving the model works for even one exam. Note: covering both the Associate and Architect tiers of Claude's own certification ladder is not the same as multi-vendor expansion — it's still one vendor, and the two tracks share most content. See Exam Topic Blueprint.
🏢 Formal organization / hiring
No editors, coordinators, or TAs are hired at this stage (deferred to the $100k ARR trigger — see Company Building). Solo execution keeps the discovery loop fast.
💻 Custom platform / LMS
Off-the-shelf tools (YouTube, a streaming tool, a chat community) are used instead of building software. See Requirements for the explicit "Won't Have" list.
📣 Paid advertising
$0 ad spend is a deliberate constraint (see Funnel Spec), not a budget limitation — it forces content quality to be the only growth lever, which is the actual thing being tested. For what to do instead of ads, and the later Stage 3 timing gate, see When to Start Paid Advertisements.

⚖️ Why Focus Matters Here

Steve Blank's Customer Discovery stage exists specifically to prevent premature scaling. A solo founder running this as a second job has a hard ceiling on available hours; every hour spent on a second certification track, a custom platform feature, or a hiring process is an hour not spent testing whether the core problem-solution fit hypothesis (H1–H3) is even true.

Scope expands only after a stage gate clears — see Quality Gates for the numeric criteria that unlock each stage, including when it becomes appropriate to revisit this out-of-scope list.
