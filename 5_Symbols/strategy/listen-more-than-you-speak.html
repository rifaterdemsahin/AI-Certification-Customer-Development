🧑‍💼 The Founder's Three Jobs

Customer Development (Steve Blank) starts outside the building. For a second-job founder, "outside" is the Sunday room, the discovery call, the comment thread, and the Skool reply. Teaching still happens. Teaching is not the default posture.

Job 1

Collect feedback

Write down what people actually said. A comment, a Sunday question, a discovery-call quote. If it is not written, it did not happen. The interview tracker is the log, not memory.

Job 2

Listen more than you speak

On Sunday, ask and wait. On Skool, reply more than you post. On YouTube and LinkedIn, batch-read comments before batch-answering. The ratio is the job. If the week ends founder-heavy, it was a lecture again.

Job 3

Let them cocreate

Leave something unfinished. Invite a vote, a glaze color, a next-Sunday topic. Ship the change they asked for and credit them by name. Cocreation is not a slogan. It is a named person on a shipped change.

🔁 The Cocreate Loop

Listening without shipping is politeness. Shipping without crediting is extraction. The loop is four steps, sized for a second job (see Single-Founder Bandwidth).

1. Hear

One question. Wait. Do not fill the silence with a lecture. Name the person if you can.

2. Write it down

Log the quote on Interview Recording or the Sunday notes. Verbatim beats a summary.

3. Ship one change

A classroom clip, a next-Sunday topic, a glossary term, a practice question. One, not ten.

4. Credit them

“Sude asked for this.” Public credit is how cocreation becomes culture, not a private thank-you.

🚫 What Listening Is Not

  • 🤐Not silence. The founder still teaches. The Classroom can lecture. The board and the live room cannot.
  • 🎁Not building every request. One shipped change a month, chosen against the current-stage gate (see Signal versus Noise).
  • 📣Not a brand voice. “We listen to our community” on a landing page is noise until a named person is credited on a shipped change.
  • 🧑‍🏫Not surrendering the exam standard. Audience pain sets the next lesson. It does not rewrite the no-guarantee rule or leak practice-exam dumps. See Risk Analysis.
  • ⏱️Not a second live call. Sunday 9pm is the hangout. Comments stay weekly. Do not add a listening hour. Bandwidth is already the constraint.
  • 💬Not private-only praise. A WhatsApp thank-you does not build the room. Ask them to post the win on Skool so others can join.
