🏗️ Scaling Milestones & Operational Delegation

Operational Scaling ($100k ARR Trigger)

Once Rifat Erdem Sahin's AI Certification Helper reaches $100,000 in annual revenue, it transitions from a solo operation into a structured corporate organization. This milestone triggers key hires to maintain student quality and scale curriculum velocity.

🚀 Founder Commitment: Full-Time Transition
Unlike the Stage 2→3 exit gate ($10k, where Rifat deliberately stayed in his contract role and kept this a second job), reaching the $100k ARR Stage 4 trigger is the point where Rifat Erdem Sahin goes all-in and runs AI Certification Helper as his full-time job, leaving the contract role behind.
🎬 Hire 1: Professional Video Editor (Target: 2028)
Rifat delegates video rendering, audio tuning, asset organization, and graphic animation. Hiring is planned for the expected timeline around 2028. This frees Rifat to focus strictly on researching new AI certification blueprints and writing code examples.
🗂️ Hire 2: Cohort Support Coordinator
Oversees cohort scheduling, handles intake forms, processes refunds, sends homework reminders, and manages member subscriptions to ensure a seamless experience. Mariana could fill this role as the Cohort Support Coordinator.
🔁 Delegated Task: Audience Follow-Up & Cohort Prep Reminders
Today the founder personally posts the weekly install-list and content-preview messages over WhatsApp/Discord and follows up 1-on-1 with attendees to confirm they're actually set up before Sunday (see Cohort Prep). This is a delegable task, not founder-only work — once the Cohort Support Coordinator (Hire 2) is on board, chasing the audience to install tools and prep for the upcoming cohort becomes one of their standing weekly responsibilities, freeing founder time for curriculum and strategy.
🛠️ Hire 3: Technical Support TAs (1-1 Support)
Qualified assistants hired part-time to help students 1-on-1 with deployment scripts, API configurations, and curriculum questions. TAs will be sourced directly from the audience who are actively invested in the process.

🚀 Long-Term Vision: From Content to Talent Placement

A later-stage direction for Company Building, beyond the hiring/scaling plan above — explicitly out of scope for Customer Discovery, Customer Validation, and Customer Creation. Tracked as H22 in the Hypothesis Tracker.

Once Customer Creation (Stage 3) has built a large, certified community through content and cohorts, Stage 4 can pilot a second product line built on top of it: placing certified graduates directly with enterprises as embedded practitioners — the same "Forward Deployed Engineer" pattern already growing 700–800%+ year-over-year (see H6). This would evolve the company from a content/education business into a certified-talent placement & recruiting company built around its own community — a materially different, higher-value model than the current cohort/membership business, and one designed to be VC-ready once the underlying community is proven.

⚠️ Vision, not a commitment
No delivery placement has been piloted. This is a founder-articulated long-term direction captured for investor conversations (see VC Deck), not a near-term roadmap item.
