🔬 Discovery Hypotheses & Methodology

Problem-Solution Fit Overview

Steve Blank's customer discovery phase starts by identifying a core problem worth solving. In our context, the massive paradigm shift of generative AI means the job market is expecting high technical AI skills overnight.

😖 The Pain Point
Professionals fear losing market value due to rapid AI shifts. They need credentials that demonstrate high competency, but typical training material is boring, dry, and slow.
💡 The Solution Hypothesis
Visual-rich, animated AI Certification helper content that breaks down high-level architectural concepts (like Claude AI Architect Professional) into fast, high-retention lessons.

✅ Key Discovery Validation Tasks

  • 📋Conduct audience surveys regarding specific exam pain points.
  • 🎥Create rapid concept videos (shorts/long form) to observe organic click-through rates.
  • 💬Monitor comment sections for qualitative validation of the visual training style.

🎬 MVP Concept: Animated Tech training Channel

The Video Production Pipeline

Building a structured video production pipeline is essential to test our value proposition without excessive initial investment. The pipeline translates complex certification curricula into visual assets.

  • 📝Scripting: Distilling official exam blueprints into clear, structured voiceovers.
  • 🎨Storyboarding: Creating dynamic illustrations representing servers, LLM chains, APIs, and safety guardrails.
  • 💻Animation: Rendering clean vector animations to keep visual interest and explain logic step-by-step (modeled in the Canva Animation Plan).
  • 🎙️Voice & Mix: Crisp audio recording overlayed with light background music to maintain momentum.
🎬

Prototype Stage

Target: Release first 3 animated lessons on YouTube to measure real-world audience response.

⚠️ Sanity Check: Missing Questions & Risks

Missing Questions:

  • Have we identified the exact profile details of early adopters who will pay for mock exam preparation?
  • What specific animation styles yield the absolute highest retention rate?

Key Risks:

  • Time Commitment Risk: Designing animations is very time-consuming; Rifat could face editing fatigue before achieving standard organic traction.
  • Hypothesis Invalidity: Organic YouTube views do not guarantee willingness to join cohorts or pay for subscription access.
