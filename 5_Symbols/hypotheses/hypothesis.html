🔍 Stage 1 — Customer Discovery

🟡 Partially Validated

H1 · Rising AI skills expectations

P1: Rapid AI advancement is reshaping job roles and technical expectations. P2: Employers expect staff to build agentic chains, configure guardrails, and audit LLM architectures. Conclusion: Demand for AI/LLM certification is structurally rising, not a fad. Status: Corroborated by WEF 2025 Skills Data and Claude Partner Network Tiers. New (2026-08-04): Anthropic, Google, and Microsoft have all shipped competing AI certification programs this year — see Evidence Map →. No direct search-volume experiment run yet.
🧪 In Testing

H2 · Animated content format

P1: Standard cert-prep material causes cognitive fatigue and low completion. P2: Animated, visually distilled explanations increase retention. Conclusion: An animated video format will outperform standard slide decks on watch retention. Status: Target is >40% watch retention on animated lessons vs. standard slide decks. FORMAL MVP metric. First real data point (2026-08-04): a produced short scored 68.5% retention — see Content Analysis →. Style templates & storyboard guidelines are mapped out on the Canva Animation Plan →.
🟡 In Progress

H3 · Audience will pay for cert prep

P1: Viewers who complete free content have expressed problem-level need. P2: A subset of highly engaged viewers will convert to paying customers if offered a low-friction next step. Conclusion: Newsletter signup with visible pricing will surface real purchase intent. Status: Target: >5% email signup conversion from a newsletter page that shows pricing up front. Newsletter build is now in progress — live at rifaterdemsahin.github.io/email-newsletter →, with two capture points (LinkedIn + YouTube) feeding it as backups for each other across different audiences — see Funnel & Channels →. See Task List →.
🧪 In Testing

H24 · Emotional Pain Drivers

P1: Preparing for professional exams is high-friction, stressful, and expensive; happy and content professionals rarely volunteer for this. P2: Candidates are driven by fear of obsolescence, greed for pay premiums/leverage, or insecurity (imposter syndrome). Conclusion: Marketing and product design must target anxiety relief, career security, and status/financial shortcuts rather than just dry academic information. Status: Qualitative discovery interviews log that candidates seek certifications to protect their career longevity or lock in raises (e.g. Tuncer, Brian, and others). Target is >80% of interviewed candidates citing fear, greed, or insecurity as their top driver. See Customer Pain Points Page →.

📈 Stage 2 — Customer Validation

🟡 In Testing

H4 · ~1% free→paid conversion

P1: Free YouTube content builds trust and traffic at scale. P2: A fraction of viewers will value the $10/mo community enough to subscribe. Conclusion: Out of every 1,000 free-course views, at least 10 viewers will join the $10/mo membership. Status: No longer untested — a free weekly cohort has run for 8 consecutive weeks (Jun–Jul 2026) with a steady audience of ~4 attendees per session. That validates free content driving repeat live engagement; the specific 1% view→paid-membership figure is still unmeasured. Production baseline of 3 animations/week now tracked — see Task List →.
🟡 In Testing

H5 · Cohorts sell out organically

P1: Cohort announcements reach an engaged audience via video and newsletter calls to action. P2 (delivery model defined): $10/mo members get access to recorded cohort replays; the $250 package buys live attendance with screen-share participation across a day-long session with scheduled breaks (no pass guarantee). Conclusion: 20–40 students will enroll per cohort launch with $0 ad spend. Status: Upgraded 2026-08-11 — the Skool LMS is now live (skool.com), with 8 cohort recordings uploaded, levels configured, and the invite link shared. 8 signups recorded, the first real data point on this hypothesis, though not yet confirmed as paid enrollments. See Skool LMS & Community Integration →.
⚠️ Claimed, Unverified

H6 · TAM/SAM/SOM market size

P1: ~25M developers worldwide must adapt to AI engineering (TAM). P2: ~3M of them actively seek certifications (SAM). Conclusion: ~50k developers are reachable via our channels within 3 years (SOM). Status: Grounded in WEF and cloud proxy data (see McKinsey study on AI Labor Shifts). New (2026-08-04): the "Forward Deployed Engineer" role — a concrete embodiment of this SAM segment — has job postings up 700–800%+ YoY. Still a working estimate.

Conclusion: 10% video→newsletter CTR, 2% newsletter→cohort conversion. Status: Untested. Has not been numerically reconciled with H4's 1% view conversion. H8 · Exam-ready PMF

P1: The cohort curriculum covers the full scope of the certification in real time. P2: Live instruction and peer accountability outperform self-study. P3: The company does not guarantee passing the exam (issued by Anthropic). Conclusion: Cohort graduates will pass the target exam at a high rate and recommend the program. Status: Target is >80% internal pass-rate, NPS >50. (See the official Claude Certified Architect Program by Anthropic via Pearson VUE, active at $175). New (2026-08-04): the free cohort audience is already self-organizing on WhatsApp between sessions — a live peer-accountability signal, see Evidence Map →.
