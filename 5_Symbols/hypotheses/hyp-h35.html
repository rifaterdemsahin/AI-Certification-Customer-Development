🏅 H35 · Repo-Count Badge Ladder Drives Real-Skill Showcasing

⚪ Hypothesized / Proposed

A 9-rank badge ladder (Cadet/Explorer → Delivery Rainmaker) where every rank is earned purely by shipping repos — not points, posts, or attendance — gets members showcasing real, working proof of skill instead of theory. The repo target roughly doubles (and then some) per rank, from 1 repo at Level 1 to 1000 repos at Level 9. See the ladder on Cohort Prep · 🏅 Badges.

🧭 Premise → Conclusion → Status

📐 The ladder

  • 🛫Level 1 — Cadet / Explorer: 1 repo. Unlocks Certified Architect Professional. Projected 88% of members.
  • ⚡Level 2 — Active Contributor: 2 repos. Unlocks Exam Prep. Projected 6% of members.
  • 🧭Level 3 — Study Regular: 6 repos. Projected 3% of members.
  • 🛠️Level 4 — Flight Engineer: 13 repos. Projected 3% of members.
  • 💼Level 5 — Senior Practitioner: 32 repos.
  • 🎯Level 6 — Mission Specialist: 75 repos.
  • 🛡️Level 7 — Squadron Leader: 178 repos.
  • 🧑‍✈️Level 8 — Pilot Graduate: 422 repos.
  • 🌟Level 9 — Delivery Rainmaker: 1000 repos, the top rank.
  • 📏Grading order (founder-stated): count first (did they ship?), then check the repos work, then raise the bar on quality — with the program and the community helping each other motivate and build together.
