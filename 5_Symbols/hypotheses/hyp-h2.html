🔍 H2 · Animated content format

🧪 In Testing

🧭 Premise → Conclusion → Status

📋 Full Detail

  • P1:Standard cert-prep material (500-page manuals, 40-hour courses) causes cognitive fatigue and low completion.
  • P2:Animated, visually distilled explanations increase retention.
  • Conclusion:An animated video format will outperform standard slide decks on watch retention.
  • Target Metric:>40% average watch retention on the animated version. FORMAL MVP metric.
  • Design Plan:Canva Animation Design & Planning board →
🧪 In Testing
Empirical telemetry confirms animation superiority: YouTube Studio Analytics (last 28 days) shows multiple animations clearing >60% retention — 68.5% on the first measured short (youtu.be/F8IBooe3bXY), 66.3% on Claude AI Certification for Architects | Masterclass Intro (0:07), and 62.6% (34 views) on Building an AI Knowledge Engine: Turn 46k Obsidian Notes into Clarity (0:43). In contrast, 2m+ non-animated tutorials averaged 10.7%–17.9% — see Content Analysis for the full 10-video per-video log. New (2026-08-19): two independent viewers of AI Isn't a Bubble did not disagree with the content. They rejected an automated voice on the Skool ask. Voice is a separate variable from format.
