🚀 H22 · Certified-talent delivery placement (FDE model)

📅 Planned

🧭 Premise → Conclusion → Status

📋 Full Detail

  • P1:Customer Creation (Stage 3) builds a large, certified community/audience through content and cohorts.
  • P2:That same certified community can be piloted as a workforce-placement pool for enterprises needing embedded AI/agent practitioners — the "Forward Deployed Engineer" role already growing 700–800%+ YoY (H6), with average base salary ~$171,911 and senior packages reaching $400k–$500k.
  • P3:This repositions the long-term company from a content/education business into a certified-talent placement and recruiting company built around its own community — a materially different, higher-value model, framed as VC-ready once the underlying community is proven.
  • Conclusion:In Company Building (Stage 4), after Customer Creation validates the community, the company should pilot "delivery placements" — placing certified graduates with enterprises as embedded talent — as a new revenue line and strategic direction.
📅 Planned — explicitly a later-stage vision
Out of scope for Discovery, Validation, and Customer Creation. No pilot placement has been attempted; this is a founder-articulated long-term direction captured for investor conversations (see VC Deck), not a near-term commitment.
