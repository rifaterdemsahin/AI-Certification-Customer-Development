📦 H21 · $29 Exam Prep Bundle is a viable entry SKU

📅 Planned

🧭 Premise → Conclusion → Status

📋 Full Detail

  • P1:Some prospects want a fast, self-paced way to test the offer before committing to the $10/mo membership or the $250–$500 live cohort.
  • P2:Memory cards + prep exam + mock exam, bundled as one $29 one-time purchase, covers a distinct, lower-commitment use case neither existing tier serves directly.
  • P3:This is additive, not a replacement — the bundle's content is already included within both the $10/mo membership and the $250–$500 live cohort; the $29 price exists purely for prospects who want only this piece, so cannibalization risk is structurally low.
  • Conclusion:Offering the $29 bundle alongside existing tiers will convert a segment of prospects who wouldn't have purchased the higher-commitment options, without materially cannibalizing them.
📅 Planned
Planned — a newly proposed SKU (see Exam Prep Bundle); not yet built, priced, or sold. No cannibalization or conversion data exists yet against the $10/mo membership or $250–$500 cohort tiers. New (2026-08-10): Discovery interview confirms the Associate exam is perceived as workflow/PM-heavy, with a "50/50" pass rate via common sense alone. This confirms that candidates seek low-friction, lower-commitment entry prep SKUs (like the $29 Mock Exam bundle) for Associate, while saving high-ticket cohorts for hands-on Developer tiers.
