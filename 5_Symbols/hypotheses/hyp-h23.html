🚀 H23 · 4DX Weekly Accountable Rhythm

🟡 In Testing

🧭 Premise → Conclusion → Status

📋 Full Detail

  • P1:A solo founder running a business as a second job has very limited hours (Founder Bandwidth). Urgent daily tasks of the main contracting role and operations create a "whirlwind" that naturally consumes all available energy.
  • P2:By establishing exactly 1-2 Wildly Important Goals (WIGs) and focusing efforts on weekly, influenceable lead measures (such as script reviews, animation density, and LinkedIn outreach) rather than just waiting for sales results, the founder gains a high-leverage predictive control loop.
  • P3:A weekly self-audit cadence (weekly commitments and a visual scoreboard) provides immediate status visibility and forces regular, strategic course corrections.
  • Conclusion:Adhering to this weekly 4DX rhythm will enable the founder to clear the Stage 1 and Stage 2 gates within the timelines estimated in Stage Timelines without burnout.
🟡 In Testing — Weekly rhythm initiated
The 4DX dashboard and sub-pages are created. Weekly lead metrics are logged, and commitments are audited weekly in the cadence meeting log. Target is ≥80% weekly compliance on updating the scoreboard and review log.
