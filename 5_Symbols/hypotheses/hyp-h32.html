📐 The Line — Five Steps in Priestley’s Words, Reworded

He draws a line. Below is getting ready. Above is running a company. Treat an entrepreneur as a scientist running fast, cheap tests.

Below the line — not yet the company

1. Apprenticeship (6 months–2 years)
Work beside an experienced operator until three things are obvious: commercial awareness (how sales and customers actually work), self-awareness (strengths and weaknesses), and access to resources (how to get money, ads, or a salesperson). You know you have them when “how does this business sell?” is not a mystery.
2. Open-and-shut 90-day side hustles
Launch a campaign that ends at day 90. Pop-up client, nightclub party, sales training — whatever you might later build. Not about profit. About confidence. Do not get permanently stuck here. Priestley did two years with John, then 90-day hustles, then the first real company.

Above the line — you are the entrepreneur

3. CHAOS
Concept, Audience, Offer, Sales. Two-person scout team. MVPs (waitlist, landing page, event, trial). 30–150 one-to-one conversations. Nos are free consulting. Founder Opportunity Fit = pain you solved + you were paid + you care. Spreadsheets, not a CRM. Monday: 3–6 high-value things. Friday: debrief.
4. Four-person ramp
Associate Key Person of Influence (not you — they lend a name). You on sales/marketing. Customer success / delivery. Operations. Two products: product for prospects (assessment, intro workshop) leading to a 1:1, then core offer in gold / silver / bronze. Perfect Repeatable Week aimed at ~$50–$100k/month. Daily social on 3 platforms. Ads only at an allowable cost per sale. JVs and a squad. Still free tools + WhatsApp.
5. Seven-figure (you are the KPI)
Founder-led growth: your name and face. 8–12 people, not more (or it splits). Four products: gift, product for prospects, core, product for clients (maintenance / subscription after the transformation). Three-part year: Perfect Repeatable Week + Spotlight every 3–4 months + one Annual Big Message. CRM, AI, portal, Slack. ~$20–$25k/week, $100k/month. Sleep-at-Night dashboard. Then stop and enjoy it.

🧪 CHAOS Detail — What to Do This Stage

Founder Opportunity Fit

Priestley: overlap of a pain you removed, something you were paid for, and something you care about. Here that overlap is enterprise AI delivery + contractor rates + teaching certs. Ten ideas, then three or four, then MVPs. Do not invent a new idea because YouTube likes glossary clips.

Waitlist / MVP questions

On join: (1) who are you, (2) result you want, (3) biggest friction, (4) what else you tried, (5) budget. Skool membership questions and self-assessment already collect this. A “no” is free consulting, not a mood.

30–150 conversations

Anything under 30 is not statistically useful. Upper band ~150. Benchmark he cites: 10 sales from that set, a few thousand each, $10k–$100k as proof CHAOS works — same order as this site’s H9 $10k gate, except we require it twice. Sunday + VIP 1:1 count. Swipe-feed views do not.

Product for prospects vs core

Promote the easy first step, not the expensive identity change. Prospects: glossary Shorts, self-assessment, Peek $0, free Sunday. Core: Sit In $1 / Share Screen $250, $10/mo, $250–$500 cohort. Promoting only “get certified / become an FDE” is the expensive ask he says fails.
