What Does It Mean When a Hypothesis Isn't Connected?

HYPOTHESIS.md's Dependency Map (added v1.16.0) gives every one of the 19 tracked hypotheses a Depends on line (upstream prerequisites it explicitly cites) and a Feeds line (downstream hypotheses that cite it back). Most hypotheses have at least one of those two edges. A handful don't — and "no connection" means three genuinely different things depending on which edge is missing. This page defines each pattern with the site's own real examples, so a disconnected node in the pipeline diagram isn't misread as a uniform red flag.

🔀 Three Patterns, Not One

⚠️ Fully Isolated

No Depends on, no Feeds

Example: H14 (multi-certification market expansion). The tracker states it plainly: "independent, untested — no other hypothesis cites or is cited by H14 in the text."

Nothing in the tracker currently relies on H14 being true, and H14 doesn't lean on any other hypothesis's result either. If H14 is validated or killed, it ripples nowhere else — a self-contained bet.

🟡 Foundational, Uncited Gap

No Depends on, Feeds implied but not written

Examples: H1 and H6 (rising AI skills expectations; TAM/SAM/SOM sizing). Flagged in HYPOTHESIS.md as a "coverage gap": both logically underlie the Stage 3 channel bets (H12, H14, H16, H18) — a market too small or a skills-gap that isn't real would sink all four — but no hypothesis's own text cross-cites either one.

This is a documentation weakness, not a business one: the reasoning exists in the site's narrative, just not as a traceable edge in the tracker.

🧪 Terminal Leaf

Has Depends on, no Feeds

Examples: H8, H10, H16, H18, H19. Each cites at least one upstream hypothesis but nothing downstream cites it back.

This is structurally normal — every dependency chain needs endpoints. These are conclusions the tracker is built toward, not claims other hypotheses are built on. No action item implied.

🎯 What To Actually Do About Each One

  • ⚠️Fully isolated (H14): lower priority to test right now — nothing else's status is blocked on it, so it's a safe one to defer while founder bandwidth is spent on hypotheses that gate other work (see Single-Founder Bandwidth).
  • 🟡Foundational, uncited gap (H1, H6): worth an explicit cross-citation edit precisely because they're the two most-foundational premises on the site — a silent failure in either one would put H12, H14, H16, and H18 at risk without the tracker showing why. HYPOTHESIS.md already names this as an open to-do, not yet resolved.
  • 🧪Terminal leaves (H8, H10, H16, H18, H19): no action needed — being a leaf is their intended role in the graph, not a gap to close.
