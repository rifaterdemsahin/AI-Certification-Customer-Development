📋 Full Detail

  • P1:Candidates book Pearson VUE exams roughly 3 weeks out and shift study behavior from theory to retrieval practice. Charles's own confirmed window for the exam he sat was 2 weeks; the ~3-week figure is a multi-candidate pattern, not his personal timeline.
  • P2:Standard mock exams and question banks are the single most critical asset anxious buyers seek during this phase. Theory content without retrieval practice does not convert the booking window into a sit date.
  • Conclusion:Without official blueprint-mapped practice exams and question banks, the customer-development value proposition is incomplete and conversions stall. This is the value-blocker H21's $29 bundle is designed to solve, and it informs the cohort curriculum pivot in H8.
