⚙️ How Skool Payouts Work

1. Wednesday Payout Cadence

Skool automatically sweeps and pays out your available balance every Wednesday. If your available balance on Wednesday is above $0, it triggers an automated ACH/BACS transfer to the connected bank account.

2. 7–14 Day Settlement Window

Pending is balance from transactions that have not yet settled through the banking clearing system. Every transaction takes 7 to 14 days to transition from Pending to Available.

3. First Payment Math Example

On the $1/mo "Sit In" tier, the gross transaction of $1.00 incurs standard merchant interchange (~2.9% + 30¢), leaving exactly $0.60 net pending balance in Delivery Pilot.
