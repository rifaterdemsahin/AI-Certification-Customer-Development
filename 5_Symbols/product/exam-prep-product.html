📦 Exam Prep Bundle

Memory cards, a prep exam, and a mock exam — a self-paced, one-time-purchase product for $29, sitting below the $10/mo membership as a low-friction entry point. Built to be picked up and used immediately: most exam takers book their real exam slot about 3 weeks in advance, so the bundle is packaged to be easy to take with no setup or onboarding, not something that requires weeks to get value from. Tracked as H21 in the Hypothesis Tracker.

✅ Already included if you're a member or cohort attendee
This bundle's content isn't exclusive — it's already included as part of the $10/mo membership and the $250–$500 live cohort. The $29 one-time price exists purely for people who want only this piece without subscribing or committing to a live cohort seat.

📋 What's Included

🗂️ Memory Cards

A structured flashcard set mapped directly to the official exam blueprint, for fast spaced-repetition review.

📝 Prep Exam

A single practice run covering the full blueprint, meant to be taken early to identify weak areas before deeper study.

🎯 Mock Exam

A timed, exam-condition run intended as the final check before sitting the real Pearson VUE-proctored exam.

🧠 Why This Works: The Mock-Exam Confidence Loop

The mock exam is the most important piece of this bundle, not an afterthought. Real discovery-call evidence — Charles among the named contacts already tracked in H3 and H8 — shows a consistent pattern: candidates book their real Pearson VUE exam slot roughly 3 weeks out, study hard against that fixed deadline, and use a mock exam as the final test of whether they're actually ready. Passing the mock exam is the specific moment anxiety turns into confidence — candidates report feeling relaxed and exam-ready right after, not gradually over weeks of study. That emotional shift, not just the content review, is what this product is really selling. See Target Audience for the full behavior-pattern write-up.
