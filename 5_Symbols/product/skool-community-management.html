🌱 Skool Community Management: The Garden Playbook

Good communities are like well-kept gardens: you must prune the toxic weeds (Broken Window Theory), add water (consistent engagement and fresh members), and leave room for emergent culture. This operational guide details the daily 5-minute management checklist and strict moderation protocols for AI Certification Helper.

🌿 The 3 Laws of the Community Garden

1

Remove the Weeds

Broken Window Theory: If one toxic comment, exam dump request, or spam promo sits unmoderated for 24 hours, other members conclude the room is unmanaged and stop posting valuable work.

Action: Immediately delete negative, cynical, or spam posts.
2

Add Water

Keep the environment fertile with fresh member introductions, provocative technical questions, weekly Sunday challenges, and prompt engagement.

Action: Greet newcomers within 12 hours & host Sunday practice rooms.
3

Leave Room for Culture

Don't micromanage every thread. Allow organic memes, study rituals, inside jokes, and peer-to-peer discussions to emerge naturally without founder domination.

Action: Listen more than you speak (H29).

⏱️ The #1 Secret: The Daily 5-Minute Care Checklist

Execute this exact sequence every morning in 5 to 10 minutes before starting daytime contract work:

  • 1️⃣Visit Your Community: Open skool.com/delivery-pilot-8938.
  • 2️⃣Clear the Admin Queue: Check notification bell, unread DMs, reported content flags, and pending membership requests.
  • 3️⃣Read New Posts & Comments:
    • 👍 Like things that add value or show vulnerability.
    • 🗑️ Delete anything that ruins the vibe or violates community rules.
    • 💬 Join the conversation where it feels natural (ask an open question).
    • 📌 Pin great contributions to the top of the feed to make stars out of members.
  • 4️⃣Build Core Relationships: Send 1 direct message to an active regular or founding member to check in on their certification study schedule.
  • 5️⃣Temperature Check: Ask yourself: "How does the room feel today? Is it warm and active, or silent and cold?"
  • 🌲Nurture Evergreen Hubs: Update living resource posts (e.g. "Courses") in place instead of creating new broadcast threads.
Second-Job Constraint: Never spend 2 hours browsing. 5–10 structured minutes daily outperforms a 3-hour weekend dump.

🛡️ Moderation Protocol: Dealing with Trouble Members

🚫 Clearly Spam / Malicious Links

Crypto bots, self-promotional link dumping, direct exam question dumps, or malicious files.

Protocol: Instant Ban + Delete All Activity for Past 7 Days (Zero tolerance).

⚠️ Rule Violations (Negativity / Bullying / Friction)

Hostile responses to newcomers, cynical trolling, or condescending technical arrogance.

  • 1st Offense: Delete post + send private polite DM explaining the community vibe guidelines.
  • 2nd Offense: 7-day mute / suspension from commenting.
  • 3rd Offense: Permanent ban + membership refund/cancellation.
