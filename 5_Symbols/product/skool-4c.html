🧭 4C — Cause, Culture, Container, Calls

A community that lasts has four layers: a Cause people would defend, a Culture they can feel in the first hour, a Container that holds the work, and Calls that put bodies in the same room on a clock. Delivery Pilot already has all four on this site — they were just named separately. This page is the map, not a fifth product. Mapping them does not sell a cohort (see H5) and does not prove exam-ready PMF (see H8).

🗺️ The four C's in this business

If one layer is missing, the others leak. A strong Cause with no Calls is a manifesto. Calls with no Culture is a lecture. A Container with no Cause is an empty Skool group. Diagnose first; then open the page that owns that layer.

1. Cause — why this room exists
Not “get certified.” The cause is the contractor path: Hands-on Training → Vendor Certification → CV / recruiter promotion → Ultimate IT Contractor Course. Members want the Forward Deployed Engineer / independent contractor persona (£500–£1,000+/day), not another lecture. The About tab must not promise the exam. See H30.
Delivery Pilot Roadmap · About tab · Audience Transformation · Why Certification
2. Culture — how people behave here
Party, not lecture. Don’t lecture, build friendships, curate culture. Listen more than you speak. Living hubs instead of spam threads. Level 2 posting gate. Garden playbook. Founder posts are evergreen, not a notification firehose. See H29 and H8.
Golden Rules · Listen More Than You Speak · Garden Playbook · Rules & bans
3. Container — where the work lives
Skool is the Stage 2 LMS: Classroom, board, calendar, billing. Public group so strangers can read; members post. YouTube and LinkedIn acquire; Skool delivers. Living hubs (Courses) instead of one-off threads. Join via the group URL, not a raw /live/ link. Container is not the Cause.
Skool LMS · Living hubs · Public vs Private · YouTube → Skool handoff
4. Calls — the clock that makes it real
Sunday 13:30 UK screen-share is the shared ritual. Monday VIP Claude Cowork is recruiter-facing, not a second lecture. Saturday VIP 1:1 tracks are bandwidth-capped. $0 / $1 joins are not paid enrollments; VIP is Sunday in the room, not on-demand 1:1. See H5.
How to join Sunday · Calendar · Monday VIP Cowork · Peek / Sit In / Share Screen
