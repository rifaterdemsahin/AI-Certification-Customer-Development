🚀 Skool Onboarding Checklist: The 3-Step Activation Engine

In Customer Creation (Stage 3), acquisition volume is meaningless if new joiners fail to activate. To maximize Day-1 retention, eliminate blank-slate friction, and establish recurring learning habits, the Delivery Pilot Skool community activates the Onboarding Checklist (Welcome Plugin). This pinned widget guides every new member through three essential actions: watching the 60-second orientation, leaving their first comment, and downloading the mobile app.

📌 The Pinned Onboarding Checklist Widget

When enabled by community admins, Skool renders this persistent banner at the top of the main community feed for every newcomer until all 3 micro-commitments are completed:

👋 Welcome! Start here
✓ Watch 60 sec intro video — Orient to the Sunday live practice room & Classroom.
✓ Find a post you like and leave a comment — Introduce yourself and engage with peers.
✓ Download app — Install Skool iOS/Android for live session push alerts.
1. Visual Orientation
Replaces 10-page text manuals with a crisp 60-second video explaining where Sunday call links live and how to navigate the 7 Classroom modules.
2. Comment Synergy
Synergizes directly with the Level 2 Posting Restriction by prompting the exact comment activity needed to earn 5 points and unlock posting.
3. Mobile Retention Loop
App downloads enable mobile push notifications for Sunday 9pm cohort calls and Monday VIP Claude Cowork, dramatically lowering 30-day churn.

🛠️ Admin Configuration Guide (How to Set Up in Skool)

Follow these step-by-step administrative instructions to activate the Onboarding Checklist in the Delivery Pilot community:

1

Navigate to Group Settings

Open your Delivery Pilot community dashboard and click on SETTINGS located in the right-hand sidebar menu.

2

Open Plugins / Onboarding

In the left navigation menu of group settings, select Plugins (or Gamification / Onboarding).

3

Toggle Welcome Plugin ON

Locate the Welcome / Onboarding Checklist card and switch the toggle to ON.

4

Link 60-Sec Video & Save

Paste the URL of your pinned 60-second "Welcome to Delivery Pilot" orientation video post. Click Save Changes.

📈 Customer Creation & Scale Dynamics

🎯 1. Closes the YouTube/LinkedIn Handoff

When top-of-funnel traffic arrives from YouTube videos or LinkedIn posts (see Audience Handoff), visitors often feel lost upon entering a new platform. The 3-step checklist immediately gives them clear direction within 10 seconds of joining.

🛡️ 2. Fixes the "Leaky Bucket" Churn

According to retention benchmarks (see Churn Reduction), >70% of community churn happens within the first 72 hours due to lack of initial interaction. Prompting an immediate comment and app install converts passive browsers into active learners.

⚡ 3. Multiplies 1,000x Sub Growth (H13)

In Customer Creation (H13), compounding subscriber growth requires high community NPS. Members who complete onboarding attend Sunday live sessions at a 4x higher rate, providing the testimonials and word-of-mouth driving viral expansion.

🤝 4. Accelerates 10 True Regulars (H8)

By encouraging members to immediately comment on active posts, the checklist populates existing discussions with fresh peer perspectives, accelerating the milestone of reaching 10 un-poked regulars (H8).
