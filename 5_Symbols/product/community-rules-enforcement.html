⚖️ Community Rules: Deletions, Suspensions & Bans

A high-trust learning community requires clear, unwavering boundaries. When a peer room tolerates spam, negativity, or exam cheating, high-caliber engineers leave. This policy details the exact triggers, mechanical enforcement, and escalation procedures for when a post is deleted, when an account is suspended, and when a user is permanently banned across Skool, Sunday live cohorts, YouTube, and LinkedIn.

🚦 The 3-Tier Moderation Framework

Tier 1 · Content Removal

Post Deleted

The post or comment violates formatting, relevancy, or quality standards. The content is removed, and the author receives an automated or private moderator note with the specific rule cited.

Impact: Content removed · Account status active · 1st warning logged.
Tier 2 · Temporary Timeout

Account Suspended (7–30 Days)

Repeated content violations, aggressive tone, or unsolicited private messaging. Posting and commenting privileges are revoked; member retains read-only access to study materials during cooling off.

Impact: Read-only access · Cannot post or join live calls · 2nd strike.
Tier 3 · Permanent Expulsion

Permanent Ban & Blacklist

Severe violations (harassment, exam brain dumps, scraping, mass DM spam). Account is terminated immediately, all previous posts deleted, membership cancelled without refund, and credentials blacklisted.

Impact: Instant ban · Zero refund · Permanent block across all platforms.

🗑️ 1. When a Post or Comment Gets Deleted

We maintain an uncluttered, signal-dense environment. Posts are removed within 15 minutes of detection when they violate any of the following rules:

🚫 Self-Promotion & Unapproved Links

  • 🔗Dropping external affiliate links, personal course promotions, Discord invites, or WhatsApp group links without moderator pre-approval.
  • 💼"DM me for AI consulting/freelance work" pitch posts.
  • 🪙Crypto, NFT, or get-rich-quick side hustle solicitations.

🛑 Exam Brain Dumps & NDA Violations

  • 📝Posting exact verbatim proctored questions from Pearson VUE / Anthropic exam pools (e.g., "Here is question 14 from my test yesterday").
  • ⚠️Sharing unauthorized exam dump PDFs or paid question leaks. (We teach deep architectural reasoning, not memorization dumps).

🤖 Generative AI Slop & Low-Effort Clutter

  • 📜Copy-pasting 10-paragraph raw ChatGPT / Claude monologues without personal commentary, working code, or a specific question.
  • 💬One-word replies or empty emoji threads that clutter the main feed.

🗣️ Cynicism, Snark & Bad Vibes

  • 😒Mocking beginner questions or condescending replies (e.g., "If you don't know Python you shouldn't be here").
  • 🌧️Chronic defeatism and unconstructive venting without proposing a solution or asking for assistance.

📥 Post-Deletion Procedure

When your post is deleted, you will receive a private notification explaining the specific violation. If the post contained useful technical thought alongside an unapproved link, you are welcome to edit the post and resubmit it cleanly. A deleted post counts as an informal notice before formal strikes are issued.

⏳ 2. When an Account Gets Suspended (7–30 Day Timeout)

Suspension is a formal cooling-off period. During a suspension, the member cannot create posts, comment on threads, send private DMs, or unmute/screen-share in Sunday live sessions.

Suspension Trigger Duration Operational Action Reinstatement Condition
Repeated Content Violations (Strike 2) 7 Days Posting & commenting permissions temporarily toggled off in Skool admin. Automatic expiration after 7 days + acknowledgment of rules.
Unsolicited DM Outreach (Pitching) 14 Days Direct message access restricted; warning recorded in member file. Formal written commitment to cease uninvited commercial DMing.
Disruptive Live Room Behavior 14 Days Excluded from Sunday live call link; Zoom/Skool event mute applied. Review call with community lead or founder prior to re-entry.
Plagiarism of Student Architecture Work 30 Days Removal of plagiarized artifact + removal from gamified leaderboard. Submission of original project & public apology to original author.

🚨 3. When a User Gets Permanently Banned (Zero Tolerance)

Permanent bans are non-negotiable and executed immediately without prior warning for severe breaches of community safety, ethics, and legal integrity.

1. Harassment, Bullying, Discrimination & Hate Speech

Any personal insults, racial, gender-based, or religious discrimination, sexual harassment, or doxxing of real-world personal information. Action: Instant ban, immediate transcript preservation for safety records.

2. Mass Scraping & Automated DM Spam Campaigns

Using browser extensions or scripts to scrape member lists from the community directory to execute cold email or cold LinkedIn spam campaigns. Action: Instant ban, domain-level blacklisting, and report to Skool Trust & Safety.

3. Fraud, Stolen Payment Credentials & Voucher Reselling

Using fraudulent credit cards, sharing pirated exam voucher discount codes, or attempting to resell subscription access. Action: Instant ban, chargeback dispute logging, and payment processor fraud reporting.

4. Ban Evasion via Alternate Accounts

Creating secondary dummy accounts to bypass an active suspension or permanent ban. Action: Immediate ban on all associated IP blocks and email subdomains.

📬 Appeals & Dispute Resolution Process

Mistakes can happen. If you believe your post was mistakenly deleted or your account incorrectly suspended:

  1. Review the Rule: Cross-check your deleted post or warning against the exact guidelines on this page.
  2. Submit an Appeal: Send a polite email directly to support@aicertificationhelper.com with your Skool handle and context.
  3. Founder Review: Appeals are personally reviewed by Rifat Erdem Sahin during the weekly administrative batch (Thursday evening).
  4. Final Decision: You will receive a written explanation within 5 business days. Fast-track bans for hate speech, harassment, or exam leaks are non-appealable.
