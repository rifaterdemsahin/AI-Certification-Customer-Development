📚 Definitions

ARR
Annual Recurring Revenue

Predictable revenue normalized to a yearly rate. This site uses $100,000 ARR as the Stage 4 hiring trigger and the point the founder transitions to full-time.

Appears on: Company Building H11 H15 Quality Gates Cost-Side Model
A/B Testing
A/B (split) testing

Comparing two versions of something (a video format, a headline, a price) against each other to see which performs better, using real measured results rather than opinion.

Appears on: H2 Exam Topic Blueprint Test Plan
API
Application Programming Interface

A programmatic way for software to talk to Claude directly, distinct from the consumer chat app. Referenced here mainly re: certification update releases and technical integration.

Appears on: Roadmap Spec PMF Spec Cohort Prep
B2B
Business-to-Business

Selling to companies (consulting firms, enterprises) rather than individual consumers. The channel behind H12's bulk-cohort-seat thesis.

Appears on: H12 Partner With Us Competitive Analysis BMC Channels
CAC
Customer Acquisition Cost

How much it costs, on average, to acquire one paying customer. This site's organic funnel targets $0 CAC through Stage 2; paid ads only turn on if CAC stays below LTV.

Appears on: When to Start Paid Ads Paid Advertisement H16 Unit Economics Funnel Spec
CTR
Click-Through Rate

The percentage of people who click a link after seeing it (e.g. video viewers who click through to the newsletter). Core to H7's funnel model.

Appears on: H7 Funnel Math Funnel Spec Content Analysis
Cocreate
Audience-originated product change, credited by name

The third job in Rifat Erdem Sahin's Stage 1–2 role: leave something unfinished, let members vote or answer, ship the change they asked for, and credit them in public. A slogan on a landing page is not cocreation. A named person on a shipped change is. Tracked as H29.

Full definition: Listen More Than You Speak · Appears on: Golden Rules H29
Core Four
Alex Hormozi, $100M Leads: the four ways to get leads

Warm outreach (1-to-1, people who know you), posted content (1-to-many, public), cold outreach (1-to-1, strangers), and paid ads (1-to-many, rented attention). This business is allowed warm outreach and posted content through Stage 2; ads stay locked by H16.

Full audit: Alex Hormozi Agent · Appears on: When to Start Paid Ads Organic Growth
Default Alive
Paul Graham's operating test: can the company reach profitability on its current trajectory without another raise?

If yes, the conversation can be about ambition; if default dead, the conversation is about survival. Applied on this site as the reason not to raise venture capital during Customer Discovery. See VF1 on the constructive-feedback page.

Full definition: VC Constructive Feedback · Source: Graham, 2015

Customer-facing copy at skool.com/delivery-pilot-8938/about. Must not say “get certified.” The listing line is the one-sentence offer (Sunday 9pm, Peek / Sit In / Share Screen). The About tab explains the room, the study path, and what we do not do. Plans stay on the join page.

Full paste: Delivery Pilot About · Related: Delivery Pilot Offer H5

DoD Directive 8140
U.S. Department of Defense cybersecurity workforce directive

A real U.S. government policy mandating staff certification in cybersecurity/IT roles — cited as precedent for why government-contracting consultancies already treat mandated certification as normal practice (H12).

Appears on: H12
FDE
Forward Deployed Engineer

An on-the-ground, client-embedded engineer who configures and operationalizes LLM/agent deployments. Named evidence for H6's SAM sizing (700–800%+ YoY job-posting growth) and the model behind H22's long-term talent-placement vision.

Appears on: H6 H22 Market Spec VC Deck
FedRAMP

A U.S. government standard for cloud security assessment and authorization. Listed as a compliance topic in the Architect exam blueprint.

First 3 named Skool community seats

Skool's community course tells the founder to invite three people before growing. On this site those seats are Marianna (corporate partner, H17), Sude (Sunday cohort student), and Bayo. A founding-member join is a warm invite, not a paid $10/mo or $250–$500 enrollment.

Appears on: Skool Steps & Founding Members H5 H17 Partner With Us
GDPR

The EU's data-privacy law. Listed as a compliance topic in the Architect exam blueprint.

Appears on: Exam Topic Blueprint
Golden Rules
Don't lecture · Build friendships · Curate the culture

Skool's three community-course operating rules, mapped onto Delivery Pilot as the playbook between the first 3 founding members and 10 true regulars. A lecture board is founder posts with no replies. A party board asks unfinished questions, DMs people by name, and publicly praises the first non-founder voice.

A promotion + value stack + premium price + guarantee so specific that the buyer cannot shop it against Udemy. This site currently lists a catalog ($10/mo, $29 bundle, $250/$500 cohort). The agent page names a candidate Grand Slam: the Architect Exam Sprint.

ICP

A description of the type of customer most likely to buy and get value from the product — the three personas on Target Audience are this site's ICPs.

IR35
UK off-payroll working tax legislation

UK tax rules governing contractor employment status, relevant here because the founder's day-job benchmark rate is an IR35 contract rate, used in unit-economics comparisons.

Appears on: Single-Founder Bandwidth Unit Economics Cost-Side Model
Listen:Speak
Audience minutes vs founder minutes (target ≥ 2:1 on Sunday)

The ratio that defines Rifat's Stage 1–2 posture. On Sunday live, listen more than you speak. On Skool, reply more than you post. On YouTube and LinkedIn, read the week's comments before answering them. If the week ends founder-heavy, the room was a lecture again. Tracked as H29.

Full definition: Listen More Than You Speak · Appears on: Golden Rules Interview Guide H29
LTV
Customer Lifetime Value

The total revenue expected from one customer over their relationship with the business. Paid ads only turn on in Stage 3 if CAC stays below LTV.

Appears on: Unit Economics When to Start Paid Ads Paid Advertisement H16 Competitive Analysis
MAOT
Minimum Awesome Outcome Threshold

A term coined on this site: the delight threshold beyond MVP where customers start referring others without being asked. See the full framework page for the diagram and evidence.

Full definition: MAOT · Appears on: H20 Value Proposition Moat
Moat
Defensive/competitive moat

What stops a competitor from copying the offer once it's proven to work — distinct from Competitive Analysis, which explains why a prospect should choose this program today. For this business: founder-market fit (the founder personally passed the target exam with a high score), a compounding SEO-indexed video catalog, live-cohort peer network effects (see MAOT), and an early position in Anthropic's Claude Partner Network practitioner-tier flywheel. Stated honestly as thin today: no proprietary technology, single-founder delivery, and single-platform (YouTube) dependency.

Full definition: Defensive Capabilities & Moat · Appears on: One-Pager Competitive Analysis VC Deck
MVP
Minimum Viable Product

The smallest version of the product that proves the core problem is solved — here, 3 animated videos plus a simple practice test page. The functional "does it work" bar, distinct from MAOT's delight bar.

Appears on: MVP Spec H10 AI Prep Demo Stage 1
NPS
Net Promoter Score

A customer-loyalty metric from −100 to +100, based on how likely customers are to recommend the product to others. This site tracks NPS >50 as an internal quality target for cohort graduates — not a promise made to students.

Appears on: H8 PMF Spec Quality Gates Value Proposition
PII
Personally Identifiable Information

Data that could identify a specific individual (name, contact details). Site convention is to strip PII before publishing candidate reports or discovery-call quotes.

Appears on: Candidate Exam Performance Evidence
PMF
Product-Market Fit

The point at which an offer satisfies real, strong market demand well enough that customers seek it out, get value from it, and recommend it on their own, without being sold.

Appears on: PMF Spec H8 Market Fit & Channels Business Overview

RFP
Request for Proposal

A formal document companies issue when soliciting bids for a project or service — relevant here because consulting firms cite named-certified headcount when responding to client RFPs.

Appears on: Marketing Tactics
Rule of 100
Alex Hormozi, $100M Leads: daily volume floor

100 minutes of outreach, or 100 minutes of content, or $100 of ads — every day. This site translates it to a second-job weekly 100: 30 warm reach-outs, 3 animated videos, and a named-offer CTA on every post. Ads stay at $0 until H9/H16 clear.

Full audit: Alex Hormozi Agent · Appears on: Single-Founder Bandwidth Task List
Signal
Decision-changing evidence

Information that would change what the founder does this week toward interviews, paid conversion, or the $10k Stage 2 gate (H9). Opposite of Noise. First-party field evidence (interview transcripts, YouTube Studio numbers, exam scores, live signups) scores highest. The ranked catalog lives on Signal versus Noise.

Full ranking: Signal versus Noise · Appears on: Evidence Map H9
Noise
Attention that does not change a decision

Content that burns founder time without changing this week's work: future-stage (Creation/Building) plans, unverified TAM, duplicate BMC restatements, and investor/prospect theater. A well-written Stage 4 page is still noise until H9 clears. Opposite of Signal.

Full ranking: Signal versus Noise · Appears on: Focus Single-Founder Bandwidth
SKU
Stock Keeping Unit

A distinct, individually priced product or offer. The $29 Exam Prep Bundle is referred to as a new SKU — additive, not a replacement for the membership or cohort tiers.

Appears on: Exam Prep Bundle H21 VC Deck
SLA
Service-Level Agreement

A commitment on response time, uptime, or delivery standard — referenced in the Architect exam's stakeholder-management topics and in scaling discussions.

Appears on: Exam Topic Blueprint Company Building
VC
Venture Capital

Equity capital from a fund that needs a small number of power-law outcomes to return the fund. Distinct from angel capital or founder-bootstrapped Customer Development. This business is explicitly not currently raising; the deck exists to make the model legible, and the constructive-feedback page explains why a partner would not write a check yet.

Appears on: VC Deck VC Constructive Feedback H22
TAM / SAM / SOM
Total / Serviceable Addressable / Serviceable Obtainable Market

A market-sizing funnel: TAM is everyone who could theoretically want this, SAM is the realistic slice actually seeking this kind of solution, and SOM is what's reachable via this business's actual channels. See H6 for this site's own working numbers and their verification status.

Appears on: Market Spec H6 BMC Customer Segments Business Plan Summary
Tripwire
A tiny paid handshake so someone raises a hand — not the real offer

Hormozi ($100M Leads): magnets are free; tripwires take a small amount of money from people already leaning in. On Delivery Pilot, Sit In at $1/month is the tripwire (listen-only Sunday). The $29 Exam Prep Bundle is a separate tripwire for people who only want mocks. Neither is the cohort. The offer is Share Screen at $250/year.

Full spec: Delivery Pilot Offer · Related: Alex Hormozi Agent Exam Prep Bundle Value Equation
True Regular
A Skool member who returns for four consecutive weeks without a founder poke

Counts toward the 10 true regulars milestone only if they are in the Skool room, they post / reply / attend Sunday without a personal reminder that week, and they are a distinct human (founder and lurkers excluded). Eight current signups are not automatically regulars. This is a community signal for H8, not a paid-enrollment count for H5 or H9.

Full definition: 10 True Regulars in Skool · Appears on: Founding Members Milestone Calendar H8

4-module curriculum in Skool Classroom covering Limited company / LLC incorporation, IR35 / tax efficiency, day-rate negotiation (£500–£1,000+/day), B2B client hunting, and collective RFP tender bidding as an alliance of certified Delivery Pilots. Tracked under H30.

Value Equation
Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)

Hormozi's four-term model from *$100M Offers*. Raise the top, crush the bottom. On this business the dream is strong (named cert) and likelihood is the leak (unfinished practice bank, no process guarantee). Interactive scorer on the agent page.

Full audit: Alex Hormozi Agent · Related: Grand Slam Offer Value Proposition H27
YoY
Year-over-Year

A rate of change measured against the same period one year earlier — used throughout for the Forward Deployed Engineer hiring-growth statistics (H6).
