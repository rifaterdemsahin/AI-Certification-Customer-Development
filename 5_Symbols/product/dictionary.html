📚 Definitions

ARR
Annual Recurring Revenue

Predictable revenue normalized to a yearly rate. This site uses $100,000 ARR as the Stage 4 hiring trigger and the point the founder transitions to full-time.

Appears on: Company Building H11 H15 Quality Gates Cost-Side Model
A/B Testing
A/B (split) testing

Comparing two versions of something (a video format, a headline, a price) against each other to see which performs better, using real measured results rather than opinion.

Appears on: H2 Exam Topic Blueprint Test Plan
API
Application Programming Interface

A programmatic way for software to talk to Claude directly, distinct from the consumer chat app. Referenced here mainly re: certification update releases and technical integration.

Appears on: Roadmap Spec PMF Spec Cohort Prep
B2B
Business-to-Business

Selling to companies (consulting firms, enterprises) rather than individual consumers. The channel behind H12's bulk-cohort-seat thesis.

Appears on: H12 Partner With Us Competitive Analysis BMC Channels
CAC
Customer Acquisition Cost

How much it costs, on average, to acquire one paying customer. This site's organic funnel targets $0 CAC through Stage 2; paid ads only turn on if CAC stays below LTV.

Appears on: Paid Advertisement H16 Unit Economics Funnel Spec
CTR
Click-Through Rate

The percentage of people who click a link after seeing it (e.g. video viewers who click through to the newsletter). Core to H7's funnel model.

Appears on: H7 Funnel Math Funnel Spec Content Analysis
DoD Directive 8140
U.S. Department of Defense cybersecurity workforce directive

A real U.S. government policy mandating staff certification in cybersecurity/IT roles — cited as precedent for why government-contracting consultancies already treat mandated certification as normal practice (H12).

Appears on: H12
FDE
Forward Deployed Engineer

An on-the-ground, client-embedded engineer who configures and operationalizes LLM/agent deployments. Named evidence for H6's SAM sizing (700–800%+ YoY job-posting growth) and the model behind H22's long-term talent-placement vision.

Appears on: H6 H22 Market Spec VC Deck
FedRAMP
Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program

A U.S. government standard for cloud security assessment and authorization. Listed as a compliance topic in the Architect exam blueprint.

Appears on: Exam Topic Blueprint
GDPR
General Data Protection Regulation

The EU's data-privacy law. Listed as a compliance topic in the Architect exam blueprint.

Appears on: Exam Topic Blueprint
HIPAA
Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act

The U.S. healthcare data-privacy law. Listed as a compliance topic in the Architect exam blueprint.

Appears on: Exam Topic Blueprint
ICP
Ideal Customer Profile

A description of the type of customer most likely to buy and get value from the product — the three personas on Target Audience are this site's ICPs.

Appears on: Motivation Revenue Streams Target Audience
IR35
UK off-payroll working tax legislation

UK tax rules governing contractor employment status, relevant here because the founder's day-job benchmark rate is an IR35 contract rate, used in unit-economics comparisons.

Appears on: Single-Founder Bandwidth Unit Economics Cost-Side Model
LTV
Customer Lifetime Value

The total revenue expected from one customer over their relationship with the business. Paid ads only turn on in Stage 3 if CAC stays below LTV.

Appears on: Unit Economics Paid Advertisement H16 Competitive Analysis
MAOT
Minimum Awesome Outcome Threshold

A term coined on this site: the delight threshold beyond MVP where customers start referring others without being asked. See the full framework page for the diagram and evidence.

Full definition: MAOT · Appears on: H20 Value Proposition Moat
Moat
Defensive/competitive moat

What stops a competitor from copying the offer once it's proven to work — distinct from Competitive Analysis, which explains why a prospect should choose this program today. For this business: founder-market fit (the founder personally passed the target exam with a high score), a compounding SEO-indexed video catalog, live-cohort peer network effects (see MAOT), and an early position in Anthropic's Claude Partner Network practitioner-tier flywheel. Stated honestly as thin today: no proprietary technology, single-founder delivery, and single-platform (YouTube) dependency.

Full definition: Defensive Capabilities & Moat · Appears on: One-Pager Competitive Analysis VC Deck
MVP
Minimum Viable Product

The smallest version of the product that proves the core problem is solved — here, 3 animated videos plus a simple practice test page. The functional "does it work" bar, distinct from MAOT's delight bar.

Appears on: MVP Spec H10 AI Prep Demo Stage 1
NPS
Net Promoter Score

A customer-loyalty metric from −100 to +100, based on how likely customers are to recommend the product to others. This site tracks NPS >50 as an internal quality target for cohort graduates — not a promise made to students.

Appears on: H8 PMF Spec Quality Gates Value Proposition
PII
Personally Identifiable Information

Data that could identify a specific individual (name, contact details). Site convention is to strip PII before publishing candidate reports or discovery-call quotes.

Appears on: Candidate Exam Performance Evidence
PMF
Product-Market Fit

The point at which an offer satisfies real, strong market demand well enough that customers seek it out, get value from it, and recommend it on their own, without being sold.

Appears on: PMF Spec H8 Market Fit & Channels Business Overview
RFP
Request for Proposal

A formal document companies issue when soliciting bids for a project or service — relevant here because consulting firms cite named-certified headcount when responding to client RFPs.

Appears on: Marketing Tactics
SKU
Stock Keeping Unit

A distinct, individually priced product or offer. The $29 Exam Prep Bundle is referred to as a new SKU — additive, not a replacement for the membership or cohort tiers.

Appears on: Exam Prep Bundle H21 VC Deck
SLA
Service-Level Agreement

A commitment on response time, uptime, or delivery standard — referenced in the Architect exam's stakeholder-management topics and in scaling discussions.

Appears on: Exam Topic Blueprint Company Building
TAM / SAM / SOM
Total / Serviceable Addressable / Serviceable Obtainable Market

A market-sizing funnel: TAM is everyone who could theoretically want this, SAM is the realistic slice actually seeking this kind of solution, and SOM is what's reachable via this business's actual channels. See H6 for this site's own working numbers and their verification status.

Appears on: Market Spec H6 BMC Customer Segments Business Plan Summary
YoY
Year-over-Year

A rate of change measured against the same period one year earlier — used throughout for the Forward Deployed Engineer hiring-growth statistics (H6).

Appears on: H6 VC Deck Why Certification Matters
