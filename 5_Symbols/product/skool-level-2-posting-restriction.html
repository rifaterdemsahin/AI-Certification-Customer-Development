🔒 Skool Level 2 Posting Restriction & Engagement Gate

In Steve Blank Customer Development, community health is governed by signal-to-noise ratio and genuine peer participation. To eliminate drive-by spam, protect founder bandwidth, and encourage meaningful comment interactions, the Delivery Pilot Skool community activates the Level 2 Posting Restriction Plugin. Members must earn 5 points (via peer likes on helpful comments) before unlocking standalone posting privileges.

🏆 CHECK LEVEL ON LEADERBOARDS
1. Read & Consume
Level 1 members immediately get full access to the Classroom modules, Sunday session recordings, and all existing discussion threads.
2. Value-Add Comments
Members answer peer questions, share notes from Sunday live practice, and introduce their cloud/AI background to earn genuine upvotes.
3. Broadcast Rights
Upon collecting 5 peer likes, the member hits Level 2 and permanently unlocks the ability to publish top-level posts, case studies, and threads.

🛠️ Admin Configuration Guide (How to Set Up in Skool)

Follow these direct operational steps to lock posting permissions behind Level 2 in the Delivery Pilot community:

1

Open Group Settings

In your Skool community view, click the SETTINGS gear icon located in the right-hand sidebar or dropdown group administration menu.

2

Navigate to Plugins

In the left-hand navigation column under Group Settings, click on Plugins to open Skool's modular platform extensions.

3

Locate Posting Permission Plugin

Find the Unlock posting at Level 2 or 3 plugin card in the list of available community add-ons.

4

Toggle ON & Select Level 2

Toggle the plugin switch to ON. Select Level 2 (5 points required). Click Save Changes to activate immediately.

💡
Why Level 2 (5 pts) instead of Level 3 (20 pts)? Level 2 requires just 5 comment likes — an achievable hurdle that rewards genuine participation within 1–2 weeks without creating excessive friction. Level 3 (20 pts) is reserved for identifying potential community moderators and TAs (see H11).

📊 Customer Development Impact: Why This Matters

🌱 1. Solves the Comment Cold-Start Problem

In early online groups, members hesitate to comment, resulting in "dead" announcement threads. By gating posting permissions behind 5 comment likes, members are systematically guided to read founder evergreen hubs (such as "Claude Architect Roadmap" and "Sunday Practice Replays") and leave thoughtful responses.

🛡️ 2. Zero-Tolerance for Drive-By Spam

Aligns with Broken Window Theory. New accounts cannot drop affiliate links, unsolicited WhatsApp links, or generic crypto/AI solicitations. Because spam comments are never upvoted by the community, spammers are mechanically locked out of posting.

🔟 3. Validates 10 True Regulars (H8)

Under Hypothesis H8, 10 True Regulars are defined as members who actively contribute and help peers without founder pokes. Reaching Level 2 is an objective, verifiable event showing peer validation and community retention before Stage 2 paid cohort launch.

⏱️ 4. Conserves Single-Founder Bandwidth

As founder Rifat Erdem Sahin operates in weekly cycles alongside contract work, automated anti-spam gating prevents the board from becoming a moderation burden. The community self-polices via upvotes (see Garden Playbook).
