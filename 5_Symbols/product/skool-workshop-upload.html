🎥 Upload the 2-Hour Workshop to Skool?

Founder question (2026-08-13): Sunday is already a fixed 2-hour live slot (9–11pm). Should those recordings be uploaded into Skool Classroom as the membership asset? This page is the operational sibling of Short vs Long Form (which places the workshop) and Founder Posting Sanity Check (which places the board). See H5, H8, H2, and H10.

  • Page ID: skool-workshop-upload.html
  • Verdict: Yes — as the replay archive, not the course
  • Report: v1.0 full write-up

📌 The Rule in One Line

Upload the 2-hour Sunday recording into Skool Classroom the same night. It is the replay of the live room. It is not the course, and it is not a public YouTube video.

🧭 Verdict at a Glance

✅ Do this
After Sunday 13:30–14:30 UK, drop the recording into the matching Classroom (one of the 7 already configured). Add 5–8 timestamps in the lesson description. Post one board line: “Replay is in Classroom N.” This is what H5 Premise 3 already sold as the $10/mo membership.
⚠️ Boundary
Do not edit the 2 hours into a polished course, and do not count replay watch-through toward the H10 >40% catalog floor. Time-poor candidates will skip; that is expected. The course remains the 8–25 min animated lesson plus files and mocks.
🚫 Never
Never list the full live workshop on public YouTube. Recaps yes (60–180s); the room no. Live YouTube would give away the paid seat and expose student screens. Already decided on Short vs Long Form.

✅ Why Uploading Makes Sense

The model already assumed this. The question is not “should workshops live on Skool?” — that is settled. The question is whether the uncut 2-hour file is a legitimate Classroom asset. It is, for five reasons that already exist in the repo:

  • 💳$10/mo already buys the replay. H5 Premise 3: the membership tier is recorded cohort replays (async). The $250 / $500 tiers buy the live screen-share seat on top. If the recording never lands in Classroom, the cheap tier has nothing to redeem.
  • 🏫Placement is already decided. Short vs Long Form puts the live workshop’s primary home on Skool (calendar + live room + Classroom replay). Skool LMS Stage 2 row: “Host recorded replays in Classroom.”
  • 🌍Missed Sunday is the default, not the exception. Brian’s discovery feedback (H5) is time constraint, not lack of interest. A second-job founder cannot add a second live slot. The recording is how the one 2-hour block serves people who could not attend.
  • ⏱️Upload is cheap; recutting is not. Same-night upload + timestamps is ~20 minutes. Turning a 2-hour screen-share into a polished course fights single-founder bandwidth and steals evening hours from the animated 8–25 min lessons that actually feed YouTube search (H7).
  • 🔒Skool keeps the room private. The paid offer is live problem-solving plus community, not a promised exam outcome (H8 Premise 3). Public YouTube would leak student terminals and skip the $0-ad test of whether people will pay to be in the room.

⚠️ Why a Raw Dump Is Incomplete

Uploading is correct. Treating the 2-hour file as the product is not. These are the failure modes if Classroom becomes a vault of uncut blobs:

H2 / H10 will look like they failed
The only measured retention number on this site is a 68.5% animated short (H2). Dry long materials are the thing H2 says causes fatigue. A 2-hour live recording will not clear H10’s >40% catalog floor, and it should not be asked to. Replays are out of the H10 catalog by design.
Candidates will not watch 2 hours async
Exam windows are ~2–3 weeks (H21 Premise 4). Brian already cannot finish installs. They want the mock, the install list, and the 8-minute RAG clip — not two hours of “can you hear me” and setup. Timestamps are the mitigation, not a recut.
Seven classrooms of blobs ≠ a curriculum
H8 Premise 1 needs scope coverage that students can finish. The 7 Skool classrooms are the path; the 8–25 min animated lesson + files + the H27 question bank are what make a module completable. A replay is the appendix, not the lesson.
Skool is a mediocre 2-hour player
Skool vs. YouTube already says native Skool playback is standard, not industry-leading. That is acceptable for a private archive. It is a reason not to pretend Classroom replaces YouTube as the teaching player for long public lessons.

🌙 Same-Night Ritual (~20 minutes)

Fits after Sunday 11pm without stealing a weekday evening from animation production. If this block grows past 30 minutes, you are editing — stop.

1. Stop recording
Save the Sunday 13:30–14:30 UK file as-is
2. Upload native
Matching Classroom of the 7
3. Timestamp
5–8 bullets: setup, build, debug, recap
4. Highlight + post

Mid-week still belongs to Cohort Prep (install list + preview, dual-posted WhatsApp and Skool). Monday LinkedIn is the recap, not a second upload of the 2 hours. Production order for the taught lesson stays the one on Short vs Long Form: animate the long → cut the Short → file in Classroom → workshop it Sunday.

💳 What Each Tier Actually Buys

$10 / month

Forum + Classroom files + this replay archive. Async. No live screen-share seat. If the 2-hour file is not in Classroom, this tier is a newsletter with extra steps.

$29 bundle (H21)

Memory cards + mock + prep exam. Not the 2-hour workshop. Do not stuff the replay into the bundle; that would blur the SKU this page is trying to keep sharp.

$250 / $500 live

Everything in $10/mo plus the live Sunday seat and the right to share a screen. The replay does not cannibalize this if the live value is real-time debug + accountability — the thing H8 Premise 2 claims.

🔒 Privacy & Hosting

Default: native Skool upload
Sunday is a screen-share room. Terminals, inboxes, and API keys show up. Native Classroom keeps that inside the paid wall. Playback is “standard” (Skool vs. YouTube) and that is fine for an archive.
Fallback: unlisted host, embed in Skool
Use only when the native file fails and the session has no student screens. Unlisted YouTube is not private. Public listing is never the fallback. Collect recording yes/no on join via Cohort Session Permissions (membership Question 3) and re-read it in the first 60 seconds of Sunday. Recording stays in the paid Classroom.
