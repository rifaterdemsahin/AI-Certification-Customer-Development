⚖️ Rationale: Why Skool at Stage 2?

Under Steve Blank's Customer Development methodology, one of the most common causes of startup failure is premise-level premature scaling—spending time, money, and focus building custom technology or complex operations before confirming that customers actually exist.

We deliberately divide our platform requirements between the stages. Discovery and reach stay on YouTube / LinkedIn; Skool is Stage 2 delivery only. Full audience handoff order: YouTube → Skool Handoff.

❌ Stage 1 (Customer Discovery): Inactive
In Stage 1, the goal is purely validating Problem-Solution Fit via qualitative interviews and organic video feedback. Free, zero-overhead tools (YouTube playlists, Substack, manual WhatsApp chats, Google Slides) are used. Setting up a paid community LMS during Discovery is premature and distracts from founder-led interviews.
✅ Stage 2 (Customer Validation): Active Onboarding
In Stage 2, the goal is proving sales model repeatability by launching paid tiers ($10/mo membership and $250–$500 cohorts) to hit the $10,000 exit gate. Here, a central, off-the-shelf LMS becomes necessary to host recorded replays, share mock exam materials, coordinate calendars, and facilitate peer discussions.

🛠️ Platform Capabilities: Why Skool?

Skool is chosen over custom-coded solutions or fragmented alternatives (like Discord + Teachable + Stripe) for four primary reasons:

📁 Classroom & Forum

Integrates the study forum directly next to the course recordings, slides, and cheat sheets. Students don't need separate logins to ask questions about a specific lesson.

🎮 Gamification

Members earn points by posting helpful replies and getting likes. Higher levels unlock exclusive materials (like the premium mock exam or advanced study guides), boosting engagement.

🗓️ Event Calendar

A single source of truth for the weekly Sunday 9 PM live sessions, complete with local-timezone conversion and automatic email reminders.

💳 Native Billing

Handles subscription signups ($10/mo) and stripe checkouts natively. Minimizes administrative bandwidth for single-founder operations.

⚠️ Sanity Check: Open Risks & Decisions

Missing Questions / Uncertainties:

  • Do candidates prefer Discord or Slack over Skool due to pre-existing usage? (We need to ask this during discovery interviews).
  • How will self-paced students who purchase the $29 Exam Prep Bundle (H21) interact on the platform? Do they get restricted Skool classroom access?
  • Will the $99/mo subscription fee strain capital reserves if paid cohorts launch slower than expected?
  • Will founder-only board posts stay a monologue after warm free-cohort members are invited in? (see posting sanity check)

Mitigation Strategies:

  • Staged Billing Setup: Do not activate the Skool $99/mo paid account until the waitlist size (for paid cohort/membership) guarantees covering the cost.
  • Explicit Tiering on Skool: Keep the main forum open to all members, but gate premium Classroom categories (cohort lessons) using Skool's native access settings.
  • Student Feedback Loops: Monitor forum engagement indices (posts/comments ratio) to gauge PMF early.
  • Warm invite + dual-post prep: Invite existing WhatsApp/Sunday attendees into Skool; dual-post the mid-week install list for 2–4 weeks rather than cutting WhatsApp cold.
