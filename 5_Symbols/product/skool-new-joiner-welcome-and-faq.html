❓ 3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ Post)

This FAQ is pinned in the Delivery Pilot Skool Classroom and General Discussion channel to proactively answer common beginner inquiries:

❓ Do I need a technical or computer science background to join?

No, absolutely not! Our members come from diverse backgrounds—business operations, design, recruiting, project management, and traditional IT. The modern generative AI paradigm is conversational. If you can clearly articulate a problem in plain language, AI tools can help you build the technical solution.

❓ Do I have to pay or spend money at the beginning?

No. Joining the Delivery Pilot Skool community, accessing the foundational resources, reading the discussion threads, and attending public preview sessions is 100% free ($0). We want you to see the value, meet the community, and experience the learning format before deciding if you ever want premium cohort seats.

❓ Do I need to know how to write code?

No. We do not expect you to manually write code syntax from scratch. We focus on prompt engineering, architectural understanding, AI agent orchestration (like Claude, Cursor, Antigravity), and visual workflows. AI writes the code; your job is to guide the architecture and verify the output.

❓ How does this community improve my self-learning?

Learning AI alone can be overwhelming with hundreds of new models and tools released every week. In Delivery Pilot, we curate the signal from the noise. You learn by seeing how fellow members build real projects (like Second Brains, predictive models, and serverless workflows) and receive immediate peer feedback during Sunday live sessions.

❓ Is taking the certification exam mandatory?

No. Taking vendor certification exams (like Anthropic Claude Certified Architect) is completely optional. Many members join solely to learn how to apply AI in their current day jobs. However, if you do choose to get certified, our curriculum and live practice scenarios provide the exact blueprint needed to pass with flying colors.

❓ How do I get involved right now?

Head to the community board, complete your 5-step onboarding checklist, introduce yourself, and mark your calendar for this Sunday's live cohort at 21:00 PM UK / London time!
