📝 Skool Membership Questions: Automated Customer Discovery

In Steve Blank Customer Development, the best customer discovery happens continuously at the moment of peak intent. When a prospective engineer or contractor joins the Delivery Pilot Skool community, they answer a structured curriculum alignment survey covering 4 core AI knowledge pillars, plus an open-ended free-text prompt: “What do you hope to get out of the group?” This transforms every new member request into an actionable customer discovery transcript.

🧠 The 4 AI Knowledge Pillars (Curriculum Alignment Diagnostic)

During onboarding, candidates select which AI conceptual domains align with their current technical focus. This categorizes the cohort's baseline technical literacy across architecture, training, operations, and governance:

Pillar 1 · Data & Patterns

📊 How AI Learns (Data & Patterns)

Learning from clear examples, discovering hidden trends on its own, and improving through trial and error. Pinpoints members focused on supervised datasets, clustering, and reinforcement loops.

Pillar 2 · Networks & Training

🕸️ How AI Builds Knowledge (Networks & Training)

Connecting layered networks to model complex ideas and continuously adjusting to minimize mistakes. Pinpoints engineers interested in transformer architectures, backpropagation, and loss optimization.

Pillar 3 · Real-World Use

⚡ How AI Adapts (Real-World Use)

Applying what it has learned to brand-new situations and evolving smoothly over time. Pinpoints Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) deploying MCP tools, RAG vector search (Qdrant), and production agents.

Pillar 4 · Ethics & Teamwork

🤝 How Humans Guide AI (Ethics & Teamwork)

Working alongside human direction to ensure results remain fair, clear, and reliable. Pinpoints lead architects and governance specialists focusing on human-in-the-loop validation, safety guardrails, and compliance.

🔍 The Open-Ended Discovery Text Box (Voice of Customer)

The second half of the intake form is an unconstrained, free-text input:

Intake Prompt (Skool Membership Form):
“What do you hope to get out of the group?”

[ Open text box — new joiners describe their career situation, exam preparation timeline, technical bottlenecks, and community aspirations in their own words. ]

What We Learn from Unsolicited Text Box Answers:

1. Persona & Career Objective
Reveals whether the applicant is an aspiring £600–£1,000/day contractor seeking FDE transformation (H30), a senior cloud architect preparing for Claude certification (H8), or an enterprise employee needing MCP tool builds.
2. Exact Friction & Urgency (H24)
Uncovers emotional pain drivers (e.g. "feeling left behind in AI," "failed sample questions," "struggling with local Obsidian/MCP setups") directly fueling copywriting and Sunday live practice session agendas.

🛠️ Admin Configuration Guide (How to Set Up in Skool)

To configure these membership intake questions inside the Delivery Pilot community:

1

Open Group Settings

In your Skool community view, click the SETTINGS gear icon located in the right-hand sidebar menu.

2

Select Membership Questions

In the left navigation menu, click on Membership Questions (or Join Questions).

3

Add 4 AI Pillar Choices

Add Question 1 as a multi-select or single-select list covering the 4 AI learning pillars (Data & Patterns, Networks & Training, Real-World Use, Human Guidance).

4

Add Free-Text Question & Save

Add Question 2 as an open text box: “What do you hope to get out of the group?”

5

Add recording permission (required)

Add Question 3 as a required single-select: Sunday recording for Skool Classroom (A/B), listen-only (C), or no record (D). Paste-ready copy lives on Cohort Session Permissions. Click Save Changes.

🔄 The Customer Development Operating Loop

1. Daily Intake Review
During the daily 5-minute Skool pass (Task List), the founder reviews responses for new joiners before approving membership.
2. Tailored Welcome DM
Founder sends a personalized direct message referencing their stated goal (e.g. "Saw you're prepping for Claude Architect — make sure to check Sunday's live practice call").
3. Transcript Archiving
High-signal answers are logged into Archived Transcripts to support ongoing hypothesis validation in HYPOTHESIS.md.
