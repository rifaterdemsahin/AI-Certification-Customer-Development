⚠️ The 3 Core Structural Flaws

1. Premature Core Course Giveaway at Level 1: By unlocking the flagship "Certified Architect Professional" course at Level 1 immediately upon joining, 88% of members get maximum consumption value without ever leaving a comment or attending a Sunday live lab.
2. The Level 3–9 "Engagement Desert": Because Levels 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, and 9 have no defined course, toolkit, or permission unlocks, members stall out at Level 2. There is no milestone incentive between 5 points and eternity.
3. Missing Contractor Transformation Linkage (H30): Engineers join to pass exams and become £500–£1,000/day contractors. Gamification must reward second-brain setups, mock exam performance, day-rate negotiations, and recruiter promotions.

🔍 Tier-by-Tier Strategic Rationale

L1–2

Stage 1: Onboarding & Anti-Spam Gate (0–5 Points)

Foundational Activation

The Friction Cure: Rather than dumping 40 hours of advanced curriculum on Day 1, Level 1 gives quick wins (Claude CLI, Ollama, Sunday Zoom links). Members earn 5 points by introducing themselves or answering peer questions on founder hubs, which unlocks both posting privileges and the initial Architecture modules.

L3–4

Stage 2: Exam Simulation & MCP Repos (20–65 Points)

Active Practitioner

The Engagement Engine: Level 3 unlocks the 60-question Mock Exam and flashcards, solving the "How do I test my readiness?" pain point. Level 4 unlocks production-grade Model Context Protocol (MCP) server blueprints and multi-agent HITL code, recognizing members who regularly attend Sunday sessions.

L5–7

Stage 3: £500–£1,000/day Commercial Mastery & Moderator Role (155–2,015 Points)

Commercial Contractor

Economic Freedom Alignment: Connects technical knowledge to high-earning contractor capability. Unlocks IR35 frameworks, CV teardowns, private VIP recordings, and tender co-bidding templates. Top members at Level 7 are recruited as paid TAs and community moderators (Garden Playbook), preserving founder bandwidth.

L8–9

Stage 4: 🧑‍✈️ Delivery Pilot Graduation & Rainmaker Alliance (8,015–30,015 Points)

Elite Climax

Meritocratic Scarcity: Delivery Pilot status cannot be bought—it must be earned through proctored Credly exam badges, production code portfolios, and community leadership. Level 8 graduates get promoted to recruiters on Monday VIP sessions. Level 9 Rainmakers co-deliver enterprise tenders with revenue share.

💎 Delivery Pilot Privileges (Level 8 & 9):

🛠️ Standard Operating Procedure: Setting Up & Managing Levels in Skool

⚙️ View Dedicated SOP Document →

Purpose: Provide step-by-step instructions for group administrators to configure custom level names, gate courses by level, and set up plugin level permissions on Skool. Follow these 3 operational procedures:

Procedure 1: Customize Level Names
1. Go to homepage → Click Leaderboards.
2. Click gear icon (⚙️) in top-left to open Leaderboards / Naming modal.
3. Type custom tier titles (Level 1–9).
4. Click SAVE at bottom-right.
Procedure 2: Gate Course Access
1. Click Classroom tab.
2. Hover over target course → Select Edit Course (or + Add Course).
3. Locate Access dropdown.
4. Select Level unlock & choose minimum level requirement.
5. Click Save.
Procedure 3: Restrict Posting & Chat
1. Click Settings (⚙️) → Select Plugins.
2. Configure level-based toggles:
• Unlock chat at a level: restrict DM/Chat access.
• Unlock posting at Level 2/3: prevent new account spam.
3. Close modal to apply automatically.
🔁
Quarterly Task to Review & Update (Every 90 Days): Group administrators must review the member distribution across Levels 1–9 quarterly. If members stall at Level 1 (e.g. 88% bottleneck), calibrate comment prompts, audit new Classroom module level locks, and ensure chat/posting plugins remain active to uphold community signal (tracked in Task List & Levels SOP).
