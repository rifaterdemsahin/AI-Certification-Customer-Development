🏆 Skool Gamification Levels: Level 8 & 9 Delivery Pilot Graduation

Only let a select few who complete the roadmap become a Delivery Pilot. We utilize Skool's native gamification levels (1–9) to establish meritocratic scarcity and massive perceived value. Members don't buy the Delivery Pilot title — they earn it at Level 8 by mastering the roadmap, passing proctored exams, and deploying real code. At Level 9, alumni step up to onboard and bring new engineers in, driving perpetual viral growth (see H5, H8, H20, and H30).

🧑‍✈️ Level 8 Delivery Pilot 🌟 Level 9 Alumni Mentor 🎮 Meritocratic Gamification 💎 Earned Scarcity

🪜 Skool Level Hierarchy & Roadmap Milestones

L1–3

Cadet Stage: Onboarding, Community Ethics & Setup

Foundational

Complete orientation, install Claude CLI / Ollama / Python tools, answer membership discovery surveys, and unlock Level 2 community post creation.

L4–7

Flight Engineer: Hands-On Repos & Exam Simulations

Practitioner

Build 3+ real GitHub repos (MCP servers, RAG pipelines, HITL agent workflows), attend Sunday live labs, and score >80% on mock certification diagnostic exams.

L8

🧑‍✈️ Level 8 Graduation: Official Delivery Pilot

Elite Milestone

Requirements: Official proctored Pearson VUE / Credly certification badge + completed production architecture portfolio + approved Delivery Pilot review.

💎 Delivery Pilot Privileges:
  • Direct admission to Monday VIP Claude Recruiter Cowork sessions.
  • Profile promoted across the Delivery Pilot agency network to enterprise clients.
  • Eligible to co-bid on £500–£1,000+/day enterprise RFP tenders as a contractor collective.
  • Special "Delivery Pilot" badge displayed across Skool and LinkedIn.
L9

🌟 Level 9: Delivery Pilot Alumni & Community Rainmaker

Alumni Leader

Requirements: Successfully landed an enterprise AI contract/promotion + actively mentoring new cadets + bringing new qualified engineers into the ecosystem.

🚀 Level 9 Rainmaker Responsibilities & Rewards:
  • Hosts breakout lab sessions and peer code reviews on Sunday cohorts.
  • Brings qualified contractor colleagues into the Skool community (viral MAOT referral flywheel).
  • Receives profit-share on co-delivered enterprise consulting contracts.
  • Lifetime VIP access to all future masterclasses and enterprise toolkits.
