⚡ Skool Validation Speed: The Exact Fix List

Stage 2 is still validation before a bigger commit (see H9: two consecutive launches combining to $10,000). That is the right gate. 🎙️ The Pitch, Translated

“Understood, you're still validating before committing to anything bigger. That makes sense. But here's the thing, the faster your Skool community is set up properly the faster you hit that x2 $10k validation. A messy or incomplete community slows down the whole process. Even at this early stage a clean setup builds trust with potential members and converts them faster. It doesn't have to be a big investment right now, even a basic optimization could make a real difference to your validation speed. Want me to take a quick look and tell you exactly what I'd fix?”

Agree with the mechanic, refuse the upsell. The “x2 $10k” line is already our Validation Repeat Gate. The look is already specified across About, onboarding, living hubs, and the garden pass. This page is the look — eight founder actions, no extra product, no paid community coach.

✅ Exactly What To Fix (This Week)

Each item is already documented. Status is operational, not a new product. Completing the row is the optimization.

1. About tab: replace “get certified”

Strangers still see the listing line that over-promises the exam. Paste the copy on skool-about.html into /about. Add the 64s orientation video. Cites H5 Premise 9.

2. Share the group URL, never a /live/ link

Tuncer bounced on a live-room URL (password / second email / “I'll deal with it later”). Send skool.com/delivery-pilot-8938 first. Playbook: How to Join Sunday.

3. Three-step onboarding widget

Watch 60s orientation → first comment → mobile app. Blank boards kill Day-1 trust. Spec: Onboarding Checklist · Script.

4. Living hubs, not announcement spam

Update Courses / Classroom / Calendar in place. One-off threads flood notifications and look unfinished. Static Posts · Garden Playbook.

5. Welcome DM + membership questions

New joiners get a human first message and the four-pillar intake. That is the trust conversion, not a paid redesign. Welcome & FAQ · Questions.

6. Level 2 posting gate

Comments first, standalone posts after five points. Stops empty spam looking like a dead board. Level 2 Restriction.

7. Daily 5-minute garden pass

08:30 UK: prune spam, water one new joiner, one 1-on-1 check-in. Incomplete communities are usually neglected ones. Checklist on Garden Playbook and todo.html.

8. Calendar + founder-voice closer

Sunday 21:00 UK on the calendar with timezone auto-convert. Spoken Skool asks stay founder-recorded. Event emails · Voice SOP.

🚫 What Not To Buy

This is not a Skool agency brief
Paid community “optimization” does not move H9. Paid enrollments do. Do not hire a setup consultant, buy extra Skool themes, or open extra live nights. Keep Freemium Peek / Sit In / Share Screen. Cap VIP as Sunday screen-share, not on-demand 1:1. Bandwidth: single-founder ledger · one Sunday class.

🎯 How This Speeds the Repeat Gate

  • 1️⃣Trust before checkout. A stranger who can read a Public board, a clear About, and a working Sunday path is likelier to Sit In or Share Screen (H5).
  • 2️⃣Less join friction. Tuncer-style /live/ failures are false negatives on the funnel. Fix the invite, then measure conversion.
  • 3️⃣Fair test of PMF. If the room is incomplete, empty Sunday attendance cannot be read as product failure (H8).
  • 4️⃣Still two paid launches. Clean setup is necessary, not sufficient. The gate stays two consecutive launches summing to $10,000 on validation-repeat-gate.html.
