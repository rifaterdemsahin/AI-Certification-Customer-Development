⚙️ Standard Operating Procedure: Setting Up & Managing Levels in Skool

🗓️ Quarterly Maintenance Cadence

Purpose: Provide step-by-step instructions for group administrators to configure custom level names, gate courses by level, and set up plugin level permissions on Skool. This standard operating procedure ensures that gamification drives active peer contribution and aligns with the Level 1–9 Value Ladder (see H5, H8, and H30).

📋 Administrator SOP ⚙️ Gamification Settings 🔒 Course Access Gating 🛡️ Level 2/3 Anti-Spam Plugins
1

Procedure 1: Customize Level Names & Gamification

Configure custom tier names (e.g. Cadet, Active Contributor, Flight Engineer, Delivery Pilot) so members have clear identity progression on the community leaderboards:

  1. Go to your group homepage and click the Leaderboards tab on the top main navigation bar.
  2. Click the gear icon (⚙️) located in the top-left area of the Leaderboards page to open the Leaderboards / Naming modal.
  3. Type your custom tier titles into each text field (Level 1 through Level 9) following the Architecture Specification.
  4. Click SAVE at the bottom-right of the modal window to store the changes.
2

Procedure 2: Gate Course Access by Level

Ensure high-value courses, mock exams, and contractor toolkits are unlocked progressively rather than given away on Day 1:

  1. Click the Classroom tab on the top navigation bar.
  2. Hover over or click the target course, then select Edit Course (or click + Add Course to build a new one).
  3. In the course settings panel, locate Access.
  4. Change the dropdown menu setting to Level unlock.
  5. Select the minimum level requirement (e.g., Level 2 for Foundations, Level 3 for Mock Exam #1, Level 4 for Advanced MCP, Level 5 for IT Contractor Masterclass) needed for members to view the course content.
  6. Click Save.
3

Procedure 3: Restrict Posting & Chat Capabilities by Level

Protect founder bandwidth and eliminate spam by gating standalone post creation and private DMs behind peer upvote hurdles:

  1. Click the Settings gear icon in the main navigation.
  2. Select Plugins from the left-side menu list.
  3. Configure the level-based plugin toggles as needed:
    • Unlock chat at a level: Turn on to restrict group Direct Messages/Chat access until members reach a specified level tier (e.g. Level 2 or 3 to prevent unsolicited DM spam).
    • Unlock posting at Level 2 or 3: Turn on to prevent brand-new accounts from posting immediately, requiring them to earn Level 2 (5 points / comment likes) status first (see Posting Restriction Spec).
  4. Close the modal window to apply the configurations automatically.

🔁 Quarterly Review & Maintenance Task (Every 90 Days)

Quarterly Gamification & Permission Audit Schedule

End of Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4

Group administrators must execute this quarterly audit to ensure member engagement health and prevent distribution bottlenecks:

1. Distribution Scan
Check the Leaderboards distribution. If >75% of members are stuck at Level 1, inspect if comment onboarding prompts are clear and if course unlocks create enough incentive.
2. Curriculum Alignment
Audit all new Classroom modules uploaded in the last quarter and verify their Level Unlock settings match the 1–9 ladder.
3. Plugin & Spam Review
Verify Level 2 posting lock and chat restrictions are active to protect founder bandwidth and uphold the Golden Rules.
