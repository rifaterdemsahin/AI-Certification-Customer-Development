✉️ Skool Manual Member Invites & Tier Assignment

In Steve Blank Customer Development, founders must rapidly onboard founding members, customer discovery interviewees, and strategic alliance partners without forcing them through public checkout friction. This playbook outlines the mechanism discovered from experienced community creators to invite members manually via email and switch their membership tiers to Premium or VIP at $0 cost.

When transitioning from free customer discovery into structured Skool delivery (H5), a primary operational hurdle is granting VIP access to high-value partners, founding beta testers, or enterprise contractors without creating artificial paywall barriers. The following workflow was confirmed through creator discussions in the Skool community:

"Can I assign them to any tier (e.g. Premium Classroom or VIP Screen-Share) without them having to pay or enter credit card information?"

🛠️ 4-Step Operational Playbook

1

Open Group Settings → Invite

Navigate to the Delivery Pilot Skool group dashboard, click the gear icon (Settings) in the top navigation, and select the Invite tab from the admin menu.

📍 Path: skool.com/delivery-pilot-8938/settings/invite
2

Send Direct Email Invitation

Enter the member's email address into the input field and click Send Invite. Skool dispatches a direct sign-up link that allows the recipient to join immediately without hitting the public checkout page.

⚡ Bypasses: Public paywalls, Stripe credit card input, and approval waitlists.
3

Member Accepts on Standard ($0)

When the invitee clicks their email link, their account is provisioned into the community on the baseline Standard (Peek $0) tier, granting immediate read/community access.

🎯 Default State: Active Member, Level 1, Standard Tier.
4

Admin Switches Member Tier

Go to Settings → Members, locate the user, click their options menu (or open their profile card), and select their assigned tier: Premium (Sit In) or VIP (Share Screen).

✅ Zero Billing: Member receives full tier entitlements without any billing charges.

🛡️ Founder Bandwidth & VIP Capacity Guardrails

🚫 Do Not Over-Comp VIP Screen-Shares

Per our business model constraints, VIP tier includes live screen-sharing during Sunday workshops. Because founder time is constrained by a primary contract role, complimentary VIP seats must be capped at no more than 5 active comped members at any one time to protect live workshop quality.

📋 Log Comped Invitations in Milestone Calendar

Whenever manually upgrading a user to Premium or VIP, log their entry in the Milestone Calendar and 10 True Regulars Tracker. Remember: manual joins do not count as paid revenue validation toward the $10,000 exit gate.
