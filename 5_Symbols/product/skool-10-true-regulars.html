📌 Verdict

Ten people who return on their own. Not ten signups. Not ten invites.

The 8 Skool joins already logged under H5 prove the door opens. Founding-member invites prove the founder can name seats. Neither proves the room has a pulse. A true regular is someone who posts or attends Sunday without a founder poke, across four consecutive weeks.

🧠 Why This Number

Ten is not a vanity target and it is not a Stage 2→3 exit gate. It is the smallest community that can talk to itself. The rationale is four stacked reasons, all current-stage.

1. It is Skool's own next step

Skool's community course is explicit: setup + first 3 members, then engagement + “10 true regulars.” This business already adopted that checklist on Skool Steps & Founding Members. Step 5 deserves its own milestone so it does not stay a table cell.

2. Three is a group chat. Ten is a room.

With three people the founder is still the conversation. With ten, two members can answer a third without waiting for Sunday. That peer loop is the live mechanism H8 Premise 2 claims (structured live instruction and peer accountability). WhatsApp already showed a hint of this; Skool has not.

3. Signups are not regulars

Eight people joined Skool when the link went live. That upgraded H5 to In Testing. A join can be curiosity. A true regular spends attention four weeks in a row. Counting signups as regulars would fake the H8 community signal the same way a one-time launch would fake the $10k gate.

4. It fits a second-job founder

Twenty or forty regulars is a community-manager job. Ten is a number the founder can still see by name on a Sunday night. See Single-Founder Bandwidth. The operating playbook that turns three seats into ten regulars is Golden Rules of Community Building. Scale comes after the room works, not instead of it.

✅ What Counts as a True Regular

Operational definition, so the milestone can flip from ⚪ estimated to ✅ done with a date. A person counts if all three are true in the same four-week window.

1. They are in the room
Joined the live Skool community (free or paid seat). Founder and anonymous lurkers do not count toward the ten.
2. They return without a poke
In four consecutive weeks they either post on the board, reply to someone else, or attend Sunday live — without a personal reminder from the founder that week.
3. They are a distinct human
One person, one seat. Alt accounts, the founder, and one-session drop-ins do not count. Marianna / Sude / Bayo can count once they meet rule 2.
Does not count
A Skool signup with no return visit. A WhatsApp-only regular who never enters Skool. A paid member who never posts or attends. A founder-seeded board post. A one-off meetup guest. Those are useful, but they are not this milestone.

🗺️ Where It Sits

This is an inside-Stage-2 community milestone. It does not replace Quality Gates. The $10,000 / 2-launch rule remains the only Stage 2→3 exit.
