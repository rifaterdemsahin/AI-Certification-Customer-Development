✅ Skool Founder Posting — Is This the Way?

Founder philosophy & sanity check: Focus on evergreen value without founder spam. The founder respects members' time by continuously updating, fixing, and maintaining existing anchor posts before creating new threads, while actively encouraging community members to post their own case studies, questions, and workflows. Question: is board-first, founder-seeded Skool with evergreen living hubs the right move right now?

🧭 Verdict at a Glance

✅ Keep doing
Build Classroom yourself. Seed the board with useful + passion posts. Skip cold email as a primary tactic. This matches Stage 2: Skool is the LMS/community home, founder-led until ~$100k ARR (see H11).
⚠️ Boundary
Board posts only reach people already inside. Skool is not a discovery channel. Without a warm invite loop (WhatsApp free cohort, Sunday attendees, LinkedIn/YouTube CTA), you get a polished empty room — not a test of H5 or the H9 $10k gate.

One-line answer: Yes — founder-built classrooms and founder-seeded posts are the right Stage 2 work. Add invites for people you already know (free cohort / WhatsApp / meetups). Do not treat “posting to the board” as a substitute for acquisition.

🛡️ Founder Guardrail: Evergreen Hubs & Respecting Audience Time

"Founder Does Not Spam" — The Evergreen Living Hub Philosophy

Core Founder Philosophy: Community health is defined by respect for members' attention and bandwidth. Rather than flooding the board with short-lived announcement threads, the founder adheres to four operational posting rules:

🌲 1. Focus to be Evergreen
All founder resources (Classrooms, glossaries, setup guides, tool comparisons) are designed as permanent, living reference hubs that retain value over months and years rather than expiring in 24 hours.
🚫 2. Founder Does Not Spam
Zero notification clutter. The founder avoids broadcasting trivial daily alerts or splintered single-course threads. The feed remains uncluttered so genuine peer interactions stay visible.
🛠️ 3. Fix & Update Existing Posts Before Posting New
Before ever publishing a new thread, the founder revisits, clarifies, debugs, and enriches existing hub posts (e.g. "Courses" or "Glossary"). Audience time is deeply respected — updates live where learners already bookmarked them.
📣 4. Encourage People to Post
The board is not a founder monologue; it belongs to the builders. Members are actively invited and celebrated for sharing their own project builds, architectural case studies (e.g. Sude's Obsidian Second Brain), and workflow questions.
  • ⏱️Respecting member time: Engineers have busy full-time jobs. Keeping founder posts centralized protects attention bandwidth (see Member Bandwidth Guardrails).
  • 💬Living discussion threads: High-signal replies and Q&A accumulate under established evergreen hubs instead of splitting into dead standalone threads.
  • 🌱Organic community transition: As member posts increase, founder activity naturally shifts from initiator to facilitator (H8).
