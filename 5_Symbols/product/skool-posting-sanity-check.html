✅ Skool Founder Posting — Is This the Way?

Founder snapshot (2026-08-12): Skool is set up, Classroom modules are being filled, and posts on the Delivery Pilot board are written by Rifat alone — welcome, install links, assessments, glossary, Claude course, Obsidian, Grok CLI notes, model comparison. No cold-email campaign. Question: is board-first, founder-seeded Skool the right move right now?

🧭 Verdict at a Glance

✅ Keep doing
Build Classroom yourself. Seed the board with useful + passion posts. Skip cold email as a primary tactic. This matches Stage 2: Skool is the LMS/community home, founder-led until ~$100k ARR (see H11).
⚠️ Boundary
Board posts only reach people already inside. Skool is not a discovery channel. Without a warm invite loop (WhatsApp free cohort, Sunday attendees, LinkedIn/YouTube CTA), you get a polished empty room — not a test of H5 or the H9 $10k gate.

One-line answer: Yes — founder-built classrooms and founder-seeded posts are the right Stage 2 work. Add invites for people you already know (free cohort / WhatsApp / meetups). Do not treat “posting to the board” as a substitute for acquisition.

🧱 Board Posts Cover One Layer, Not All Five

1. Acquisition

YouTube, LinkedIn, meetups, WhatsApp referrals — outside Skool. Board posts do not grow this. See Organic Growth.

2–3. Activation + Retention ✅

This is what founder board posts + Classroom + calendar are for. Welcome, installs, assessments, and Sunday events live here.

4–5. Revenue + Referral

Still untested on Skool. Free-cohort referrals already exist on WhatsApp (H5 P6). Paid path is the real Stage 2 test (H9).

🎚️ Content Mix Rule

~70% on-mission
Welcome, installs, assessments, glossary, Claude course, Sunday prep, practice questions (H27), Classroom progress.
~30% passion / tooling
Grok CLI, model comparisons, Second Brain tooling — authentic founder brand, but not the majority of the feed.

Every post should end with one clear action: introduce yourself, install X, complete assessment, or RSVP Sunday.

🗓️ Next 14 Days Operating System

  • ✅Keep: Classroom build (Cohorts 1–5 first), 2–4 high-quality board posts/week, passion posts in the mix.
  • 🚀Do this week: one warm invite message to every free-cohort / WhatsApp person — “Lab home is Skool → introduce yourself under Welcome.”
  • 🔁Dual-post prep: same mid-week install list on WhatsApp and Skool for 2–4 weeks (do not cut WhatsApp cold — see Cohort Prep).
  • 📣One public CTA: LinkedIn post or YouTube description pointing at free community / next Sunday — not a cold sales blast.
  • 📌Reply SLA: answer every intro within 24–48h (fits weekly batch cadence as a second job).
  • 📊Log weekly: member count, non-founder posts/comments, Sunday attendance.
  • ⏸️Defer: cold email sequences, paid ads, perfecting all 7 classrooms before first paid offer.
