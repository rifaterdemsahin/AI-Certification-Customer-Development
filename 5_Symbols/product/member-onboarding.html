🪜 Detailed Step-by-Step Onboarding Walkthrough

Follow these 5 core milestones to transition smoothly from a brand-new joiner into an active practitioner building real-world AI pipelines and preparing for industry certification.

1

Orientation & Expectation Setting ("What to Expect")

Estimated Time: 3 Minutes · Mindset & Cadence Alignment

The first step is understanding what the community is — and what it is not. Delivery Pilot is an active practitioner dojo, not an endless video library that collects dust.

✅ What You Should Expect:
  • $0 Free Tier by Default: Access to free cohort replays, starter toolkits, and open community discussions.
  • Weekly Live Screen-Shares: Real live coding, terminal walkthroughs, and architecture teardowns every Sunday 13:30–14:30 UK.
  • Hands-On Practitioner Reality: Focus on real-world enterprise delivery, avoiding academic fluff.
  • Zero Gatekeeping: Direct answers from founder Rifat Erdem Sahin and veteran peer engineers.
🚫 What We Never Do:
  • No High-Pressure Upsells: No forced upgrades or spammy webinars pretending to teach.
  • No Guaranteed Pass Claims: We provide real skills and practice; passing requires your hands-on work.
  • No 100-Tab Cognitive Overload: We enforce the 5-10-5 rule and clean, curated vaults.
  • No Silent Observers: Everyone is encouraged to ask questions, break code, and learn together.
2

Community Profile Setup & The 60-Second Icebreaker

Estimated Time: 2 Minutes · Connect with Peers

Communities thrive on connection, not anonymous lurking. Setting up your profile unlocks peer matching and enables founder Rifat to point you directly at the exact Classroom modules tailored to your background.

📋 The 3-Item Profile Checklist:
  1. Add a Real Photo & Location: Helps peers recognize you during Sunday live sessions.
  2. State Your Target Role / Exam: (e.g. "Prepping for Claude Certified Architect" or "DevOps Engineer moving into AI Contracting").
  3. Post Your Icebreaker in #introduce-yourself: Use our 3-bullet template:
    • Background: [e.g. Non-tech, SysAdmin, Python dev, PM]
    • Current Goal: [e.g. Pass Claude Cert / Automate workflows / £500/day contract]
    • Biggest Challenge: [e.g. Where to start / Context Engineering / Setting up local models]

❓ Frequently Asked Questions & Friction Busters

🤔 I have no coding or DevOps background. Can I really follow along?

Absolutely. Modern AI architecture with tools like Claude Code and prompt orchestrators allows non-programmers to build complex software. Our Sunday live cohorts start from first principles with zero jargon, and our community includes product managers, career switchers, and executives learning alongside engineers.

⏰ What if I cannot attend the Sunday 13:30 UK London live session?

No problem. Every Sunday cohort is recorded in full and published directly to the Classroom tab within 2 hours of call completion. You can submit questions asynchronously on the community board, and founder Rifat answers them during the daily community pass.

💳 Is the community really free, or will I hit a paywall?

The Standard Membership ($0) is completely free and provides access to free cohort replays, starter templates, and community discussions forever. We offer optional paid upgrades ($1/mo Premium for full archive vaults, or $250/yr VIP Share Screen for recruiter promotion and live screen-share seats), but upgrading is 100% voluntary.

🎯 Which certification should I target first?

We recommend starting with the Claude Certified Architect – Professional (issued by Anthropic via Pearson VUE). It is the premier enterprise credential testing practical agentic workflows, prompt caching, and context engineering. In Stage 3, we also provide roadmaps for AWS Certified AI Practitioner and Azure AI Engineer Associate.
