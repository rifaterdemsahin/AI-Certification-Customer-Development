🎮 Implementing the Skool Games Gamification Engine

How XP Leaderboards Drive 80%+ Monthly Retention

Top Skool creators don't just deliver courses; they turn participation into a game. Members earn 1 point per like on their posts and comments. By locking valuable certification resources behind level gates, members actively help each other:

Level 1 → 3 (Onboarding & Fundamentals)
Unlocks foundational cheat sheets, exam blueprints, and syllabus checklists. Encourages new members to introduce themselves.
Level 4 → 6 (Core Practice & Scenarios)
Unlocks complex multi-agent coding templates, practice exam answer keys, and prompt evaluation scripts.
Level 7 → 9 (Elite & Mentorship)
Unlocks private direct Q&A sessions, live hot-seat priority, and super-affiliate rev-share permissions.
