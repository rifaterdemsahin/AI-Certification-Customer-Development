AI CERTIFICATION HELPER — Business Summary
Founder: Rifat Erdem Sahin | Updated: Aug 2026 | Stage: Customer Discovery → Validation (Steve Blank Customer Development)

ONE-LINE PITCH
Animated YouTube videos that teach AI/cloud certification content, funneling free viewers into a $10/mo prep membership and $250-$500 live cohort bootcamps aimed at Anthropic's Claude Certified Architect - Professional exam (issued via Pearson VUE; this business never issues the certification itself and makes no pass guarantee).

PROBLEM
Generative AI is reshaping technical job requirements faster than professionals can retrain. 63% of employers name the AI skills gap their top transformation barrier and 75% report no formal in-house AI training. Existing cert-prep material (500-page manuals, 40-hour courses) is dry, with low completion.

SOLUTION
Short, animated, visually distilled training videos, free on YouTube via organic search + the founder's LinkedIn network ($0 ad spend), funneling into three paid tiers:
- $10/mo membership: recorded cohort replays + community
- $250 (3-month) or $500 (12-month) live cohort: real-time, screen-share problem-solving, Sundays 9pm
- $29 one-time Exam Prep Bundle: memory cards + practice exam + mock exam (low-friction entry SKU)

WHO IT'S FOR
Technical professionals (developers, cloud/ML/LLM engineers, IT consultants) who feel AI is outpacing their ability to retrain and find existing prep material too dry to finish.

MARKET SIZE
TAM ~25M developers adapting to AI-driven engineering worldwide. SAM ~3M actively seeking cloud/ML/LLM certification - corroborated by Forward-Deployed-Engineer job postings up 700-800%+ YoY (Indeed data, 643 to 5,330 postings Apr'25-Apr'26). SOM ~50k reachable in 3 years via founder's channels (working estimate, not yet independently verified).

TRACTION (as of Aug 2026)
- Free Sunday cohort running 8+ consecutive weeks, audience growing past its initial ~4/session baseline, with an organic WhatsApp peer community and first word-of-mouth referrals
- First produced video: 68.5% average view retention (target floor: >40%)
- 5 completed discovery calls with working professionals expressing real problem-level need
- A candidate met through the meetup channel (not our product) passed the Claude Certified Associate exam 917/1000 - confirms the certification and the channel both convert to real action
- Capgemini Invent publicly confirmed sponsoring an employee's Anthropic certification - corroborates the B2B/consulting bulk-certification channel thesis
- $0 of $10,000 collected so far toward the Stage 2 to 3 revenue gate (gate = 2 consecutive paid cohort launches combining to $10k)

UNIT ECONOMICS
CAC: $0 (organic-only acquisition by design). Membership LTV: ~$100 (10-month average, unvalidated assumption). Cohort seat: $250-$500 per student; ~4 students/cohort today. Fixed costs: ~$100/mo AI infra, $0 hosting (YouTube-native).

MOAT
Founder-market fit (founder personally passed the target exam with a high score; people now reach out directly because of that result), a compounding SEO-indexed video catalog, live-cohort peer network effects, and an early position in Anthropic's Claude Partner Network practitioner-tier flywheel. Honestly thin today: no proprietary technology, single-founder delivery, single-platform (YouTube) dependency.

BIGGEST RISKS
Founder-as-single-point-of-failure; YouTube platform dependency; unsourced TAM/SAM/SOM precision; single-niche focus on one vendor's certification (Anthropic/Claude).

MILESTONES / ASK
Next gate: $10,000 across 2 consecutive paid cohort launches (Stage 2 to 3). Then: 1,000x subscriber growth per video (~30 to ~30,000 subs) as the Stage 3 metric. Then: $100,000 ARR triggers the first hires (editor, cohort coordinator, technical TAs) in Stage 4. Run solo as a second job, self-funded by cohort revenue - no external funding sought at this stage.

SELF-ASSESSED CONFIDENCE
28/100 on this project's own internal hypothesis-validation rubric (22 tracked hypotheses; most are still "in testing" or "planned" rather than "validated"). Published transparently as part of an ongoing Customer Development process, not a sales claim.
