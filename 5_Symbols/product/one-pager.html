AI CERTIFICATION HELPER — Business Summary & Investor Executive Pitch
Founder: Rifat Erdem Sahin | Updated: Aug 2026 | Stage: Customer Discovery → Validation (Steve Blank Customer Development)
Positioning: Visual AI-Powered Technical Education

ONE-LINE PITCH
AI-generated animated lessons that transform dense cloud and AI certification blueprints into 5-minute visual explanations, funneling free viewers into a $10/mo Skool community and $250–$500 live problem-solving cohorts.

THE 4 CORE CARDS
1. PROBLEM: AI is reshaping cloud, DevOps, data, and software roles faster than traditional training can keep pace. Existing certification content (500-page manuals, 40-hour lecture courses) is text-heavy, dry, and suffers from notoriously low completion rates. Employers report major AI skills gaps (GOV.UK AI Labour Market Survey 2025).
2. SOLUTION: AI-generated animated lessons transform dense technical concepts into 5-minute visual explanations, helping professionals learn faster, retain architecture blueprints, and prepare for high-stakes certifications. Paired with weekly Sunday live screen-share workouts.
3. MARKET: Rapid AI adoption drives global demand for cloud, AI, and automation skills across a multi-billion dollar certification market (Research & Markets). Employers increasingly use IT certifications to close high-risk skills gaps and gain competitive advantage (Pearson VUE 2026). Order-of-magnitude working model: ~35M+ tech pros (TAM across Dev, DevOps, QA, Security, Grads) → ~3M–5M active seekers / 729% YoY FDE growth (SAM) → ~50k reachable (SOM).
4. BUSINESS MODEL & VALUE STACK: 6-step conversion stack: Free YouTube animations ($0 CAC) → Skool Community ($1/mo or $10/mo) → $29 Exam Prep Bundle (memory cards + mock exams) → Hands-on Labs & Second Brain presets → Sunday Live Screen-Share Sessions → $250–$500 Live Cohorts & FDE Career Bootcamps.

TRACTION (as of Aug 2026)
- Free Sunday cohort running 8+ consecutive weeks with active peer attendance and WhatsApp community
- First produced video: 68.5% average view retention (target floor: >40%)
- 6+ completed qualitative discovery interviews (Jamal, B., Brian, Tomiwa, Mehmet, HCL DevOps screen)
- Candidate passed Claude Certified Associate 917/1000; corporate partners (Capgemini, Accenture) actively sponsoring cert vouchers

UNIT ECONOMICS & FUNNEL
CAC: $0 (organic YouTube search & LinkedIn audience). Entry SKU: $29 one-time prep bundle. Membership: $10/mo. Cohort: $250–$500/year. Fixed monthly costs: ~$100 AI infra + $0 hosting.

THE SECRET INSIGHT (MOAT)
"AI-generated visual explanations reduce learning time for complex cloud and AI concepts by turning dense technical material into 5-minute animations." Visual AI-powered technical education creates an authentic instructional and production speed moat over legacy text publishers and generic MOOCs.
