📦 The 6 Core Skool Business Models

1

Free

Mechanic: $0 entry to maximize top-of-funnel discovery, organic member volume, and word-of-mouth momentum.

Role in our funnel: Initial 8-week free Sunday cohort & $0 Peek preview tier.
2

Subscription

Mechanic: Predictable monthly recurring revenue (MRR) for continuous access to Classroom lessons, weekly live calls, and community boards.

Role in our funnel: Core $10/mo (or $1/mo pilot) Sit In monthly membership tier.
3

Freemium (Live)

Mechanic: Free tier for community discussion with locked premium courses, recordings, or VIP live room privileges.

Our current structure: Standard ($0) / Premium ($1/mo) / VIP ($250/yr) on Delivery Pilot.
4

Tiers (Ladder)

Mechanic: A 3-step value ladder segmented by access depth (e.g., Bronze / Silver / Gold or Peek / Sit In / Share Screen).

Role in our funnel: Peek ($0) / Sit In ($1–$10/mo) / Share Screen ($250/yr).
5

1-Time Payment

Mechanic: Upfront fixed fee for lifetime community access, intensive cohort bootcamp passes, or single course asset unlock.

Role in our funnel: $250–$500 8-week intensive certification bootcamps (H9).
6

Custom Combo

Mechanic: Hybrid architecture combining free trials, annual discounts, corporate seat bundles, and add-on exam reviews.

Role in our funnel: Stage 3 multi-cert bundle (Claude + Azure + Nvidia + Google).

🔄 The 4 Stages of Skool Monetization Evolution

1

Stage 1: Free — Figure It Out

Objective: Dial in topic resonance, organic growth channels, member engagement, and weekly watch retention before asking for money.

  • ✅Status: Executed during the initial 8-week free Sunday cohort (July–August 2026).
2
📍 Current Active Position

Stage 2: Freemium — Test Willingness to Pay

Objective: Introduce low-friction paid options ($1/mo tripwire, $250 VIP) alongside a $0 preview to measure real conversion without scaring away organic traffic.

  • 🟡Status: Active on skool.com/delivery-pilot-8938 ($0 Peek / $1 Premium / $250 VIP screen-share).
3

Stage 3: Subscription — Paid-Only Filtering

Objective: Eliminate free tire-kickers. Once high-retention regulars are established, transition community access to paid-only ($10/mo baseline).

  • ⚪Gate: Triggered once community crosses 50 paid members (Pricing Change Roadmap).
4

Stage 4: Tiers — 3-Tier Value Maximization

Objective: Deploy a mature 3-tier membership ladder with price points matching customer depth: Community ($10/mo), Full Course Bundle ($29/mo), and Live Mastermind/Enterprise VIP ($1,000/yr).

  • ⚪Gate: Unlocks at Stage 3 Customer Creation / $100k ARR Company Building (Stage 4).
