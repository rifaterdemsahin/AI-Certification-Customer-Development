🔄 YouTube / LinkedIn → Skool Audience Handoff

Order is fixed: discovery and reach live on YouTube and LinkedIn; paid delivery, classroom, forum, calendar, and billing live on Skool. Skool is the Stage 2 home for members who already decided to come inside — not a Stage 1 discovery channel. Which format goes on which of those three surfaces — short vs long, animated vs workshop vs course — is on Short vs Long Form.

📌 The Order (One Line)

YouTube / LinkedIn find people → CTA / warm invite hands them off → Skool teaches, bills, and retains them.

🔀 Audience Flow

1. Acquisition (outside)

YouTube — search, playlists, animated free courses, description links (H4, H7).

LinkedIn — founder posts, newsletter, cert-badge social proof loop.

Plus meetups, WhatsApp free Sunday cohort, face-to-face — all outside Skool. See Organic Growth.

2. Handoff (the bridge)

Explicit CTAs move a viewer from public reach into a Skool destination:

  • 📺YouTube description / end-screen → Skool free community or $10/mo Classroom
  • 💼LinkedIn post / newsletter → same invite link
  • 💬WhatsApp free-cohort invite → warm members into Skool (not cold board posting)

Title → destination mapping: YouTube Titles → Skool Mapping.

3. Delivery (inside Skool)

Stage 2 platform only — not discovery:

  • 📁Classroom — modules, 2-hour Sunday replays, practice questions
  • 💬Forum — peer help, founder seeding
  • 🗓️Calendar — Sunday 9 PM live sessions
  • 💳Billing — $10/mo, $29 bundle path, $250–$500 cohort

Spec: Skool LMS Integration. Replay rule: 2-Hour Workshop Upload.

🧠 Why This Order (Rationale)

Why acquisition stays outside Skool

YouTube already ranks for exam-prep search intent; LinkedIn already carries professional social proof. Those networks bring new people at near-zero CAC when content quality holds (≥40% retention floor, free-course playlists).

Skool has no public search engine and no professional graph. Board posts only reach members already inside. Treating Skool as discovery produces a polished empty room — not a test of H5 organic sales or the H9 $10k gate. Documented in the founder posting sanity check.

Why delivery lives on Skool (Stage 2)

Stage 2 proves repeatable paid sales, not more free reach. That needs Classroom structure, event calendar, peer forum, and billing without custom code ($99/mo off-the-shelf).

In Stage 1 (Customer Discovery), paid LMS setup is premature — interviews and free tools (YouTube, WhatsApp) are enough. Skool activates when we host memberships and cohorts for the validation gate. See Skool LMS Integration and Skool vs. YouTube.

Why a deliberate handoff (not a merge)

Collapsing both jobs onto one platform fails differently each way:

  • ❌YouTube-only delivery: weak LMS, weak peer accountability, awkward monetization (30% cut or external paywalls) — hard to run cohorts at Stage 2 quality.
  • ❌Skool-only acquisition: no algorithm, no LinkedIn graph; founder board posts cannot replace organic distribution.
  • ✅Stack + handoff: YouTube/LinkedIn maximize reach; Skool maximizes completion, community, and checkout; CTAs and warm invites are the only bridge that must be built and measured.

🔁 The Reverse Handoff (Skool → public)

Audience also flows out of Skool into acquisition channels — that is intentional, not leakage:

Cert success stories
Members who pass post badges on LinkedIn (social proof loop in Organic Growth). That is Skool → LinkedIn reach, not Skool discovery of strangers.
Content source
Live cohort questions and Classroom friction become the next YouTube scripts — delivery informs acquisition topics.
Referral invite
Existing free-cohort / Skool members bring peers via WhatsApp (H5 P6). Still warm invite, not board-as-ads.

✅ Operational Rules

Do

  • 1Publish free animated courses on YouTube with a single clear Skool CTA.
  • 2Share the same invite on LinkedIn and the free Sunday WhatsApp list.
  • 3Build Classroom + calendar + billing on Skool for people who click through.
  • 4Map every title to a destination before publishing (mapping page).

Don't

  • 1Treat Skool board posts as the growth plan (see posting sanity check).
  • 2Run paid checkout primarily on YouTube Channel Memberships.
  • 3Spin up Skool as a Stage 1 discovery experiment before interviews and free cohorts prove demand.
  • 4Publish a YouTube CTA with no live Skool landing destination.

🔗 Related Hypotheses

  • 💭H4 — free YouTube trust/traffic converts a fraction to paid membership (~1% target, still measuring).
  • 💭H7 — structured playlists + CTR path from view → newsletter → cohort.
  • 💭H5 — organic cohort sales need warm acquisition + a real delivery home (Skool), not board-only growth.
  • 💭H8 — PMF evidence (completion, peer help, pass readiness) is measured inside the delivery surface.
