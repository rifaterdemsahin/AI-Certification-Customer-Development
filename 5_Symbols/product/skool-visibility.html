🔀 What the two options mean

These are Skool's own labels, copied as they appear on the settings screen.

Do not pick this week

🔒 Private

“Only members can see what's inside.”

  • Logged-out visitors see About + the join page only.
  • Posts, members, and the board are hidden until they join (even $0 Peek).
  • Content is hidden from search engines and from Skool Discovery.
  • Keywords on the sibling page stop matching searchers.
Recommended

🌐 Public

“Anyone can see what's inside. Only members can post.”

  • Anyone can read the community board without joining.
  • Only members can post, reply, or comment.
  • The group can appear in Skool Discovery and on Google.
  • Classroom lessons and calendar events stay locked by membership level.

🔗 Why this is Public (H5 Premise 11)

Organic cohort sales (H5) need strangers to see the room before they pay. Private would force a join just to look, which fights the Peek preview ramp and makes the keywords page a no-op. Public does not count as a paid enrollment — flipping the toggle is an ops step, not a $250 Share Screen sale.
