✅ Invite Script (keep it short)

What to send
A one-message WhatsApp or LinkedIn note: the Skool room is live, they are invited as a founding member, Sunday 9pm still happens, Classroom has the replays. Include the existing invite link. Do not attach a price. Do not pitch the onsite pilot in the same message.
What not to claim
Do not call a join a paid enrollment. Do not treat three founding members as the 20–40 H5 target. Do not treat Marianna's Skool seat as the H17 onsite pilot. Do not add more names until these three have either joined or declined.
