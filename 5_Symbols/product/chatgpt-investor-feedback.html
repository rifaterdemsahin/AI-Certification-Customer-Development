🎴 The 4 Rewritten Core Cards (Investor-Ready)

1. Problem
AI is reshaping cloud, DevOps, data, and software roles faster than traditional training can keep pace. Existing certification content is long, text-heavy, and has notoriously low completion rates.
2. Solution (The Secret Insight)
AI-generated animated lessons transform dense technical concepts into 5-minute visual explanations, helping professionals learn faster, retain architecture blueprints, and prepare for certifications.
3. Market (Grounded & Cited)
Rapid AI adoption is driving global demand for cloud, AI, and automation skills across a multi-billion dollar market (Research & Markets). Employers increasingly value certifications and practical proof to close high-risk skills gaps (Pearson VUE 2026).
4. Business Model & Value Stack
Free YouTube content ($0 CAC) → $10/month Skool community membership → $29 entry exam prep bundles → $250–$500 live cohorts, hands-on labs, screen-shares, and Forward Deployed Engineer career bootcamps.
