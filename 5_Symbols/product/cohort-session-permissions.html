🔒 Cohort Session Permissions

Joiners already answer discovery questions on the membership intake. This page adds the missing recording approval for Sunday live labs. Sunday is a screen-share room (terminals, inboxes, API keys). Replays live in Skool Classroom for members — not public YouTube. See H5 Premise 3 and workshop upload.

  • Collects:Yes / listen-only / extra quote permission
  • Where:Skool Membership Question 3 + Sunday verbal read
  • Default store:Paid Classroom replay only
  • Public YouTube:Never without a second, named yes
  • Linked:H5 · H29 · H8
  • Decline path:Camera off, no screen share — still welcome

📌 What we ask permission for

In scope (if they say yes)

  1. Record Sunday 9–11pm UK audio, camera, and shared screens.
  2. Store the file in Skool Classroom as the member replay (same night, timestamps only).
  3. Optional extra: first-name quotes in discovery notes (H29). Never a full legal name on the public site.

Out of scope unless they opt in twice

  1. Public or unlisted YouTube of the live room.
  2. Clips that show a member’s face or terminal on social.
  3. Using their screen as a marketing thumbnail.

📝 Membership Question 3 — paste into Skool

Add this on the same audience collect form as the 4 pillars and “What do you hope to get out of the group?” Required. Single select.

Question (required, single select): Sunday live labs are recorded so members who miss 9pm can replay inside Skool Classroom. This is not a public YouTube video. I give permission to: A) Record my voice / camera / screen in Sunday cohort and keep the replay inside Skool Classroom (members only). B) Same as A, plus first-name quotes in internal discovery notes (no full legal name on the public site). C) Listen only — camera off, I will not share my screen. Do not put my face or terminal on the replay. D) I do not consent to being recorded. I will stay muted with camera off, or I will skip live and use other materials.

Founder gate: A or B → they may share screen. C or D → still approve the join; they sit listen-only or skip live. Never block membership for D.

🎙️ Sunday verbal read (first 60 seconds)

This session is recorded for Skool Classroom members only. It is not going on public YouTube. If you share a screen, assume keys and inboxes can show — blur or stop share if needed. Camera-off / listen-only is fine. Say “no record” in chat and I will keep you off the replay. If you already answered the join question, that answer still stands tonight.

🛠️ How the founder uses the answer

A / B — screen-share OK
VIP Share Screen and Saturday 1-1 tracks may proceed. Upload native to Classroom. B also allows a first-name quote on Interview Recording.
C — listen only
They keep Peek/Sit In access. Do not ask them to share screen. If they appear on the recording by accident, crop or mute that stretch before upload.
D — no record
Still a member. Prefer they skip the live call or stay fully off-camera. If they attend, pause record or edit them out. Do not treat D as a ban.
