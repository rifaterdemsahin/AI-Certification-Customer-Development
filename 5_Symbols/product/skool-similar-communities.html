💡 Strategic Takeaways for Delivery Pilot

1. Price Elasticity Validation ($97/mo)
Pope's 214 members at $97/mo (~$20.7k MRR) confirms that AI engineers and builders readily pay premium subscription rates when live builds, roundtables, and technical peer interaction are offered. Our $10/mo membership and $250/yr VIP are well within proven willingness-to-pay thresholds (H5).
2. Proctored Exam vs. General Building
General "AI builder" communities do not solve the formal credentialing and enterprise resume filter problem (H1). By focusing specifically on Pearson VUE proctored certifications (Anthropic Claude Architect, expanding to Nvidia/Azure in Stage 3), we maintain an uncontested wedge against generic builder communities.
3. Public Visibility & Tripwire Advantage
While AI Architects is Private ($97 barrier to view), our Public Skool model enables organic keyword discoverability and zero-friction entry via our $0 Peek / $1 Sit In tripwire. This lowers the friction to enter our Sunday live screen room before buying the $250 seat.
