Discipline 3 · Visibility

📊 Keep a Compelling Scoreboard

Step 3 of 4

The Problem: If the scoreboard is complex, buried in spreadsheet directories, or only shows lag measures, the team doesn't know where they stand. Accountability fades when people cannot easily determine if they are winning or losing.

💡 The Solution: The 5-Second Rule

A compelling scoreboard must pass the **5-Second Rule**: Any observer (or the founder himself) must be able to look at the scoreboard and, in 5 seconds or less, tell exactly whether the business is **winning** or **losing**. It must display both:

  • 📊Lag Measures: Where we stand relative to our ultimate WIG milestones (e.g., $10k validation revenue).
  • ⚙️Lead Measures: Whether we completed the high-leverage weekly behaviors that drive the lag indicators.
