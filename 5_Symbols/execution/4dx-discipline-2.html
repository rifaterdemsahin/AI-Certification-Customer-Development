⚖️ Act on Lead Measures

Step 2 of 4

The Problem: Lag measures are historical outcomes. By the time you get the data, the performance that drove it is in the past, and you cannot change it. You cannot "manage" revenue directly—you can only manage the activities that produce it.

💡 The Solution: Focus on Predictive, Influenceable Measures

While a lag measure tells you if you have achieved the goal, a **lead measure** tells you if you are *likely* to achieve it. Lead measures must meet two criteria:

🔮 1. Predictive
It measures a behavior or activity that directly influences the lag measure. If the lead measure moves, the lag measure is mathematically expected to follow.
⚙️ 2. Influenceable
It can be directly controlled or performed by the team or founder without relying heavily on third parties. Rifat must be able to directly do it.

📊 Lag vs. Lead Measures (AI Certification Helper)

Lag Measures (What We Want) Lead Measures (What We Control)
💰 $10,000 Validation Revenue (WIG 1)

Cannot force someone to buy in real-time.

  • Outreach Volume: Send ≥20 personalized outreach posts or DMs weekly to target audiences in LinkedIn watering holes (Watering Holes).
  • Cohort Attendance: Maintain ≥80% attendance rate in the Sunday free cohort (indicates high buy-in).
  • Curriculum Design: Commit 8 hours/week strictly to cohort workbook preparation.
🎬 ≥40% Video Retention & 100+ Subs (WIG 2)

Calculated post-publish. YouTube algorithm determines views.

  • Visual Asset Density: Incorporate ≥3 visual animations or whiteboard components per minute of video script to maintain visual stimulation.
  • Script Editing passes: Perform ≥2 script reviews for brevity (trimming "filler" talk) before recording.
  • Upload Baseline: Publish 3 short educational video components per week.
