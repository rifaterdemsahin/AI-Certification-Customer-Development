🎯 Focus on the Wildly Important

Step 1 of 4

The Problem: Attempting to achieve 10 to 12 strategic goals simultaneously guarantees achieving 0. Diluting founder bandwidth across multiple certifying vendors, custom LMS developments, or premature scaling actions guarantees that nothing gets validated.

💡 The Solution: Identify 1-2 WIGs

A **Wildly Important Goal (WIG)** is a goal that can fail all other goals but makes a failure in any other goal irrelevant. It must be isolated from the whirlwind and defined in a way that makes success unambiguous.

📐 The WIG Formula
"From [Baseline X] → To [Target Y] → By [Deadline]"

Every WIG must have a clear beginning point, ending point, and timeline. No vague assertions like "improve marketing" or "grow subscribers." It must be a measurable gap closed by a date.

🔍 AI Certification Helper WIG Breakdown

Given Rifat's constraints as a solo founder working part-time, the project isolates exactly two WIGs representing the exits of Steve Blank's Stage 1 (Customer Discovery) and Stage 2 (Customer Validation).

WIG 1: Validation Revenue (Stage 2 Exit)

💰 Validate Repeatable Sales

Establish that developers will pay high-ticket prices for live exam preparation cohorts without hiring help.

Formula Alignment:
From $0 to $10,000 USD in combined gross revenue across 2 consecutive cohort launches by Q2 2027.

WIG 2: Content MVP Quality (Stage 1 Exit)

🎬 Prove Organic Lesson Value

Ensure the produced animated video lessons build genuine engagement before spending money.

Formula Alignment:
From 0% to ≥40% average watch retention across 5 lessons and 100+ organic subscribers by Q4 2026.
