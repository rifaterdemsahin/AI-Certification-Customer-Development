🔄 Create a Cadence of Accountability

Step 4 of 4

The Problem: Even the best-defined goals and scoreboards fade if there is no regular rhythm. When the whirlwind of the main contracting job gets heavy, strategic goals are the first to get forgotten unless there is a set time for reckoning.

💡 The Solution: The Weekly WIG Meeting

A **WIG Meeting** is a weekly 10-to-15 minute meeting dedicated solely to reviewing progress against WIGs. It is fast, structured, and strictly protects strategic time.

📋 3-Step WIG Meeting Agenda
  1. 1. Account: Review commitments made the prior week (Did I hit my outreach? Did I record the videos?).
  2. 2. Review Scoreboard: Look at the scoreboard (Discipline 3) to see if lead activities are moving the lag results.
  3. 3. Plan: Make 1-2 new commitments for the upcoming week that will directly move the lead measures.
Cadence Time
Sunday 13:15 – 13:30 UK
Scheduled exactly 15 minutes before the live cohort starts at 13:30 UK (Bandwidth Schedule).

🧑‍💻 WIG Cadence for a Solo Founder

When operating as a solo founder, the WIG meeting is a **weekly self-audit**. Without a team to keep you accountable, you must treat your future self as a partner. On Sunday evening, Rifat logs his scorecard results, writes down his commitments for the next week, and explicitly checks off past targets.

🚨 Crucial Rule of WIG Meetings
**Never allow the whirlwind into the WIG meeting.** It is not a general operational catch-up. Do not discuss day-job projects, admin issues, or generic emails. Focus exclusively on moving the lead measures.
