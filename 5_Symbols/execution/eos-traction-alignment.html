1. Vision

Two-page V/TO, eight questions: Core Values, Core Focus, 10-Year Target, Marketing Strategy (target + 3 Uniques + proven process + guarantee), 3-Year Picture, 1-Year Plan, Rocks, Issues List.

2. People

Accountability Chart (seats first). Right Person / Right Seat. GWC = Get it, Want it, Capacity. You fail Capacity. Integrator is a 45-minute weekly hat, not a hire.

3. Data

Scorecard: 5–15 weekly numbers, one owner, on/off only. Dashboards on this site are catalogs. A Scorecard is a pulse you read in five minutes.

4. Issues

IDS: Identify the root, Discuss once, Solve forever. You Identify at world-class level (H1–H31). Writing another page is not Solve.

5. Process

6–10 core processes, 20/80 documentation, packaged as The Delivery Pilot Way, Followed By All. You have dozens of playbooks. Cut to seven (video, LinkedIn batch, Sunday, Skool garden, interview, checkout, money review).

6. Traction

Rocks (3–7 / 90 days, no yellow) + Meeting Pulse. Level 10 is the heartbeat. Sunday cohort is customer delivery, not the L10. 4DX cadence (D4) is the closest habit — fold it into one L10.
