🎬 YouTube Video: Top-of-Funnel Engine of the Sales & Marketing Plan

Acquisition Engine

In our Business Model Canvas, animated YouTube videos are explicitly classified as a marketing acquisition channel within the overall Sales & Marketing Plan, rather than the core product itself. The video engine captures developer mindshare and systematically funnels qualified candidates toward our paid transformation tiers.

🎥 1. Marketing Hook

Reverse Response animated shorts from the current workspace (aug-video-animation-6-core-message; SOP pipeline) targeting trending AI topics with FDE contractor insights.

🔗 2. Traffic Bridge

Pinned comments, end screens, and video descriptions routing viewers to free cheatsheets and Skool join links.

🏫 3. Community Discovery

Freemium Skool join ramp ($0 Peek / $1 Sit-In) allowing prospects to preview Sunday live interactive workouts.

💎 4. Product Sales

Conversion into the paid course ($250/yr VIP / $250–$500) and 1-1 bespoke coaching (100x multiplier: £2.5k–£10k).

🤝 Volunteer & Free Customer Discovery Strategy

During early Customer Discovery (Stage 1) and Customer Validation (Stage 2), our channels explicitly avoid formal B2B sales cycles or corporate procurement pipelines. Instead, the founder acts as a volunteer, providing free training, workshops, and coaching (such as the Sunday 13:30 UK cohort) to build trust.

The primary objective of these channels is to secure high-quality discovery interviews and customer feedback (e.g. testing willingness to pay, diagnosing study challenges, and validating curriculum fit). Formal B2B sales and outbound corporate training channels are deferred to the **Customer Creation (Stage 3)** and **Company Building (Stage 4)** phases. See Customer Discovery Process.

🛠️ Channel & Tooling Strategy: Stage-Based Comparison

The channels and toolchains evolve depending on the target audience and development stage. During discovery, we focus on zero-cost, lightweight tools for individual non-technical learners, transitioning to enterprise paid tools in the Company Building stage.

Dimension No-Tech/No-Code Track (Discovery & Validation) Enterprise/Paid Track (Company Building)
Target Audience Individual learners, career switchers, no-tech/non-technical backgrounds. Enterprise IT departments, consulting firms, government contractors.
Core Channel Organic YouTube search, personal LinkedIn network, free developer communities. B2B outbound sales, corporate onsite pilots (e.g., Marianna pilot), vendor partner networks.

Acquisition Focus Free feedback, problem discovery interviews, product testimonials. High-margin contract procurement, corporate seat licenses, recurring revenue streams.
