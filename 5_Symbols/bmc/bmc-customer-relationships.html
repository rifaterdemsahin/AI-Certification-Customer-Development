Business Model Canvas Block

💌 Customer Relationships

🎬 Self-Serve Content

Free YouTube lessons and cheatsheets require no relationship management — scalable to unlimited viewers at zero marginal cost.

💬 Comm. Engagement: YouTube and LinkedIn comments are batch-answered in weekly cycles, as Rifat works a contract role during this content generation phase.

🤝 High-Touch Cohort Community

Live streamed sessions, group chat, and 1-1 support during the 4–8 week cohort build real accountability and peer bonds — the WhatsApp/Discord group stays active all week, not just during the live session, see Cohort Prep. Inside Skool, operate as a party not a lecture: Golden Rules of Community Building.

🧑‍🎓 Personal Founder-Led Support

Rifat personally teaches cohort sessions and answers questions, a direct-founder relationship that will partially shift to Technical TAs post-$100k ARR. In Stages 1–2 the posture is listen more than you speak — collect feedback and let the audience cocreate the next change (see Listen More Than You Speak / H29).

Relationship intensity scales with price tier: free content is fully automated, the $10/mo membership is community-assisted, and cohorts are founder-led — see Revenue Streams.
