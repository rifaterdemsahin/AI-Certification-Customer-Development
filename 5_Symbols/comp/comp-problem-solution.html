📐 Problem-Solution Transformation Blueprint

How AI Certification Helper transforms high-friction developer learning into high-retention visual blueprints, validated proctored certifications (Anthropic Claude Architect), and enterprise contractor career readiness.

⚖️ The Core Hypotheses Under Review

😖 The Customer Problem

Friction Profile

Rapid advancements in artificial intelligence are shifting company business structures. Job roles are transforming, and engineers face severe skills inflation (R.A.I.S.E.).

  • ⚠️Expectation Pressure: Employers expect staff to build Agentic chains, configure guardrails, and audit LLM architectures correctly.
  • 📖Dry Study Materials: Preparing for certifications via dry 500-page manuals or boring 40-hour video courses causes cognitive fatigue and low completion rates (<10%).
  • ⏳Time Scarcity & Setup Friction: Working professionals lack the hours to navigate broken setups, outdated tutorials, and uncurated documentation.

💡 Our Proposed Solution

The Value Wedge

"AI Certification Helper Content" — A highly optimized, visually animated syllabus helper that makes preparing for difficult cloud/AI certification exams clear, fast, and high-retention.

  • 🎬Animated Lessons: Complex RAG pipelines, Context Engineering, and API states represented as animated, clean technical drawings (aug-video-animation-2).
  • ✅Practical Guides & Blueprints: Summarizing official exam syllabus blueprints directly into step-by-step interactive cheatsheets and mental models.
  • 🚀Delivery Pilot & Contractor Upskilling: Bridging exam credentials directly into real-world Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) contract opportunities (£500–£1,000+/day).

See Focus for the single core problem statement this component tests, and what's deliberately out of scope while it's being validated.
