🚪 The Transition Gateways

💰 Gate 1: $10,000 Validation Revenue (Stage 2 Exit)

Before any customer creation or company-building activity begins, Customer Validation (Stage 2) must independently produce $10,000 (USD) worth of validated, paying customers. This work is done solely by Rifat, with no hires yet, specifically to keep the validation loop agile and cheap to run.

"Revenue is the ultimate customer validation indicator."

We calculate this as gross cohort enrollments within a single launch period. Generating $10k verifies that both the message and product are positioned correctly in the target market, and is the explicit exit gate from Stage 2 into Stage 3 — not a milestone inside Stage 3 itself.

📈 Gate 2: 1,000x Subscriber Growth Per Video (Stage 3 Focus)

Once Stage 2 is validated, the Customer Creation metric we are actually optimizing for is channel growth, not retention: each published video is expected to move the channel toward 1,000x its current subscriber baseline.

"Subscriber growth proves demand creation is working, not just content quality."

Baseline as of this writing: ~30 subscribers on the channel. The >40% average-view-retention figure used during MVP/PMF testing remains a content-quality floor, but it is no longer the primary Stage 3 gate — subscriber growth per video is. To maximize paid market reach and decrease Claude-only dependency, the curriculum will expand to cover Nvidia, Microsoft (Azure AI), and Google (Gemini) certifications.

🧑‍💻 Rifat Erdem Sahin's Ongoing Second-Job Commitment

Role Profile: Subject Matter Expert (Not a Full-Time Transition)

Rifat is uniquely suited to lead this customer creation engine. He has already completed multiple cloud and AI certification exams, demonstrating deep familiarity with the exam architectures.

Rifat is not planning to leave his primary job. This project is run as a structured, ongoing second job, not a full-time transition — a deliberate choice to keep the model agile and low-risk while it is still being validated. Because technology evolves daily, Rifat still follows a disciplined learning regimen around his primary job: researching API changes, reading papers, building sample multi-agent code templates, and auditing Anthropic's developer documentation to keep the curriculum up-to-date.

Focus: Content Integrity, Sustainably
Rifat's priority within this second job is content integrity. By staying ahead of standard study materials, Rifat ensures the "AI Certification Helper" remains the premium resource for engineering talent, without requiring him to give up his primary income.
