🔍 The Core Diagnostic: Misdiagnosis vs. Decoupled Reality

❌ The Misdiagnosis (Conflation)

Treating animated YouTube videos as the Product. A video is an acquisition and distribution mechanism. High video retention proves people like the animation style, but it provides zero evidence that users will pay for cohort prep, participate in live practice sessions, or find real exam utility.

⚠️ Risk: Optimizing video production while neglecting live student transformation and willingness to pay.
✅ The Decoupled Reality (Steve Blank Framework)

Marketing Materials generate top-of-funnel attention and funnel qualified candidates into the community. The Product is the transformation delivered inside the Skool Delivery Pilot hub: live Sunday screen-shares, interactive scenario drills, mock question breakdowns, and recruiter promotion.

🎯 Result: The paid course and 1-1 coaching are the products; videos are the top-of-funnel traffic bridge.
