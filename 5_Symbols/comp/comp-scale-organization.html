🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Hiring & Governance Blueprint

Reaching $100k annual run-rate triggers the shift from product development to scale organization. To prevent burnout, maintain student support quality, and scale community moderation, four specific roles are introduced:

ROLE 1

🎬 Freelance Video Editor

Takes raw scripts, audio recordings, and visual storyboard assets from Rifat to render high-quality animated tutorials.

  • Responsibility: Animation, editing, sound, rendering.
  • Benefit: Cuts Rifat's video creation time by 80%.
ROLE 2

🗂️ Cohort Coordinator

Manages student scheduling across multiple concurrent cohorts on weekday timelines, processes registrations, moderates community chat channels, and coordinates event calendars.

  • Responsibility: Administrative management, multiple cohort scheduling.
  • Benefit: Weekday cohorts run smoothly at scale with minimal founder friction.
ROLE 3

🛠️ TAs & Cohort Lecturers

Qualified graduates hired to run cohorts on weekday schedules, facilitating the sessions and providing 1-1 support independently of Rifat's active time.

  • Responsibility: Weekday cohort delivery, code reviews, discord help.
  • Benefit: Scales class throughput and tutoring without bottlenecking Rifat's time.
ROLE 4 (MILESTONE)

🛡️ Skool Community Moderators

Elevated cohort alumni and active regulars who run 24/7 peer community triage, enforce the Broken Window anti-spam rules, and curate resource hubs.

  • Responsibility: Community triage, spam pruning, onboarding help.
  • Benefit: Decentralized peer-led governance; zero founder moderation fatigue.
