📉 Cost-Side Model

Acidity check Finding F7: every metric this site tracks is top-line (views, retention, revenue) — nothing prices out the cost side. This page is the business-level cost stack; see Unit Economics for the per-customer version of the same math.

💳 What Actually Costs Cash Today

Line item Monthly Annualized Status
AI infrastructure & inference $100 $1,200 Active, only recurring hard cash cost identified
Video hosting / LMS $0 $0 YouTube used as the platform, avoids this cost entirely
Payment processing fees Unmodeled Unmodeled Gap — a real % of every transaction, not yet tracked
Refund reserve $0 $0 No exam-pass guarantee is offered (F8 resolved), so no refund liability is being reserved against
Freelance editor / cohort coordinator / TAs $0 $0 Deferred until the $100k ARR hiring trigger (H11, see Company Building)

Total known hard cash cost today: $100/month ($1,200/year) — against the $100,000 ARR Stage 4 hiring trigger, that's under 1.2% of the threshold. Almost all of the "margin" the business is currently running on is founder time, not cash, which is exactly what the time-cost model below prices out.

⏱️ The Real Cost: Founder Time

Content production, live cohort delivery, and 1-on-1 feedback all run through one person, valued against his £525/day inside-IR35 contracting rate (see Unit Economics for the hourly breakdown, and Single-Founder Bandwidth for the weekly time ledger this is built on).

📌 This is opportunity cost, not lost income
Because content/cohort work happens in contract-free evening and weekend hours, it isn't directly displacing paid contracting time. It is still a real cost in foregone rest and personal time, and becomes an actual lost-income cost whenever weekend contract extension work is available and turned down in favor of this project.

⚖️ Break-Even Math

Membership vs. fixed infra cost

$100/month infra ÷ $10/mo per member = 10 active members needed just to cover the fixed cash cost, before any founder time is counted.

Cohort vs. fixed infra cost

A single $250 cohort seat covers 2.5 months of the $100/month infra bill outright — the fixed cash floor is trivially easy to clear; the founder-time cost is the real constraint (see Unit Economics).
