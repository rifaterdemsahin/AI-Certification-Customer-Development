💡 Core Empirical Insights & Operational Takeaways
  • The >60% Kinetic Visual Formula: The top performer ("Building an AI Knowledge Engine: Turn 46k Obsidian Notes into Clarity" — 0:43, 62.6% retention, 34 views) and the masterclass teaser (0:07, 66.3% retention) prove that punchy, animated conceptual models hold viewer attention well above the >40% MVP quality floor (H2 / H10).
  • The 2-Minute Fatigue Curve: Standard long-form tutorials and coding demos (2:00–2:50 duration) exhibited heavy drop-offs on YouTube browse traffic (averaging 10.7%–17.9% retention). This reinforces the architectural rule from Short vs Long Form: deep multi-minute tutorials and live code gehören inside Skool and Sunday Live Labs, whereas YouTube must lead with fast animated hooks under 60 seconds.
  • Visual Animation vs. Weak Static Clips: Videos 8 & 10 (0:05–0:12 without animated diagrams or strong narrative hooks) collapsed to 1.0%–1.6% retention. Brevity alone does not guarantee retention; kinetic storyboard animation and immediate cognitive reward are essential.
  • Active Production Strategy: These findings directly validate the aug-video-animation-2 Reverse Response Marketing workflow: reverse conventional tech premises, script tightly for 30–45 second animated delivery, and embed visual models.
