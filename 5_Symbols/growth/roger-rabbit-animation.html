Our Signature Format

To stand out in the technical training market, we are adopting a "Roger Rabbit" animation style. This involves combining real-world video footage with high-quality, animated overlays and characters. This mixed-reality approach creates a unique and highly engaging visual experience.

By using this format, we aim to test the market's response to highly produced, entertaining educational content. This distinct visual style will become our signature, differentiating our materials from standard screen-recordings or dry slide presentations.

This animation style is directly linked to our Reverse Response Marketing strategy.
