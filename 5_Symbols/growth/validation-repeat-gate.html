🚪 Validation Repeat Gate

Acidity check Finding F6, and the question comp-creation-validation.html itself flags but never answers: "What if we hit the $10,000 target once but cannot repeat it?" Launch-day revenue is inflated by the founder's personal network and one-off promotion — a single event is a false-positive risk for a decision this consequential. This page formalizes the fix: the Stage 2→3 gate requires the result to repeat.

🔁 Old Gate vs. New Gate

PREVIOUS (H9, as originally written)

Single Launch, $10,000

$10,000 gross from one cohort launch clears the gate outright. Vulnerable to a one-off spike from warm personal-network intros that doesn't reflect repeatable organic demand.

REVISED (this page)

Two Consecutive Launches

The gate requires two consecutive cohort launches, combining to at least $10,000, before Stage 2→3 transition is considered cleared.

✅ The Revised Gate, in Full

  • 1️⃣Two consecutive cohort launches, back to back, each independently selling seats — not one launch counted twice.
  • 💰Combined revenue ≥ $10,000 across the two launches, at the existing $250–$500 pricing (see Business Model Spec).
  • 🌱Launch 2 must show organic replication — if launch 1's buyers were mostly warm personal-network intros, launch 2 needs to draw meaningfully from the free Sunday cohort funnel and general audience instead, or the "repeatable demand" claim isn't actually being tested.
  • 📋Pre-launch waitlist tracked both times — per the existing mitigation in Risk Analysis, waitlist signal from the weekly free cohort should be logged before each launch date is committed to.

🔗 How the Free Sunday Cohort Feeds This Gate

The weekly free Sunday 9–11pm cohort (see Evidence Map and Test Plan) is the pre-launch waitlist-building mechanism this gate relies on. It's also the same venue running the site's discovery interviews, so the population being converted into paid cohort launches 1 and 2 is the same population already being interviewed about the problem — making it possible to check whether launch 2's buyers are new/organic or the same warm contacts as launch 1.

⚠️ Open Question

This gate has not run yet — no paid cohort launch has been reported in the repo (H5 status: 🟡 In Testing on 8 Skool community signups, not paid enrollments). The two-launch structure is a design decision to reduce false-positive risk, not yet a tested process itself.
