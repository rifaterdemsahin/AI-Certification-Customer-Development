Grandfathering Rule & Authentic Scarcity

Every founding member who joins at today’s $1/month Sit In or $250/year Share Screen tier is permanently grandfathered at that rate for the lifetime of their active membership. Once milestone criteria are satisfied, new signups automatically step up to $10/mo and $1,000/yr.

🎯 The 4 Hard Milestones Triggering Price Changes

Prices never increase arbitrarily. Every step up is triggered by objective, observable business proof points:

Milestone 1: The "10 True Regulars" Activation (Stage 2)

In Progress (8/10 Joined)

Condition: 10 active community members attending Sunday cohorts and posting weekly in Skool.
Price Action: Keep Tier 2 at $1/mo and Tier 3 at $250/yr to maximize initial activation and qualitative feedback.

Milestone 2: First 50 Paid Tier 2 Members → Step to $5/mo

Intermediate Stepping Gate

Condition: Skool community reaches 50 members paying $1/mo.
Price Action: Close the $1/mo founding window; new Sit In members pay $5/mo (stepping stone to $10). Existing 50 members locked at $1 forever.

Milestone 3: The $10,000 Repeat Validation Gate (Stage 2 → 3 Exit)

Stage 3 Creation Trigger

Condition: 40 cumulative $250 cohort seats sold across 2 consecutive cohort launches ($10,000 gross).
Price Action:

  • Tier 2 Sit In: Permanently moves to $10/month (full BMC baseline).
  • Tier 3 Share Screen: Moves to $500/year (the upper bound of Stage 2 validation).

Milestone 4: $100k ARR Run-Rate & TA Delegation (Stage 4 Building)

Stage 4 Building Trigger

Condition: Business generates $100,000/year ARR run-rate (H11), hiring video editors, cohort coordinators, and dedicated Teaching Assistants for multi-cert tracks (Nvidia, Microsoft, Google, Claude).
Price Action: VIP Share Screen evolves into an Enterprise/Pro Bootcamp at $1,000/year ($99/mo or $1,000 annual) with weekday live lab coverage.
