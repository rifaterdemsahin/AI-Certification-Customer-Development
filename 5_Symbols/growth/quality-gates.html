Quality Gates by Stage

Explicit pass/fail numeric criteria for advancing between the four Customer Development stages. These are stage-level gates — for individual experiment-level thresholds, see Test & Metrics, and for the execution calendar behind them, see Test Plan.

🔍 Gate 1 → 2: Discovery → Validation (Dual Scorecard Alignment)

Do not progress to Customer Validation without meeting both halves of the Dual Scorecard (comp-mvp-separation.html):

🚦 Pass Criteria (Dual Scorecard)
1. Marketing Scorecard: ≥40% average watch retention on videos >3 mins (H10) AND ≥100 organic subscribers gathered without paid promotion.
2. Product Delivery Scorecard: ≥10 True Regulars in Skool (H27) and ≥5 live participants attending Sunday screen-share labs (H4).

🧪 In Testing

⛔ Kill Gate K1: £100k Pure-Time Pivot (Discovery stuck)

This is a stop-loss, not a pass gate. It is not the $100,000 USD ARR hiring trigger (H11). Founder-decided 2026-08-27: four courses of institutional value (£40,000) plus founder time (£60,000 at the £525/day IR35 rate ≈ 114 days) = £100,000 GBP. See £100k Pure-Time Pivot and H33.

🚦 Fail Criteria (AND)
The £100k ledger is full and Gate 1→2 Dual Scorecard is still uncleared and Skool is not charging back ($10/mo or $250–$500 seats; $0 Peek / $1 Sit In do not count). Then pivot the same week via When to Pivot — do not ship a fifth course.

📈 Gate 2 → 3: Validation → Creation

Customer Validation must independently produce real, paid revenue — not survey interest — before Customer Creation activity begins. This reconciles with the $10,000 figure already used in stage-customer-creation.html and stage-customer-validation.html.

🚦 Pass Criteria
$10,000 USD combined across 2 consecutive cohort launches (not a single launch), achieved solely by the founder (no hires) to keep the loop agile — see Validation Repeat Gate for why this was revised.

⚡ Gate 3 → 4: Creation → Building

Customer Creation is managed against subscriber-growth momentum, not a single pass/fail event, until the revenue threshold for hiring is reached.

🚦 Pass Criteria
1,000x subscriber growth per video trend sustained (baseline ~30 → target ~30,000) AND $100,000 annual run-rate reached.

📅 Planned

🏢 Gate 4: Company Building Steady-State

Company Building is considered stable once core operations are delegated without degrading student experience.

🚦 Pass Criteria
All 3 planned roles hired (video editor, cohort coordinator, technical TAs) AND cohort NPS remains >50 post-delegation.
