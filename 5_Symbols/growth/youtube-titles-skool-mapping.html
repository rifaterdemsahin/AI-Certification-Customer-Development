🎯 YouTube Titles → Skool Content Mapping

Every YouTube title is a promise, and every promise needs a landing spot. This page takes the headlines already drafted in Marketing Tactics plus a few new ones, and pins each one to the exact Skool destination it should route to — free community feed, the $10/mo membership Classroom, the $29 Exam Prep Bundle, or a $250–$500 live cohort seat — with the reasoning for why that title belongs at that tier, not another one.

  • Status: Planned — Skool destinations not yet live

  • Source Headlines: Marketing Tactics Swipe File

⚖️ Why This Mapping Matters

H4 and H7 already track two numerically unreconciled funnels pointed at the same set of viewers (view→membership vs. view→newsletter→cohort — see Funnel Math). A title that doesn't clearly imply where it sends the viewer next makes that reconciliation harder to measure, not easier — every video effectively becomes its own untracked experiment.

Pinning each title to one specific Skool destination up front does two things: it keeps the video's promise and its landing page honest with each other, and it gives every future video a labeled funnel stage so click-through and conversion can eventually be measured per-destination instead of as one blended number.

🧭 The Four Landing Destinations

Free

💬 Community Feed

Open forum + Sunday 9pm event calendar. No paywall, no signup required beyond joining the group.

$10/mo

🏫 Membership Classroom

Recorded cohort replays, ongoing access, drip-released lesson modules.

$29

📦 Exam Prep Bundle

Self-paced, one-time purchase: memory cards, prep exam, mock exam.

$250–$500

🎥 Live Cohort

Live, screen-share problem-solving, 3-month or yearly join, plus everything in Membership.

"This Sunday, 9PM. Free. For Now." 💬 Free Feed Names the real, already-running free weekly cohort (H5 Premise 4). The lowest-friction destination is the open event calendar, not a paid tier — the free cohort is the acquisition vehicle, not the monetized product.
"Stop Paying $2,000 For A Bootcamp That Doesn't Even Cover The New Claude Exam" 🏫 $10/mo Contrasts against a pricier one-off alternative to sell an ongoing, always-current subscription. The membership's recorded-replay format (H5 Premise 3) is the literal answer to "not up to date," so it belongs next to the recurring tier, not a one-time purchase.
"30 Subscribers To A Sold-Out Sunday Cohort — Steal The Exact Playbook" 🎥 $250–$500 Social proof about the live cohort belongs next to the live cohort's own purchase page, not the cheaper membership — it is specifically selling the screen-share, real-time format (H5 Premise 3), and pointing it at the membership would undersell what the title promises.
"3 Weeks Out From Your Pearson VUE Exam? Take This Mock Exam First" 📦 $29 Matches the real ~3-week booking pattern in H21 Premise 4 and the Target Audience mock-exam confidence loop. It times the CTA to the exact moment candidates are deciding, and points at the one bundle component (the mock exam) built to close that gap.
"The Cheat Sheet 200 Developers Used Before Their AI Interview — Free" 💬 Free Feed A free download belongs in the open forum, not a gated Classroom — it is a top-of-funnel lead magnet (one of H3's dual capture points), not a sale. Compliance note: "200 developers" is a placeholder per Marketing Tactics's own rule — swap for a verified number before this title ships.
"I Passed The Claude Associate Exam Through Meetups Alone — Here's What I'd Change" 📦 $29 Built on the real anonymized candidate in H5 Premise 7, who passed using official Anthropic materials, not our product. The honest hook is "here's the gap I'd have closed faster," pointing at the bundle's memory cards/prep exam — never implying our product produced their result.
"Which AI Certification Should You Get First: Claude, Nvidia, Microsoft, or Google?" 🚧 Not Yet Buildable Only makes sense once Skool's Classroom is segmented by certification track, which Skool LMS Integration's Stage 3 row already plans. Hold this title until that segmentation ships — publishing early with no destination would waste the click.
"Don't Buy Another AI Course Until You've Watched This" 💬 Free Feed A pre-purchase-decision title should route to a free diagnostic (Self-Assessment) rather than straight to checkout — it builds trust before any ask, matching the video's own "clarity before spending" premise.

⚠️ Sanity Check: Missing Questions & Risks

Missing Questions:

  • None of these title→destination pairings have been click-tested yet — H7's 10%/2% click/conversion targets remain unmeasured against any specific title.
  • Skool itself is still "Planned / Hypothesized" — none of the four destinations above exist as live pages yet, so this mapping is a design spec to build toward, not a set of working links today.

Key Risks:

  • A title that overpromises relative to its destination (e.g. a proof-angle title landing on a paywall with no proof visible) risks the same trust cost flagged in Marketing Tactics's compliance rule.
  • The multi-certification title depends on Classroom segmentation that hasn't shipped — publishing it early would send traffic to a destination that doesn't exist yet.
