📌 The Core Architecture at a Glance

📺 YouTube (Public Discovery)

Role: Top-of-funnel reach, SEO search capture, animated visual courses, and high-trust authority building.

Payment Status: Does NOT handle course/cohort billing. Monetization via YouTube is avoided to prevent 30% cuts and split community attention.

💼 LinkedIn (Professional Graph)

Role: B2B trust, certification social proof loops, contractor networking, and the Delivery Pilot Organization Page (actorCompanyId=26187296).

Payment Status: Does NOT process learner payments. Rifat's 30k connection limit is preserved strictly for verified Skool community members.

🏫 Skool (Classroom & Billing)

Role: Stage 2 validation delivery engine, Classroom video replays, Sunday 9 PM live calendar, gamified peer forum, and native checkout.

Payment Status: Processes all subscription & cohort payments via integrated Stripe Connect directly into Delivery Pilot.

💳 Which Organisation Does the Payments?

🏢 The Payment Settlement Entity: Delivery Pilot

All customer, membership, cohort, and corporate training payments are collected, invoiced, and legally settled under the founder's commercial venture: Delivery Pilot.

💳 Skool / Individual Student Payments
  • ⚙️Payment Engine: Skool integrates natively with Stripe Connect.
  • 🏦Merchant Account: The Stripe merchant account is directly owned and controlled by Delivery Pilot.
  • 💵Settlement: Member transactions ($1/mo Sit In, $10/mo membership, $250/yr VIP Share Screen) process through Stripe and payout directly to Delivery Pilot's business bank account.
🏢 B2B / Corporate Training Invoicing
  • 📄Invoicing Entity: Invoices for enterprise cohort seats and corporate pilots (e.g. Marianna pilot, consulting firm bulk seats) are issued by Delivery Pilot.
  • 🏛️Direct Bank Transfer: B2B wire transfers (BACs / IBAN) settle directly into Delivery Pilot's corporate treasury.
  • 🚫No Third-Party Intermediaries: Neither YouTube LLC, Google, nor LinkedIn/Microsoft act as billing intermediaries.

🛡️ Strategic Guardrails

Mandatory Best Practices

  • ✅Centralized Checkout: Direct 100% of consumer transactions through Skool/Stripe to keep customer records unified in Delivery Pilot.
  • ✅Evergreen Hub Posts: Update central Skool living hubs rather than spamming the board with announcement threads.
  • ✅Connection Buffer: Restrict Rifat's personal LinkedIn invitations to verified Skool members to stay under the 30k limit.

Prohibited Pitfalls

  • ❌Never split payments across platforms: Avoid enabling YouTube Channel Memberships or third-party Gumroad links that bypass Delivery Pilot's Skool hub.
  • ❌Never treat Skool as top-of-funnel: Do not rely on cold board posts to find students; discovery happens on YouTube and LinkedIn.
  • ❌Never accept non-community LinkedIn invites: Protect founder network bandwidth for paying and active Delivery Pilot members.
