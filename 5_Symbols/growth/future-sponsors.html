🚦 When Do We Accept Sponsors? (Stage-Gating Strategy)

🛑 Stages 1 & 2: Zero Sponsorships (Active Policy)

During Customer Discovery and Customer Validation, external sponsorships are strictly prohibited.

  • 🛡️Protects Objective Discovery: Course reviews and architectural advice must remain 100% unbiased.
  • 🎯Focus on the $10k Gate: Rifat's bandwidth must be focused on validating organic student willingness to pay (H5, H9), not selling ad slots.
  • 📉Low Early Leverage: Small view counts yield trivial sponsorship revenue ($50–$100) while polluting video trust.

⚡ Stages 3 & 4: Selective High-Value Partners

Sponsorships unlock once the business transitions to Customer Creation and Company Building.

  • 📈Subscriber Scale: Requires passing the 1,000x growth milestone (~30,000 YouTube subscribers per H13).
  • 👥Community Density: 500+ active Skool members and proven contractor placement alumni (H30).
  • 💰Enterprise Rates: Capped, premium packages (£1,500–£5,000/integration) targeting high-value enterprise AI tools.

🛡️ Non-Negotiable Sponsorship Guardrails

Strict Editorial Independence

No sponsor can purchase a guaranteed passing score endorsement or dictate curriculum content.

  • 🚫Zero Pay-to-Pass: We do not promote cheating engines, exam brain dumps, or unverified shortcut software.
  • 🔍Hands-On Founder Test: Rifat Erdem Sahin must personally test and approve the tool before accepting any sponsorship.

Full Regulatory Disclosure

Every paid placement adheres strictly to ASA (UK) and FTC (US) advertising disclosure regulations.

  • 📢Clear Tagging: Clear visual and verbal `#ad` / `Sponsored` tag displayed in the first 5 seconds.
  • 💳Direct Settlement: Invoicing and contracts are settled directly via Delivery Pilot (Payment Architecture).
