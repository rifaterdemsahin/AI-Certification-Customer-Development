💵 Unit Economics

Per-customer economics for both revenue lines, priced against the founder's real opportunity cost as an inside-IR35 contractor at £525/day. This is the per-unit companion to Cost-Side Model, which covers the business-level cost stack.

🧑‍💻 The Founder's Time Benchmark

  • Day rate:£525/day, inside IR35
  • Implied hourly (8hr day):£65.63/hr
  • Illustrative USD:~$83/hr (at an illustrative $1.27/£ rate — confirm the live FX rate before using this for real decisions)

This isn't lost contracting income directly — content and cohort work happen in contract-free hours (see Single-Founder Bandwidth), not during paid contract time. But it's the best available number for what an hour of the founder's skilled time is worth, so it's used throughout this page as the opportunity-cost yardstick against which cohort and membership pricing is measured.

⭐ Cohort Seat: $250 / 8 Weeks

The $250 tier buys 8 weeks of live, 1-on-1 supported sessions (reviews and feedback included), delivered in the existing weekly Sunday 9–11pm slot.

The $16/hr live-delivery rate holds per seat, but the same 2-hour Sunday block is shared across every attendee in the cohort — with 4 students in the room (the current average, per Evidence Map), that's 4 × $250 = $1,000 for 16 hours, or ~$62.50/hr, close to the founder's own benchmark. Below ~4 concurrent students, the founder is pricing well under his own time's worth; this doesn't count the unpaid review/feedback time between sessions, which isn't tracked anywhere yet.

👥 Membership: $10/mo

💰 Costs Per Unit

  • 🤖AI infrastructure & inference: $100/month, fixed — not allocated per seat or per member, see Cost-Side Model for the break-even math against this fixed cost.
  • 📺Distribution/hosting: $0 — YouTube is the platform of record for course delivery, so there's no separate LMS or video-hosting bill.
  • 💳Payment processing fees: not itemized anywhere in the repo yet — a small, real percentage of every $10/mo and $250 transaction that should be priced in before treating gross revenue as margin.
  • 🎯CAC: $0 — acquisition is organic YouTube/LinkedIn by design (see Focus and When to Start Paid Advertisements), so the only real cost per unit is the founder's delivery time above.
