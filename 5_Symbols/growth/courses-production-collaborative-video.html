🎬 Courses Production: Collaborative Video Building

Live Skool living hub (posted 2026-09-05) that turns members from video consumers into co-producers. The post is honest about building the engine in public, then routes each person to one of three doors: official Anthropic Claude 101, the unofficial Skool Exam Prep Classroom, or Canva comment review of course videos. This is an activation and quality loop, not a paid-enrollment signal (see H5). Nurture it in place as a Courses hub — do not spawn a new announcement thread each week (anti-spam guardrail).

🧭 What The Tactic Is

Most tactics in this catalog convert a viewer into a member. This one converts a member into a production partner. The ask is not “join Skool.” The ask is: watch the course videos in Canva, comment on process and craft, and help shape the next cut. That is H34 (collaborative video production) — H29 (listen more than you speak) applied to the animation pipeline (H2 / H10), using the same living-hub pattern already specified on Skool Static Posts.

It also does a second job: it disambiguates official vs unofficial. Claude 101 is Anthropic’s course. Delivery Pilot is exam-prep community practice. Mixing those two is how people think we issue the cert. The post keeps the doors separate on purpose. Exam seating stays partner-gated (F12) — this hub does not claim otherwise.

🚪 The Three Doors

One post, three jobs. Members self-select. Do not collapse them into a single CTA.

🎓 Door 1 — Official Basics

Send beginners to Anthropic’s Claude 101 (Claude Academy). 13 lessons, ~2.5h, first conversation through Projects, Artifacts, Skills, Connectors. This is the vendor source of truth — not our Classroom, not a pass guarantee.

🔥 Door 2 — Unofficial Exam Prep

Skool Exam Prep Classroom for people who want to learn, practice, and build with the crew. Maps to Learning Objectives and the $10/mo + $250–$500 revenue model later. A Classroom click is not a paid enrollment.

🎬 Door 3 — Canva Co-Review

Course videos live in Canva. Members comment in Canva on two lenses: operational (process, mechanics, execution) and artistic (flow, visual impact, engagement). That comment stream is the production backlog, not a YouTube like count.

🎨 Dual-Lens Review Rubric

The post asks for both an operational and an artistic read. Without a rubric that request becomes noise. Use this as the pinned comment under the hub and as the Canva comment prompt.

⚙️ Operational lens

  • 1Does the cut teach one Learning Objective, or does it dump three?
  • 2Can a new member reproduce the mechanic without Sunday hand-holding?
  • 3Where does setup friction start (install, API key, MCP, Cowork)? Flag the minute.
  • 4Is the Key Result named on-screen (artifact, prompt, repo)?

🎭 Artistic lens

  • 1Does the first 3 seconds interrupt, or does it start with a title card?
  • 2Flow: where did attention drop? Name the beat, not “make it nicer.”
  • 3Visual impact: is the metaphor doing work, or is it decoration? (Bora: analogies beat infographics.)
  • 4Does the viewer feel smart after, or stupid? (Marianna first-principles bar.)

🔬 Analysis: What Works, What To Watch

Why this is a real tactic

  • Build-in-public without the con tell. Mehmet & Halil flagged unverifiable income claims and “he’s just selling courses.” This post sells collaboration, not a dollar-per-agent fantasy. Honest unfinishedness is the opposite of that reel.
  • Cocreation is already validated qualitatively. Marianna offered a Practical Workshops Programme; anonymous member praise was for growing together, not lectures. Door 3 operationalizes that on the actual video files.
  • Self-driven learning + dual lens matches the flipped-classroom ask (2–4 min primers before Sunday) and the H10 quality floor: comments name the drop-off beat instead of waiting for Studio AVD.
  • Three-door routing reduces support load. Beginners go to Claude 101; exam people go to Classroom; producers go to Canva. One founder does not have to diagnose intent in DMs.
  • Evergreen hub, not a blast. Fits the Courses living-hub rule. Update the post and comment stream; do not create a new “please review video 7” thread every drop.

Risks and how to cap them

  • Canva-account friction. Commenting requires a Canva login. Put the share link (commenter access, not editor) in the hub. If people bounce, take one clip into Skool Classroom comments as a fallback — do not require everyone to become a designer.
  • Unbounded review is founder-time. Batch Canva comments in the same weekly cycle as YouTube/LinkedIn (second-job constraint). Ship one credited change per month from named comments (H29 target). Do not reply in real time.
  • “Unofficial” can still confuse exam access. Keep F12 visible: we do not seat Pearson VUE. Partners do. Claude 101 does not equal Claude Certified Architect.
  • Not paid conversion. Classroom clicks, Canva comments, and $0/$1 joins do not count toward H5 Premise 3 or the $10k gate.
  • Emoji-dense copy vs Cheuk’s AI-post fatigue. Fine for an in-community hub. Do not paste this block as a LinkedIn/YouTube caption. Face-to-face and founder-voice CTAs stay cleaner.

📏 What To Measure (Not Paid)

Canva comments / week

Count dual-lens comments, not emoji reactions. Target: ≥ 3 named operational or artistic notes per week.

Credited ships / month

H29 already requires ≥ 1 audience-originated change credited by first name. Log the Canva comment → recut here.

Door mix

Claude 101 clicks vs Classroom clicks vs Canva comments. If 100% Door 1, the hub is a link dump, not co-production.

None of these numbers is $10/mo, $250 Share Screen, or a Pearson VUE seat. Status on H5 / H8 / H9 does not move from this post alone.
