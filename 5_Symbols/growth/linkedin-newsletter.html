Why We Moved Our AI Cloud Practice to Skool: Building the Delivery Pilot Community

"Tutorials give you knowledge; live community build rooms give you confidence. To thrive as an AI engineer or contractor in 2026, you don't need another theoretical video playlist — you need hands-on reps with real production tools."

Over the past few months of running weekly certification masterclasses and building animated AI cloud courses on YouTube, one pattern became overwhelmingly clear: watching someone else build in Claude or deploy an MCP server is not enough.

Engineers and architects were asking the same core questions in comments and DMs: "Where do I ask questions when my local database fails?", "How do I turn my chats into a structured knowledge base?", and "How do I position these skills to recruiters for high-rate contracts?"

That’s why we launched Delivery Pilot on Skool — a dedicated, zero-noise interactive home for cloud engineers, AI builders, and IT contractors.

🚀 Why Skool? The Zero-Friction Community Experience

Traditional social platforms and Discord channels are noisy, disorganized, and filled with distraction. We chose Skool because it combines three vital ingredients in one unified interface:

  • Evergreen Community Board: Structured discussion channels where discussions stay organized into living knowledge hubs (Courses, Glossaries, Environment Setups, Second Brain workflows) rather than vanishing into endless scrolling chat streams.
  • Integrated Classroom Modules: Step-by-step video replays, sample code repositories, and certification question banks readily accessible in sequence.
  • Live Calendar & Build Workshops: Direct access to Sunday live practice labs (13:30 UK) and VIP Claude Cowork sessions without juggling external meeting links.
  • Gamification & Leveling: Members earn points and level up by helping peers and sharing technical breakthroughs, unlocking advanced architecture blueprints organically.

🛠️ What Happens Inside Delivery Pilot?

Delivery Pilot is not a passive broadcast feed; it’s an active lab where members progress along a proven 4-stage transformation pipeline:

The 4-Stage Delivery Pilot Pipeline:

  1. Hands-on Environment Training: Setting up local MCP servers, Qdrant vector databases, and Obsidian knowledge vaults.
  2. Vendor Certification Mastery: Systematically preparing for prestigious credentials like the Claude Certified Architect – Foundations & Professional (issued by Anthropic via Pearson VUE).
  3. Portfolio & Recruiter Promotion: Optimizing LinkedIn positioning and participating in Monday VIP Claude Cowork sessions to present pre-vetted capabilities directly to tech recruiters.
  4. Independent Contractor Collective (£500–£1,000+/day): Co-bidding on enterprise tenders and client engagements as a trusted alliance of Forward Deployed Engineers (H30).

🌱 A Community Built by Builders, for Builders

A community thrives on the discoveries of its members. Recently, member Sude documented how she turned raw Claude conversations into an Obsidian Second Brain vault for certification revision — sparking vibrant discussions and peer improvements across the board.

Whether you are an aspiring AI architect looking to clear your first credential, or a seasoned contractor aiming to command top-tier day rates, Delivery Pilot gives you the room, the tools, and the peers to get there.

🎟️ How to Join the Community

We maintain an accessible freemium onboarding path with clear, transparent tiers:

  • Standard ($0 / Peek): Join the discussion board, access starter workshops, and participate in community threads.
  • Premium ($1/mo / Sit In): Unlock classroom replays, question banks, and join Sunday live practice rooms.
  • VIP ($250/year / Share Screen): Dedicated screen-share troubleshooting on Sundays, Monday VIP Claude Cowork access, and direct recruiter promotion over the Delivery Pilot network.
