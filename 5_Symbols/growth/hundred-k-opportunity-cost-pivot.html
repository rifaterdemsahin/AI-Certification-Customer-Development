🚦 When the ledger fires

The milestone is an AND gate. Filling £100k is not automatic failure. Filling £100k while still stuck is.

Stay the course (do not pivot)
Dual Scorecard Gate 1 → 2 has cleared (Quality Gates: ≥40% retention + ≥100 organic subs and ≥10 true regulars + ≥5 Sunday live) and Skool is charging back real cash — $10/mo membership and/or $250–$500 cohort seats, not $0 Peek or $1 Sit In preview joins. $0 and $1 are not paid enrollments.
Pivot (this milestone)
The £100k ledger is full and the business cannot turn Discovery into Validation and Skool cannot charge the investment back (no paid $10/mo or $250–$500 enrollments that recoup). Then stop treating this as a validation-in-waiting. Run the vectors on When to Pivot (B2B licensing, async masterclass, contractor tooling) instead of a fifth course.

🧮 Charge-back test on Skool

“Charge back over the school” means Skool billing is returning cash against the £100k ledger. Preview tiers do not count. The $10,000 USD Stage 2 → 3 exit (H9) is the success revenue gate; H33 is the stop-loss if that path never starts.
