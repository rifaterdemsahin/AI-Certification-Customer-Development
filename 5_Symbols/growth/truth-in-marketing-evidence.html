⚖️ Truth in Marketing: What Data We Promote vs. Protect

Most tech courses sell marketing illusions and fake screenshots. In our ecosystem, we only publish verifiable empirical truth. This page outlines the transparent framework of what data people put in, what data people take out, and what verified evidence we promote publicly while strictly protecting candidate confidentiality (see H5, H8, H29, and H30).

📊 The Input / Output / Published Truth Architecture

📥 1. What Members Put In (Inputs)

The honest raw investment members provide into the community and training sandbox:

  • Raw Time & Attendance: Showing up to Sunday live labs (1pm UK) and weekly VIP coworking sessions.
  • Curriculum Surveys: Honest answers regarding AI skill gaps (MCP, RAG, Claude Code, IR35).
  • Code Commits & Pull Requests: Real repository implementations submitted to GitHub.
  • Real Exam Failure / Pass Scores: Sharing unvarnished diagnostic score reports (e.g. Jamal 644/1000 diagnostic).
  • Career Frustrations & Burnout: Frank feedback on corporate redundancy and contractor rate stagnation.

📤 2. What Members Take Out (Outputs)

The tangible career and technical capital members extract from the ecosystem:

  • Official Proctored Certifications: Claude Certified Architect, Azure, M365 Copilot Credly badges.
  • Production Second Brain Architecture: Operational local & cloud agentic workflows.
  • Optimized Recruiter CV & LinkedIn: Delivery Pilot organization backing and warm recruiter intros.
  • £500–£1,000+/Day Contractor Strategy: Ltd company structuring, IR35 navigation, and enterprise B2B bidding.
  • Lifelong Contractor Alliance: Sub-contracting and RFP co-bidding network.

📢 3. What We Publicly Promote (The Empirical Truth)

We never publish fabricated marketing hype, fake earnings claims, or stock photo testimonials. We publish verifiable artifacts:

Verifiable Credly Badges

Publicly provable Pearson VUE proctored passes with candidate consent.

Open Source Code Repos

Public GitHub PRs, Claude CLI MCP tools, and live running architecture demos.

Diagnostic Failure Data

Real breakdowns of missed domains and practice score improvements (e.g. Jamal 644/1000 → Pass).

🔒 4. What We Strictly Protect & Anonymize (Confidentiality Guardrail)

Trust is our foundation. Under no circumstances do we publicize sensitive personal or corporate data without explicit consent:

  • Candidate Anonymization: Cohort members like B. (who provided burnout/redundancy video discovery) are strictly referenced by initial only.
  • Current Employer Proprietary IP: No internal enterprise codebases or client-confidential schemas are ever recorded or broadcast.
  • Contractor Rate Negotiation Details: Specific member company names and private invoices remain strictly confidential.
