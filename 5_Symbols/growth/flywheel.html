Growth Flywheel

How content compounds into a self-reinforcing growth loop, and the recurring "prospecting calendar" that keeps the flywheel turning every week. Complements Sales Pipeline, which covers the linear conversion path.

🔄 The Loop

Why It Compounds

Each weekly video adds to a growing catalog that ranks for more search terms (audience). Engaged viewers who subscribe to the newsletter or membership build trust in the teaching style. A share of that trusted audience converts into a paid cohort. Cohort graduates who pass produce testimonials and shared LinkedIn badges — social proof that markets the next round of content for free, feeding back into reach.

Unlike a paid-ads growth model, each cycle lowers future acquisition cost rather than raising it, because the catalog and social proof are owned assets that keep working after they're published.

🗓️ The Prospecting Calendar

A fixed recurring cadence is what actually turns the flywheel — without a schedule, "content compounds" is just a slogan. This is the concrete commitment (see also the founder's weekly routine in Customer Creation):

Cadence Activity Flywheel Stage Fed
Weekly One new animated YouTube lesson published (Saturday production block) 🎬 Content → 👥 Audience
Biweekly Newsletter digest: new lessons, exam-blueprint updates, cohort dates 👥 Audience → 🤝 Trust
Monthly Live Q&A / webinar open to the free community (Sunday cohort-support slot) 🤝 Trust → 💳 Paid Cohort
Quarterly Cohort launch window (4–8 week bootcamp, $250–$500) opens and closes 💳 Paid Cohort → ⭐ Testimonials
