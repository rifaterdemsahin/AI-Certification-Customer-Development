🧮 Funnel Math: Views Needed → $10,000

Acidity check Finding F5: comp-funnel.html (H7: 10% video→newsletter CTR, 2% newsletter→cohort) and comp-business-model.html (H4: 1% of views→$10/mo membership) describe two different funnels to two different offers, and neither was ever worked backward into "views needed to hit the $10k gate." This page does that arithmetic explicitly.

🎯 The Target

The Stage 2→3 exit gate (H9, see Stage 3) is $10,000 gross from a cohort launch. At the stated $250 cohort price point, that's 40 paying seats in one launch window (or 20 seats at the $500 end of the range — this page uses 40 × $250 as the worked example throughout).

📣 Path A — The Cohort Funnel (H7)

Working backward from 40 cohort buyers through H7's stated conversion rates:

40
Cohort buyers needed
2,000
Newsletter subs needed (40 ÷ 2%)
20,000
Video views needed (2,000 ÷ 10%)

Result: ~20,000 total video views, spread across the catalog, are needed to fill a 40-seat cohort at H7's stated rates.

The 2,000 "newsletter subs needed" figure is the combined total across both live signup entry points — the LinkedIn-linked and YouTube-linked capture forms both feed the same list (see Funnel & Channels) — not 2,000 from a single source.

👥 Path B — The Membership Funnel (H4)

H4 converts views directly into $10/mo members, not cohort seats — a different offer with recurring, not lump-sum, revenue. Per Unit Economics, a member who stays 10 months is worth $100 lifetime. To reach $10,000 through membership revenue alone:

100
10-month members needed (100 × $100 = $10,000)
10,000
Video views needed (100 ÷ 1%)
~10 months
Time for that $10,000 to fully accrue
⚠️ Why Path A and Path B aren't interchangeable
Path A produces $10,000 in a single launch window; Path B produces the same $10,000 only after ~10 months of retained subscriptions. Treating H4 as an alternate route to the same gate (as the site implicitly does today) confuses a lump-sum revenue test with a slow-drip retention test — they answer different questions. The $10k gate, as currently written, is effectively a cohort-launch gate (Path A), not a membership gate, unless it's explicitly redefined as cumulative revenue over a stated window.

📊 Sensitivity: How Much the Funnel Assumptions Matter

Path A's 20,000-view estimate is only as good as the 10%/2% assumptions behind it. Nothing in the repo has measured either rate yet (both H4 and H7 are still Hypothesized/In Testing). Varying them shows the real range of uncertainty:

Scenario View→Newsletter CTR Newsletter→Cohort Conv. Views needed for 40 seats
Conservative 5% 1% 80,000
Stated in H7 (base case) 10% 2% 20,000
Optimistic 15% 3% ~8,900

The real requirement could be anywhere from ~9,000 to 80,000 views depending on which assumption holds — an 8x range. Worth noting: the H13 subscriber-growth target (baseline ~30 → ~30,000, see Hypothesis Tracker) is the same order of magnitude as the base-case 20,000-view figure, which is a useful cross-check once real view/subscriber data exists.

  • 🔀Decide explicitly whether H4 (membership) counts toward the $10k gate at all, or whether the gate is Path A (cohort) only — see Validation Repeat Gate for the revised gate definition.
