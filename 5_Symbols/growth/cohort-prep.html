🧰 Cohort Prep — The Week Before Sunday

The cohort's value isn't just the live Sunday session — it's a full week. Between sessions, the founder shares what needs to be installed and what's coming up in the next cohort over WhatsApp and Discord, so attendees arrive already set up and can spend the live time actually building, not troubleshooting installs.

🎓 The Delivery Pilot Program

This weekly prep loop is one piece of the Delivery Pilot Program — the product itself. People don't just watch a video; they join and become part of a community that follows a structured program together to close their skills gaps in the age of AI (see H30 and the public slogan on Get Certified in AI. Shape the Future.). Cohort Prep is the connective tissue between live sessions that keeps that community moving through the program, not a standalone perk.

🗓️ What Happens During the Week

  • 🛠️Install list, posted mid-week: the exact tools, SDKs, CLI, API keys, and accounts needed for that Sunday's topic — so setup happens on attendees' own time, not during the two live hours.
  • 📋Upcoming content preview: a short heads-up on what the next session will cover, so people can skim relevant docs and show up with context instead of cold.
  • 💬Channel: WhatsApp (the same group already used to market the free Sunday cohort — see H5) and/or Discord for attendees who prefer it. Migration note (2026-08-12): as Skool becomes the lab home, dual-post the same install list to WhatsApp and the Skool board for 2–4 weeks so warm people land inside Skool without losing the existing prep habit — see Skool Founder Posting Sanity Check.
  • ✅Reminder cadence: posted once mid-week and again roughly 24 hours before the Sunday 13:30 UK session.
  • 🖐️Result: the live session is spent hands-on — implementing, debugging, and solving real problems together — not on environment setup.
  • 🎥After Sunday: the 2-hour recording lands in Skool Classroom the same night with timestamps. That is the $10/mo replay, not a recut course and not a public YouTube video — see 2-Hour Workshop Upload.

🎯 Why This Matters

🖐️ Hands-on time is protected
Every minute spent fixing a broken install during the live session is a minute not spent on the real problem-solving that's the actual value prop (see Component: Business Model).
🤝 The community is active all week, not 2 hours
Extends the peer-accountability signal already documented for H8 — WhatsApp/Discord activity between sessions is itself evidence people are engaged beyond the live slot.
📈 Builds toward the paid cohort
Same free-tier channel mechanics (Sunday 13:30 UK, $0-spend WhatsApp) already tracked for H5 — this weekly prep habit is part of what the founder reports builds content, collects feedback, and increases his confidence ahead of a paid launch.
🛠️ Matches the Cohort Platform requirements
The "community space for peer accountability between sessions" already listed as a Must-Have in Requirements is exactly this weekly WhatsApp/Discord prep loop, not just a session-time chat window.
⚠️ Setup friction is a real barrier
Field evidence (Brian's feedback, 2026-08-10) confirms time-constrained professionals struggle to complete installs on time, even when attending repeatedly (his 3rd cohort). This means some students will attend to listen/absorb first, pacing their setup slower — see H5.

🏅 Badges

Proposed · See H35

Build more, climb the ranks

Every badge is earned the same way: ship repos. The climb starts easy and gets exponentially harder — each rank roughly doubles (and then some) the repo count of the one before it, ending at 1000 repos for the top rank.

Level Rank Repos Unlocks % of Members
🛫 Level 1 Cadet / Explorer 1 repo Certified Architect Professional 88%
⚡ Level 2 Active Contributor 2 repos Exam Prep 6%
🧭 Level 3 Study Regular 6 repos — 3%
🛠️ Level 4 Flight Engineer 13 repos — 3%
💼 Level 5 Senior Practitioner 32 repos — 0%
🎯 Level 6 Mission Specialist 75 repos — 0%
🛡️ Level 7 Squadron Leader 178 repos — 0%
🧑‍✈️ Level 8 Pilot Graduate 422 repos — 0%
🌟 Level 9 Delivery Rainmaker 1000 repos — 0%
🛫➡️🌟 Every rank starts with one repo
Ship something small this week, log it in your recap, and watch the badge count climb.

Why repo count first: this ladder exists to get members showcasing their skills with real, shipped repos — not slide decks or theory. Start with the count (did they ship?), then check whether it works, then raise the bar on quality as the program and the community help each other motivate and build together.
