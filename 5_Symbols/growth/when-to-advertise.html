🚦 When to Start Paid Advertisements

The default answer is do not advertise. This page is the timing gate for H16 — it says when paid spend is allowed and what to do instead until then. The channel catalog and CAC/LTV check live on Paid Advertisement. Organic YouTube and LinkedIn stay the primary acquisition model through Customer Validation (see Organic Growth and Focus).

Do not buy ads. The business is still in Discovery / early Validation.

The $10,000 / 2-launch Stage 2 gate (H9) is not cleared. Organic funnel rates (H4, H7) are not yet a measured CAC benchmark. Paid spend now would hide whether the content and the offer actually work.

🗺️ The Timing Decision

🚫 Do Not Advertise When

Paid ads are a scale lever, not a discovery tool. Any of the conditions below is enough to keep spend at zero. Steve Blank's rule is the same one on Focus: do not scale a funnel you have not yet proven.

The offer is not yet selling itself
If organic viewers will not join the Sunday cohort, buy the $10/mo membership, or pick up the $29 bundle, buying strangers will not fix that. Prove conversion on Funnel Math first (H4, H7).
The $10k / 2-launch gate is still open
Customer Validation must produce $10,000 from two consecutive paid launches before Customer Creation activity begins. See Quality Gates and Validation Repeat Gate (H9).
Content quality is still the variable
$0 ad spend exists so watch retention and engagement can be tested in isolation. If average guide retention is below the >40% floor (H10), fix the videos on Content Analysis and YouTube Channel Metrics instead of amplifying them.
The landing and the offer are not ready
A paid click needs a destination. Until YouTube titles map to a live Skool destination (Titles → Skool) and the handoff exists (YouTube → Skool Handoff), ads would dump traffic into an unfinished path.
Founder bandwidth is already maxed
This is a second job. Comments are batch-answered weekly. Paid traffic creates support load the founder cannot absorb yet. See Single-Founder Bandwidth. Hire capacity only after the $100k ARR trigger (H11).
Ads are being used to feel like growth
Impressions, clicks, and a busy Ads Manager are not evidence. The flywheel is content → retention → subscribers → members → cohort stories on LinkedIn. See Flywheel. If that loop is not turning, paid spend only buys a louder silence.

✅ What to Do Instead of Advertising

Until the three gates on the diagram all clear, every hour goes into proving organic demand. This is the work that later makes a paid test honest — it produces the CAC benchmark ads would have to beat.

1. Publish organic content

Animated shorts and long-form courses on YouTube; professional posts on LinkedIn. Placement rules are on Short vs Long Form. Growth mechanics are on Organic Growth.

2. Run the Sunday cohort

The live session, Skool Classroom replay, and weekly prep loop are the product being validated — not an ad campaign. See Cohort Prep and 2-Hour Workshop Upload.

3. Write organic titles, not ads

Use the headline swipe file on Marketing Tactics for YouTube thumbnails and LinkedIn posts. Map each title to a Skool destination on Titles → Skool.

4. Measure the funnel

Views → click-through → membership / cohort. Without those rates there is no organic CAC to compare a paid channel against. See Funnel Math, Sales Pipeline, and Test & Metrics.

5. Meet people in person

Thursday Venture Coffee, Cambridge meetups, hallway openers. Discovery interviews and word-of-mouth are cheaper and more informative than a first ad set. See Watering Holes and Conversation Starters.

6. Price the unit, not the ad

Know what one member and one cohort seat are worth against the founder's time before anyone bids on a keyword. See Unit Economics and Cost-Side Model.

🟢 When It Does Make Sense to Advertise

Paid ads become an option in Customer Creation (Stage 3), never a default. All of the following must be true at once. One missing row means stay at $0. This is H16.

Must be true Why it matters Where it is tracked
$10,000 from 2 consecutive paid launches Proves people pay without ads. One lucky launch is not enough. Quality Gates · Repeat Gate · H9
Organic funnel numbers exist Paid CAC needs a real organic benchmark to beat. Hypothesized CTR is not a benchmark. Funnel Math · H4 · H7
A capped test shows CAC below LTV, with margin Must sit under the $10/mo membership plus $250–$500 cohort value, after founder time cost. Unit Economics · Paid Advertisement
Organic is still growing and stays primary Ads only supplement YouTube and LinkedIn. They never replace the flywheel. Organic Growth · BMC: Channels
One channel, one kill switch, no standing budget Start by extending a proven organic channel (YouTube or LinkedIn), not a brand-new paid-only venue. Stop if the test window fails. Paid Advertisement · Milestone Calendar
The founder can operate the extra demand Comments, onboarding, and cohort seats still run in weekly cycles around a contract job. Bandwidth · Stage 3

📣 If the Gates Clear

Then — and only then — open Paid Advertisement and run the sustainability check on a single candidate channel. Prefer the channel that already works organically:

  • 📺YouTube Ads first if search and watch-time already convert. Discovery / in-stream extends the existing course channel, not a new audience.
  • 💼LinkedIn Ads second if the founder's professional audience is already producing cohort seats. Sponsored content extends a known ICP, not cold display.
  • 🔍Google Search only after those two — high-intent exam-prep terms, never broad brand awareness.
  • 🚫Skip Reddit, X, newsletters, and podcasts on the first test. Those are paid-only venues with no organic baseline on this site. They wait until a proven channel has already passed the CAC bar.

The Stage 3 job after the gate is still 1,000x subscriber growth per video (H13), not ad-spend share of voice. Ads are a supplement if the math holds. See Customer Creation.
