🚫 Lead Qualification: The Hormozi Anti-Admittance Funnel

Do not let everyone in. As Alex Hormozi teaches in $100M Offers and $100M Leads, value is inversely proportional to accessibility. When admission is guaranteed, commitment is zero. To build a world-class cohort of Delivery Pilots (£500–£1,000+/day contractors), we implement aggressive qualification filters and disqualify low-commitment spectators before they dilute the room (see H5 and H30).

🎯 Hypothesis H5 🛡️ Anti-Spam Gate 💎 True Regulars Filter 🧑‍✈️ Delivery Pilot Standard
“If you want people to value what you have, make it hard to get. The easiest way to increase perceived value and client compliance is to disqualify 80% of applicants. Tell them who this is NOT for, and enforce it ruthlessly.” — Alex Hormozi

🛑 The 4-Tier Qualification Filter Stack

Tier 1: Intake Survey Mandatory Gate (Skool Questions)

Gate 1

Anyone who clicks "Join" on Skool must answer 3 non-negotiable questions. If answers are generic, blank, or generated with zero skin-in-the-game ("just looking", "crypto", "free AI tips"), the membership request is instantly declined.

❌ Instant Rejection Triggers:
  • Blank or 1-word answers ("AI", "Dev", "Hi").
  • Crypto / dropshipping / spam promotional intent.
  • Unwillingness to dedicate minimum 3 hours/week.
  • Looking for a magic "make money overnight without coding" button.
✅ Admitted Profiles:
  • Identifies current tech role (DevOps, Cloud, Software, Solution Architect).
  • States concrete exam goal (Claude Architect / AWS / Copilot).
  • Expresses appetite for live hands-on engineering lab sessions.
  • Agrees to the community covenant and peer-learning etiquette.

Tier 2: Skool Level 2 Anti-Spam Posting Restriction

Gate 2

New members cannot create top-level discussion threads on Day 1. They must earn Level 2 by liking, commenting thoughtfully on existing hub posts, and completing the orientation lesson. See Skool Level 2 Posting Restriction.

Tier 3: Sunday Live Cohort Attendance & Camera Proof

Gate 3

Spectators are weeded out during the live Sunday 1pm–2pm lab. To move up the ladder towards VIP contractor matching, members must show up live, contribute code or architecture insights, and actively engage in the 10-10-15 breakout structure.

Tier 4: Level 8/9 Delivery Pilot Graduation Gate

Gate 4

Only members who reach Skool Level 8 (by completing practical repos, mock exams, and verified proctored certification) are invited into Delivery Pilot status to co-bid on enterprise contracts, participate in Monday VIP Recruiter Coworks, and step into Level 9 Alumni Mentorship.
