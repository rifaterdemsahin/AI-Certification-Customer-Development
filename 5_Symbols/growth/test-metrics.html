🎯 Fix 1: Dual Scorecard Framework (Marketing vs. Delivery)

Relying solely on >40% retention on short clips creates false validation. The Dual Scorecard decouples channel acquisition from live delivery mastery:

📣 Marketing (Channel Scorecard):
  • Watch Retention > 40% on videos > 3 mins (H10)
  • Skool Profile/Join Page CTR > 3%
  • Top-of-funnel reach > 1,000 views per video
🚀 Delivery (Product Scorecard):
  • Weekly Active Members in Skool (≥10 True Regulars, H27)
  • Sunday Live Screen-Share Attendance (≥5 active participants, H4)
  • Paid conversions ($1 Sit-In → $250 VIP Share Screen, H5)

🛠️ 5 Strategic Fixes Implementation Reference

  • Fix 1: Dual Scorecard — Channel Scorecard (>40% retention on >3min, >3% CTR, >1k views) + Product Scorecard (≥10 True Regulars, ≥5 live attendees, paid upgrades).
  • Fix 2: Decoupled Video Production — 60–180s Reverse Response Marketing shorts in aug-video-animation-2; deep curriculum taught live unscripted on Sundays.
  • Fix 3: Aligned CTA — Direct viewers to "Join our Sunday Live Screen-Share Lab on Skool to debug Claude enterprise architecture scenarios in real-time" (skool-about.html).
  • Fix 4: FDE Contractor Transformation — 4-stage pipeline (delivery-pilot-roadmap.html) targeting £500–£1,000/day day-rate potential and tender co-bidding (H30) over cheap dumps.
  • Fix 5: Continuous Customer Discovery — Skool 3-Question Intake (skool-membership-questions.html) + post-Sunday debriefs (cohort-session-8-analysis.html).
