🎯 Sales Tactics

A concise, action-oriented catalog of direct sales and conversion tactics deployed across YouTube, LinkedIn, and Skool. Each tactic addresses a specific stage of viewer friction — turning passive traffic into active community members and qualified cohort candidates without heavy corporate sales cycles.

Fast-Conversion Shorts (60s Sales Engine)

4-beat video structure benchmarked on YouTube Short TzjDPMfsb3o: 0–3s Pattern Interrupt Hook → 4–35s High-Density Proof / Live Demo → 36–52s Low-Friction Skool CTA (founder voice) → 53–60s Infinite Audio Loop.

Tactic 2 · Direct-to-Lens

Face-to-Face Core Message (NetworkChuck Pattern)

Speak directly to the audience with zero production shield (Chuck Keith pattern). State the one uncomfortable practitioner thesis face-to-face, then flip to active listening (H29).

"Don't blame your degree. Keep educating yourself, raise your circle, and become a Forward Deployed Engineer."

Tactic 3 · Content Arbitrage

Reverse Response Marketing (MVP Engine)

Identify trending AI/developer topics on YouTube, flip the conventional premise on its head, inject contractor reality ($1.3M+ contracting insights), and route viewers into Skool.

"Everyone tells you to learn Prompt Engineering. Here's why prompt writers get laid off while Delivery Pilots get £800/day."

Tactic 4 · Diagnostic Trigger

Private Quiz Assessment Lead Magnet

Engineers buy when they uncover a diagnostic deficit (Brian's discovery law). A 3-minute quiz exposes exam blind spots and routes directly to the relevant course/cohort.

"Take the 3-minute diagnostic test to benchmark your real exam readiness before buying your Pearson VUE voucher."
🕵️ Open Private Quiz Tactic
Tactic 5 · Engagement Tokens

Funfair Token System (Weekly Expiry)

Audience earns time-based tokens for attending Sunday, commenting, and referring. Tokens expire weekly (no fake countdown timers) and are spent inside the fair on sample Memory Cards and Mock Exams.

"Tokens minted Sunday 13:30 expire next Sunday. Use your tokens to unlock this week's practice exam sample."
🎡 Open Funfair Tokens SOP
Tactic 6 · Video Outro

Skool Walkthrough End-Card Card

Every long-form YouTube video dedicates its last 20 seconds to a live walkthrough of the Skool Classroom, showing viewers exactly where to download the discussed architecture diagram.

"Click the video card right on your screen to jump inside our free Skool community and grab the full unredacted PDF."

Tactic 7 · Hard Contrast

The Cost of Inaction (Samuel Leeds Formula)

Explicitly names the financial cost of waiting: A hard number (£600/day contract) + contrarian claim + closing deadline + cost of silence.

"You can spend 3 weeks guessing why Pearson VUE tests fail on scenario questions, or audit your code with us this Sunday."

Tactic 8 · Frictionless Bridge

1-Click Pinned Comment Asset Drop

The top pinned comment on every Short and long-form video provides the direct 1-click link to the exact prompt template, GitHub scaffolding, or Sunday live room.

"📌 [PINNED] Complete repository code & Sunday live screen-share access link: https://www.skool.com/delivery-pilot-8938"
