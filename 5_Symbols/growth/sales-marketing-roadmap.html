📚 The 7-Cohort Curriculum Sequence

As of 2026-08-12, seven cohort classrooms are configured in Skool. The sequence runs from the broadest top-of-funnel topic (Cohort 1) to the narrowest, most advanced topics (Cohorts 6 & 7) — mirroring the funnel itself: wide audience for discovery, narrowing to the highest-intent, highest-retention members.

# Cohort Title Funnel Role Sells Against
1 Foundations: AI Market & Certification Top of funnel — the free Sunday cohort entry point, the widest audience. $0 free tier / $10/mo membership
2 Agentic Architectures & Token Economics Mid-funnel — retains members past the intro topic into technical depth. $10/mo membership
3 Multi-Agent Patterns & Multimedia Learn Mid-funnel — builds toward the hands-on architecture problem-solving that live cohorts sell. $10/mo membership / $250 cohort
4 Claude Code CLI & Personal Automation Practitioner-tier — hands-on tooling that differentiates from pure theory content. $250 cohort
5 Certification, RAG & Guardrails Exam-readiness core — the closest cohort to the actual Claude Certified Architect blueprint domains. $250–$500 cohort, $29 bundle
6 Forward Deployed Engineering Bottom of funnel — targets the highest-paying, fastest-growing role named in H6's SAM evidence (FDE postings up ~729% YoY). $500 cohort / B2B (H12)
7 Second Brain Retention & advocacy — a personal-knowledge-management capstone for graduates, keeping them active after certification. $500 cohort / membership retention

🎯 How the Sequence Maps to the Funnel

📺 Marketing (Cohorts 1–2)
YouTube videos and free Sunday cohort sessions draw on Cohort 1–2 material — broad, low-commitment topics that build the top-of-funnel audience described in Sales Pipeline.
🏫 Sales (Cohorts 3–5)
Skool's $10/mo membership and $250–$500 live cohorts are sold against the hands-on, exam-adjacent material in Cohorts 3–5, where the actual willingness-to-pay decision (H5) gets tested.
🔁 Retention & Expansion (Cohorts 6–7)
Forward Deployed Engineering and Second Brain retain graduates past certification and open the door to the multi-cert, B2B expansion tracked in H14 and H12.
