🤝 Skool Affiliate Launch Timeline & Milestone Gates

Strategic roadmap and readiness criteria for opening the Skool Affiliate & Referral Program. Aligned with Steve Blank's Customer Development framework: affiliate distribution is postponed during early discovery to avoid incentivizing artificial traffic, unlocking only after paid retention and repeat validation gates are proven.

🗺️ 4-Stage Affiliate Activation Pathway

G1 · Validation (Active)

1. 10 True Regulars

Free community engagement & retention baseline

  • Establish ≥10 active members participating weekly (10 Regulars)
  • Weekly Sunday room attendance > 5 members
  • Affiliate Status: ❌ Closed (Focus on unprompted organic word-of-mouth)
Estimated: Aug – Oct 2026
G2 · Private Beta

2. 50 Paid Members

Internal Peer Referral Pilot

  • Prove paid willingness-to-pay ($1/mo → $10/mo)
  • 30-day member retention > 80%
  • Affiliate Status: 🟡 Private Beta (Active certified alumni only — seat credits or 1 free month per invite)
Estimated: Nov 2026 – Q1 2027
G3 · Official Launch

3. $10k Revenue Gate

Customer Creation Stage 3 Kickoff

  • $10,000 USD cumulative cohort revenue validated (H9)
  • Repeat paid enrollment mechanics verified
  • Affiliate Status: 🚀 Full Public Launch (30% recurring on $10/mo membership + 20% on $250 cohorts)
Estimated: Q2 2027
G4 · Scale Organisation

4. $100k ARR Scale

Company Building & Partner Network

  • $100k ARR delegation threshold reached (Company Building)
  • Dedicated TA / Community Coordinator manages partner payouts
  • Affiliate Status: 🏆 Super-Affiliates (B2B corporate trainers, AI YouTubers, APN consultants)
Estimated: 2028+

⚠️ Why Affiliate Opening Is Strictly Postponed (The Steve Blank Rule)

1. Distorts Problem-Solution Fit Signals
If cash bounties are offered before the curriculum itself proves it helps engineers pass the Pearson VUE exam, members join for affiliate kickbacks rather than genuine skill acquisition. This muddies retention metrics (H2, H8).
2. Founder Bandwidth Protection
Managing affiliate tax forms, payout queries, and tracking attribution drains founder hours needed for weekly YouTube animation (H4) and Sunday live delivery (H5) during our second-job constraint (Bandwidth Ledger).
