Tactic 2 · Micro-Commitment

The "Question 1 in Comments" Discussion Trap

End the video content not with a conclusion, but with a controversial scenario question taken directly from the official Anthropic exam blueprint:

On-Screen Callout: "Would you use Bedrock Guardrails or native Anthropic Tool Call regex validation for this latency constraint? Drop A or B in the comments — the full code solution is pinned in the top comment."

Why it works: Drives immediate comment engagement (activating YouTube algorithmic recommendation H28) and routes 100% of comment readers to the pinned Skool link.

Tactic 3 · Visual Bridge

The "Blurred Cheat Sheet" Mid-Video Reveal

At the exact halfway mark of the video (minute 2:30–3:00), display the complete multi-branch architecture mindmap, with 3 high-leverage exam traps visibly blurred with a padlock icon.

Visual cue: Small animated pop-up banner: "🔓 High-Res Uncensored Mindmap PDF in Skool Classroom."

Why it works: Creates an open loop while retention is highest, preventing end-of-video drop-off.

Tactic 4 · Channel Page Routing

The "Channel Trailer" Auto-Play Alignment

Set the YouTube Channel Featured Trailer (for non-subscribers) to the exact 60-second animated Skool community overview.

Why it works: When viewers click your avatar from any video, the channel banner (Canva Banner Spec) and the trailer immediately pitch the free Sunday cohort and resource vault.

Tactic 5 · Community Tab Previews

Sunday Lab Screenshots on YouTube Community Tab

Every Monday morning, post a cropped screenshot of Sunday night’s live screen-share troubleshooting session on the YouTube Community Tab:

Post Copy: "Sunday 13:30 UK Lab Teardown: How we fixed a 429 Rate Limit cascade across 4 Claude subagents. Replay and code templates uploaded to Skool Classroom for all members. Join next Sunday’s screen-share room at [Link]."
Tactic 6 · Weekly Install List

"Cohort Prep" Setup Video as Prerequisite

Publish a 3-minute video on Friday afternoons: "Install These 3 CLI Tools Before Sunday 13:30 UK." Direct attendees to the step-by-step checklist on Cohort Prep inside Skool.
