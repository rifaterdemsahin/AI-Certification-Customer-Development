🛑 1. LinkedIn 30,000 Connection Limit & Skool Gating Policy

LinkedIn imposes a hard, non-negotiable ceiling of 30,000 1st-degree connections on all personal member profiles. Once a creator reaches this ceiling, LinkedIn disables standard connection requests, allowing external users only to "Follow" the profile.

For a creator-led business, unrestricted acceptance of cold, random connection invites fills the connection budget with low-intent contacts, permanently blocking genuine students and cohort members from obtaining 1st-degree messaging access, mutual endorsements, and feed priority.

✕

The Unrestricted Trap

Accepting incoming invites indiscriminately fills the 30,000 slots with automated bot spam, cold sales reps, and passive followers who never engage or join the community.

🛡️

Preserving the Gap

Leaving a deliberate connection buffer ("gap") by routinely filtering invites and clearing inactive connections, guaranteeing that high-intent community joiners always have open slots.

✓

Skool-Only Acceptance

Only accepting connection requests from confirmed members in the Delivery Pilot Skool Community. Connecting becomes an explicit member benefit.
