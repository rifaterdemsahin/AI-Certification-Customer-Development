📅 Stage Timeline Estimates

quality-gates.html defines what clears each Steve Blank stage; this page estimates when. These are the founder's own projections, built from the pace already observed (the 8-week free Sunday cohort, the 3-videos/week production baseline) and the single-founder bandwidth ceiling — not measured outcomes. Nothing below is a promise or a deadline; it is a falsifiable estimate, tracked as H19 in the Hypothesis Tracker and due for revision at every real milestone (first paid cohort launch, $10k gate clearance, etc.).

🗺️ Timeline Overview

The dashed/lighter segment of the Customer Creation bar marks the widest uncertainty band on this page — the 1,000x subscriber-growth target (H13) has no historical compounding rate to project from yet. Company Building is drawn as open-ended (arrow, not a closing edge) because $100k ARR triggers an ongoing structural transition, not a fixed end date.

⚠️ Why These Are Estimates, Not Commitments

🧑‍💻 Single-founder bandwidth ceiling
Every stage above is produced and delivered by one person working a second job (see Single-Founder Bandwidth). There is exactly one live delivery slot a week, which caps how fast any stage can move regardless of demand.
📈 Stage 3 has no compounding precedent
The 1,000x subscriber target (H13) has zero data points logged so far — the Q4 2028–2029 range on Creation is a placeholder band, not a projection from an observed growth curve.
🚪 The Stage 2 gate can slip
H9's repeat gate requires 2 consecutive paid launches to combine to $10k. If the first launch underperforms, a 3rd cycle is needed, directly pushing the Q2 2027 exit and every date after it.
🔁 Delegation could compress Stage 4's ramp
Handing weekly audience follow-up/cohort-prep reminders to a hired Cohort Support Coordinator (see Company Building) frees founder time once Stage 4 is reached — a lever that could shorten later timelines but isn't reflected in the chart above since it only applies after the $100k ARR trigger.

⚠️ Sanity Check: Missing Questions & Risks

Missing Questions:

  • What is the actual pace of a paid cohort launch cycle (marketing lead time to seats filled), versus the free cohort's 8-week precedent?
  • Should this page log real dates as each milestone actually happens, turning the chart into an actuals-vs-estimate comparison?

Key Risks:

  • Treating these estimates as commitments could create pressure that pushes toward the removed "guarantee" framing (see H8) applied to timing instead of outcomes.
  • A slipped Stage 2 gate cascades through every later date, since Stage 3 and 4 start dates are both defined relative to it, not as fixed calendar targets.
