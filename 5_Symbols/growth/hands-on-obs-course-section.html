🔄 The Strategic Evolution: Why This Section Was Created

Old Model: Rigid Calendar 1-1 Workshop Slots

Previously, three 2-hour 1-1 workshop slots (Beginner Mon 7pm, Intermediate Tue 7pm, Advanced Fri 7pm) were pinned to the public calendar. This introduced major friction:

  • Timezone Mismatches: Fixed 7pm–9pm London slots excluded US East and Asia-Pacific working schedules.
  • Repetitive Setup Burden: Founder spent valuable 1-1 time repeatedly debugging baseline Docker installs and API keys.
  • Calendar Clutter: Cluttered the community calendar with 17 weekly entries that required manual rescheduling.

New Model: Daily OBS Codification + On-Demand 1-1 Booking

Founder Rifat Erdem Sahin decoupled self-paced technical learning from synchronous consultative pairing:

  • Codified Daily in OBS: Baseline setups and advanced architecture patterns are recorded daily and published to the classroom for 24/7 self-paced viewing.
  • Frictionless 1-on-1 Booking: Members book 1-on-1 calls simply by sending a direct message or leaving a comment to agree on a custom mutual time.
  • High-Leverage 1-1 Calls: Live pairing focuses on deep enterprise architecture, tender co-bidding, and confidential IP protected under mutual NDA.

📐 The Three Leveled Hands-on Paths

Every member starts where they are. Recordings in each track show authentic practitioner terminal screens, live code editor setups, configuration files, and troubleshooting steps without fluff or artificial cuts.

🔰 Beginner Level Hands-on

Targeted at non-coders, students, founders, and junior analysts getting their hands dirty with modern AI tooling for the first time.
