🎡 Where Shorts Sit in Priestley’s Five Steps (see H32)

Shorts are product for prospects — the easy first step. CHAOS is 30–150 conversations. Sunday is the intro workshop. VIP 1:1 is the sales meeting. The core offer is Share Screen / $10/mo, not another clip.

1. Short form (<60s)
Daniel Priestley: 30–60s, pain / prize / news, every day if bandwidth allows. Here: 3 glossary shorts/week on the train (Gemini → Flow → Canva). Job is notice, not the exam. Each Short must point at a course video or Sunday — never at another Short as the only next step.
2. Course format
YouTube Courses / sequenced playlists (H7 Premise 1). One term in a Short becomes one module in a playlist. This is Mehmet’s “3dk course” after the “1k short.” Searchable, rewatchable, the asset that ranks after the swipe.
3. Long form + CTA
Priestley 7-Eleven: ~7 hours of long-form, 11 shorts, 4 platforms. Signature 15–60 min, then slice. Conversion lives here and in the lead form — on this business that form is Skool (Peek $0 / Sit In $1 / Share Screen $250/yr) and Sunday, not a comment keyword.

Priestley’s own line: shorts drive discovery; signature content converts. Shipping 300 shorts with no course playlist and no Sunday ask is the formula with the converting layer cut off. Flywheel page: Growth Flywheel. Format placement: Short vs Long Form.

  • 📉Wrong intent. 64.7% of Shorts views are External + Shorts feed. That audience is swiping, not sitting a Pearson VUE exam. H4’s 1% view→$10/mo was never specified for Shorts feed traffic.
  • 🔗No converting layer. Priestley converts on long form + lead form. This catalog has one glossary Short with 34 views and zero attributed Skool joins. Volume without a course playlist and a Sunday ask is unpaid entertainment.
  • ⏳Bandwidth. 300 × 1 commute hour = 300 hours. That is Stage 3 catalog scale (H13 1,000x subs), not the Stage 2 $10k repeat gate (H9). Sunday and paid seats still move Validation. See Single-Founder Bandwidth.
  • 🎯What to keep. 3 glossary shorts/week as the commute SOP. Each Short ends on a course video or the group URL (skool.com/delivery-pilot-8938, not /live/). Do not pause Sunday to fill a 300-clip backlog.

✅ Stage 2 Operating Rule

Use Shorts to test language and hooks. Use course videos to teach. Use Sunday and Skool to convert. Count a Short as Validation evidence only when a named viewer joins Skool or shows up Sunday from that URL.

Until that happens, 224 channel views and 34 Short views stay H28 / H2 quality signals, not H5 paid-enrollment proof. Funnel arithmetic: Funnel Math.
