🔍 Why Top-of-Profile Custom Links Are Behind a Paywall

1. Creator Mode Retired

Previously, LinkedIn users could toggle on Creator Mode for free to place a clickable website URL directly underneath their headline. When LinkedIn retired Creator Mode, that free toggle was permanently removed.

2. Moved to Custom Button Paywall

The top header link was replaced with the Custom Button feature, restricted exclusively to LinkedIn Premium accounts (Business, Sales Navigator, Recruiter Lite). Only legacy accounts grandfathered during Creator Mode retain it for free.

🆓 Free Ways to Market Links on Your Profile (Validation Phase)

While paid Customer Creation tasks are postponed, Rifat can leverage these three free placement tactics on a standard LinkedIn account to drive traffic to Skool with zero monthly spend:

Option 1 · Best Visibility

🌟 Featured Section

Creates a large, clickable card with a visual preview image near the top of the profile.

  1. Go to profile → Add profile section
  2. Select Recommended → Add Featured
  3. Click + → Add a link
  4. Paste skool.com/delivery-pilot-8938
Option 2 · Standard Contact

📇 Contact Info

Embeds the link inside profile contact details.

  1. Click Pencil icon on top profile card
  2. Scroll down → click Edit contact info
  3. Under Website → click Add website
  4. Paste URL & select "Company" or "Portfolio"
Option 3 · Contextual Link

📝 About / Experience

Direct URL text in summary descriptions for warm readers.

  • Add clean URL in About section copy
  • Add dedicated experience block for AI Certification Helper
  • Include Sunday cohort invite text in summary
