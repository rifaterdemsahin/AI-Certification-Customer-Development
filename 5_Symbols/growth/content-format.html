🎬 Format: Short Form & Long Form

Where each format lives — YouTube, LinkedIn, and Skool — and why. This page places three production types: animated videos, workshops, and course-based videos. Channel order is already decided on YouTube / LinkedIn → Skool Handoff; this page decides the shape of the asset on each channel. See H2, H7, H4, and H5.

📌 The Rule in One Line

Short form finds people. Long-form courses teach at search scale. Workshops happen live, inside Skool.

YouTube
Public discovery + sequenced courses
LinkedIn
Short clips, proof, invite — not the LMS
Skool
Full course + live workshops + billing
Never
Full live workshop as a public YouTube show

⏱️ Short Form vs Long Form

Short form — under ~90 seconds
One idea, one hook, one next step. Vertical or square. Built to interrupt a feed, not to pass an exam. First measured proof on this business is an animated short at 68.5% average view retention (H2). Job: reach + curiosity + a click toward a long video, a newsletter, or Skool.
Long form — 8–25 minutes, or a sequenced playlist
One exam topic or one workshop-length problem, taught in order. Horizontal, searchable, rewatchable. H7 Premise 1: structured “YouTube Course” playlists rank for educational search better than one-off clips. Job: teach enough that the viewer trusts the next paid step — not dump the whole paid classroom in public.

Short form is not a dumbed-down course. Long form is not a Short that overran. They have different jobs. Mixing them on the wrong channel wastes founder hours (see Single-Founder Bandwidth) and muddies H4 / H7 measurement.

🧩 The Three Production Types

✨ Animated videos

Distilled concept explainers (guardrails, RAG, multi-agent, exam topics). The format this business is testing as its differentiator — H2 / H10 >40% catalog retention. Comes in both short and long cuts from the same storyboard.

🛠️ Workshops

Live, hands-on, screen-share problem-solving. Sunday 9pm is the current slot. Value is peer accountability and real-time setup, not a polished edit — H5 / H8. Prep loop: install list during the week (Cohort Prep).

📚 Course-based videos

Sequenced lessons that map to a classroom path (the 7 Skool classrooms: Foundations → Second Brain). Public version = YouTube Course playlists. Paid version = Skool Classroom with files, mocks, and replays. See Sales & Marketing Roadmap.

🧠 Rationale, Channel by Channel

YouTube — search + recommendation
Candidates already search “mock exam” and concept names here. Shorts feed the recommendation graph; long Course playlists feed search intent (H7). Public hosting is free; the algorithm is the CAC. Putting the live workshop here would (a) skip the $0-ad test of whether people will pay to be in the room (H5) and (b) expose student screens. Track retention and engagement on YouTube Channel Metrics (H28).
LinkedIn — professional proof loop
Rifat's existing professional audience lives here. The channel's job is social proof (badge posts, recaps) and a second capture point for the newsletter (H3 dual capture). Native short video outperforms a YouTube link in-feed. Long courses and live workshops do not belong here: LinkedIn is not an LMS, comments are batch-answered weekly (second-job constraint), and a 20-minute lesson dies in a professional scroll.
Skool — delivery, not discovery
Decided on Skool vs. YouTube and the handoff page: Classroom, forum, calendar, billing. This is where the full course and the live workshop earn the $10 / $29 / $250–$500. Using Skool as a discovery channel (cold Shorts, public live) fights the platform: no search graph, founder-only board is expected noise until warm invites land. See Skool Posting Sanity Check.

🚫 Do Not Put

  • 📺The live Sunday workshop on public YouTube. Recaps yes; the room no. The paid offer is live screen-share + community, not a promised exam outcome (H8 Premise 3).
  • 💼A full course on LinkedIn. Module posts and clips only. LinkedIn is the invite and the badge loop.
  • 🏫Discovery Shorts as the Skool growth plan. Skool does not replace YouTube search. Warm-invite first.
  • ✂️Rebuilding the same lesson three times. Storyboard once (animated long). Cut a Short. Post the native LinkedIn clip. Drop the master into Skool Classroom. One production hour, three surfaces.
  • 📦The mock / question bank in public long form. That is the $29 bundle and the paid Classroom (H21, H27).
