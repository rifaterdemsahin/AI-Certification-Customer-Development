🌐 Inbound Channel Qualification Playbooks

1. Skool Membership Intake Gate

Gate 1

Every applicant to the Skool community must answer 3 mandatory intake questions (see spec). If any question is blank or spammy, membership is declined immediately.

Intake Questions:
1. What is your current technical role and cloud/AI experience level?
2. Which certification or enterprise outcome are you preparing for in the next 30–60 days?
3. Can you commit to at least 3 hours/week of hands-on practice labs?

2. LinkedIn DMs & 30k Cap Triage

Gate 2

Because Rifat's LinkedIn is capped at 30,000 connections (see LinkedIn 30k Cap), incoming DMs are batch-answered weekly. Connection requests without verified Skool email are rejected.

Qualification Auto-Reply:
"Thanks for reaching out! Because my network is at capacity, I only connect with active peers building hands-on AI architectures in our Skool cohort. Join us at skool.com/delivery-pilot — introduce yourself there and let's build together on Sunday!"

3. YouTube Comments Weekly Triage

Gate 3

Commenters asking deep architectural questions or expressing certification exam anxiety are qualified via pinned comment links to the free Jab-Jab package (see Jab-Jab package).

Triage Script:
"Great catch on the vector retrieval latency. We broke down the full production code repo for this in Sunday's lab. Grab the free architecture template and join the Sunday live lab in our Skool description link."

4. Enterprise & Partner Referrals

Gate 4

Corporate teams looking to certify 10+ engineers or enterprise partners seating candidates (see Cheuk National Grid finding F12) are qualified on team size, Pearson VUE voucher sponsorship, and delivery timeline.

Enterprise Criteria:
• Minimum 5 engineers per cohort
• Dedicated partner sponsor / voucher provision
• Custom enterprise repository sandbox setup

🚫 Disqualification Scripts (Hormozi Anti-Admittance)

When a lead is disqualified, we deliver an immediate, polite, but firm redirection. This protects community quality, reinforces exclusivity, and prevents time waste.

Scenario A: Asking for Dumps or Exam Leaks

"We strictly teach hands-on architecture engineering. Pearson VUE and Anthropic proctored exams evaluate applied judgment, not memorized dumps. If you want dumps, this community is not for you. Check out our public YouTube channel for conceptual primers."

Scenario B: Asking for Uncapped Free 1-on-1 Time

"As an active contractor operating enterprise engagements, Rifat's 1-on-1 calendar is strictly reserved for Delivery Pilot VIP members during Monday coworks. You are welcome to join our Sunday group lab or ask questions asynchronously in our Skool hubs."
