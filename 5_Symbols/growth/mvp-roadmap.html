📈 Five growth stages (community-driven)

Stage 1 · Customer Discovery
Reach the audience via popular videos (YouTube / LinkedIn reverse-response shorts). See H2, H4.
Stage 2 · Skool enrollment
Successful bridge into the community (Peek $0 / Sit In $1 / Share Screen $250/yr experiment). See H5.
Stage 3 · Customer Validate
Certification of community members via partner-gated Pearson VUE seating (F12). Practice + live labs — no pass guarantee. See H8.
Stage 4 · Customer Create
Transition to independent contractors — Delivery Pilot persona, Monday VIP Claude Cowork, recruiter promotion. See H30.
Stage 5 · Company Building
Long-term project partnership: co-bid tenders, alumni as Delivery Pilots. See H22, H11.

🛠️ August 2026 operating plan

Taken from the bottom of the source image and the v7 Excalidraw clipboard. This is what to do this month.

🎬 Studio & product polish

  1. Organize equipment and light the set.
  2. Clean the workspace (physical + content folders).
  3. Product UI refinement (icons, buttons, classroom hubs) — not new announcement posts.

📞 Cohorts & engagement

  1. Group support and attendance tracking (Sunday lab).
  2. VIP: Sunday screen-share + Saturday calendar 1-1 tracks (Environment / AI Security / HITL) — capped, not on-demand.
  3. Sell on the phone: 1:1 VIP discovery calls convert Peek/Sit In into Share Screen.

📜 Claude course

  1. Content refinement (clarity and structure).
  2. Create “pass the certs” practice materials (memory cards, mocks) — H27.
  3. Keep exam seating partner-gated (F12); D2C acquires, partners seat.

  1. Define and refine the contractor persona (FDE, £500–£1,000+/day).
  2. Tracking system for personas and member progression.
  3. Backup: partnership@deliverypilot.net.
