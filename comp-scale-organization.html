Scale Organization Spec

  • Component Name: Organizational Scaling Strategy
  • Associated Stage: 4. Company Building
  • Hiring Threshold: $100,000 Annual Revenue
  • Owner: Rifat Erdem Sahin
  • Core Target: Delegate production, operations, & 1-1 support
  • Current Status: Hypothesized

Hiring Blueprint & Responsibilities

Reaching $100k annual run-rate triggers the shift from product development to scale organization. To prevent burnout and maintain student support quality, three specific roles are introduced:

ROLE 1

Freelance Video Editor

Takes raw scripts, audio recordings, and visual storyboard assets from Rifat to render high-quality animated tutorials.

  • Responsibility: Animation, editing, sound, rendering.
  • Benefit: Cuts Rifat's video creation time by 80%.
ROLE 2

Cohort Coordinator

Manages student scheduling, processes registrations, moderates community chat channels, and coordinates event calendars.

  • Responsibility: Administrative management, email ticketing.
  • Benefit: Ensures cohort runs smoothly as class sizes scale.
ROLE 3

1-1 Technical Support

Employs top graduates from previous Claude AI Architect cohorts part-time to help new students work through code bugs.

  • Responsibility: Technical code reviews, discord help.
  • Benefit: Delivers premium 1-1 help without bottlenecking Rifat's time.

Scale Operational Flow

Phase 1: Founder-Led (Current to $10,000/mo)

Rifat runs all research, editing, marketing, teaching, and support.

Phase 2: Hybrid Transition ($10k/mo to $100k ARR)
