What This Business Does

A one-page reference answering "what is this?" in plain language — the elevator pitch, not the framework. For the structured Problem/Solution/Market/Traction breakdown, see Business Plan Summary instead; this page exists as a standalone citation for anyone who needs the whole business explained in under a minute.

🎬 The One-Line Pitch

Get Certified in AI. Shape the Future.

Animated YouTube videos that teach AI/cloud certification content, funneling free viewers into a $10/mo prep membership and $250–$500 live cohort bootcamps aimed at the Claude Certified Architect – Professional exam.

Run as a second job by founder Rifat Erdem Sahin, who works a contract role alongside it — YouTube and LinkedIn comments are batch-answered in weekly cycles rather than in real time. The exam itself is issued solely by Anthropic via Pearson VUE; this business never issues the certification and makes no claim to guarantee a pass — see H5 and H8. Canonical slogan and 20-second pitch: Slogan & Elevator Pitch.

🎯 Who It's For

Technical professionals who feel AI is reshaping their job expectations faster than they can retrain, and who find existing certification prep material (500-page manuals, 40-hour courses) too dry to finish. See H1 for the demand evidence and Target Audience for the full persona breakdown.

💰 How It Makes Money

📺 Free YouTube Courses
Animated videos, distributed via organic YouTube search and Rifat's LinkedIn network — $0 ad spend. Top of the funnel; builds trust and traffic.
💳 $10/mo Prep Membership
Recorded cohort replays and community access for viewers who convert past free content.
🎓 $250–$500 Live Cohorts
Day-long, live-streamed problem-solving bootcamps with screen-share participation and scheduled breaks.

🚫 What This Is Not

Not a certification issuer — Anthropic alone grants the Claude Certified Architect credential via Pearson VUE. Not a guarantee of passing the exam — that language has been deliberately removed site-wide; the value proposition is live cohort practice and community, not a promised outcome. Not a full-time operation (yet) — see Single-Founder Bandwidth.
