🔍 Stage 1 — Customer Discovery

🟡 Partially Validated

H1 · Rising AI skills expectations

P1: Rapid AI advancement is reshaping job roles and technical expectations. P2: Employers expect staff to build agentic chains, configure guardrails, and audit LLM architectures. Conclusion: Demand for AI/LLM certification is structurally rising, not a fad. Status: Corroborated by WEF 2025 Skills Data and Claude Partner Network Tiers, but no direct search-volume experiment run yet.
🧪 In Testing

H2 · Animated content format

P1: Standard cert-prep material causes cognitive fatigue and low completion. P2: Animated, visually distilled explanations increase retention. Conclusion: An animated video format will outperform standard slide decks on watch retention. Status: Target is >40% watch retention on animated lessons vs. standard slide decks. FORMAL MVP metric.
📅 Planned

H3 · Audience will pay for cert prep

P1: Viewers who complete free content have expressed problem-level need. P2: A subset of highly engaged viewers will convert to paying customers if offered a low-friction next step. Conclusion: Newsletter signup with visible pricing will surface real purchase intent. Status: Target: >5% email signup conversion from a newsletter page that shows pricing up front.

📈 Stage 2 — Customer Validation

📅 Hypothesized

H4 · ~1% free→paid conversion

P1: Free YouTube content builds trust and traffic at scale. P2: A fraction of viewers will value the $10/mo community enough to subscribe. Conclusion: Out of every 1,000 free-course views, at least 10 viewers will join the $10/mo membership. Status: Untested. No view or membership subscription data reported in the repository yet.
📅 Hypothesized

H5 · Cohorts sell out organically

P1: Cohort announcements reach an engaged audience via video and newsletter calls to action. P2: The audience is willing to pay $250–$500 for live-streamed, real-time practice & community (no pass guarantee). Conclusion: 20–40 students will enroll per cohort launch with $0 ad spend. Status: Untested. This is the basis of the $10,000 revenue validation gate.
⚠️ Claimed, Unverified

H6 · TAM/SAM/SOM market size

P1: ~25M developers worldwide must adapt to AI engineering (TAM). P2: ~3M of them actively seek certifications (SAM). Conclusion: ~50k developers are reachable via our channels within 3 years (SOM). Status: Grounded in WEF and cloud proxy data (see McKinsey study on AI Labor Shifts), but remains a working estimate.
📅 Hypothesized

H7 · Funnel conversion rates

P1: Structured "YouTube Course" playlists rank higher for search intent. P2: This ranking translates into a predictable click funnel. Conclusion: 10% video→newsletter CTR, 2% newsletter→cohort conversion. Status: Untested. Has not been numerically reconciled with H4's 1% view conversion.
📅 Hypothesized

H8 · Exam-ready PMF

P1: The cohort curriculum covers the full scope of the certification in real time. P2: Live instruction and peer accountability outperform self-study. P3: The company does not guarantee passing the exam (issued by Anthropic). Conclusion: Cohort graduates will pass the target exam at a high rate and recommend the program. Status: Target is >80% internal pass-rate, NPS >50. (See the official Claude Certified Architect Program by Anthropic via Pearson VUE, active at $175).
📅 Planned

H12 · B2B / consulting channel

P1: Consulting firms must certify staff to hit partner tiers (Claude Partner Network). P2: These firms prefer bulk-buying cohort seats over individual expensing. Conclusion: Direct corporate outreach to firms will yield high-value bulk sales. Status: Planned. Next step is direct outreach to 3–5 target firms.

⚡ Stage 3 & 🏢 Stage 4

✅ Decided

H9 · $10k exit gate & job status

P1: Gross revenue is the strongest signal of customer validation. P2: Stage 2 must produce $10,000 in revenue before starting Stage 3. P3: All validation work is done solely by the founder for agility. Conclusion: Hitting $10,000 USD is the exit gate. The project stays a second job for the founder. Status: Decided. Exit gate is defined, and keeping primary job is a firm decision.
🧪 In Testing

H10 · >40% video retention

P1: Viewers staying engaged past 30 seconds absorb the material. P2: Consistent catalog-wide retention reflects video quality. P3: The MVP targets visual clarity on the core certification prep problem. Conclusion: >40% average retention viewed across published lessons confirms video quality. Status: In Testing. Demoted to a quality signal rather than a Stage 3 exit gate.
📅 Planned

H13 · 1,000x subscriber growth

P1: Customer Creation (Stage 3) is about demand generation and audience scale. P2: Channel subscriber growth is a compounding signal of demand creation. Conclusion: Each video moves the channel toward 1,000x its baseline (~30 → ~30k subscribers). Status: Planned. Primary metric for Stage 3, tracked incrementally per video.
📅 Hypothesized

H11 · $100k ARR triggers hiring

P1: Administrative tasks consume critical founder time at scale. P2: $100k ARR generates sufficient margin to support delegation. Conclusion: Hitting $100k ARR triggers hiring a video editor, cohort coordinator, and TAs. Status: Hypothesized. Needs cost model to confirm margin viability.
