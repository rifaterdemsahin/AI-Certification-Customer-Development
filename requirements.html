Functional & Non-Functional Requirements

MoSCoW-prioritized requirements for the three systems the business depends on: the content pipeline, the cohort platform, and payment/certification-progress tracking. Requirements are scoped to what each customer development stage actually needs, not a fully-built platform on day one — see Focus for what is deliberately deferred.

🎬 Content Production Pipeline

Must Have

  • ✅Script → storyboard → animate → voice → publish workflow the founder can run solo in evenings/weekends (detailed in the Canva Animation Plan).
  • ✅YouTube "Courses" tab structuring for each certification track.
  • ✅Per-video retention analytics (YouTube Studio) to track the >40% MVP quality floor.
  • ✅Topics scoped against the official exam blueprint — see Exam Topic Blueprint for the full Associate/Architect topic lists this content is built to cover.

Should Have

  • 🔷Reusable animation asset/template library to cut per-video production time (modeled in Canva design board).
  • 🔷A subscriber-growth log per publish date to track H13's 1,000x metric.

Could Have

  • 🔶A contracted editor for post-production (deferred until $100k ARR, see Key Resources).

Won't Have (yet)

  • ⛔In-house animation studio or full-time production staff.

🎓 Cohort Platform

Must Have

  • ✅Live video/streaming tool for real-time problem-solving sessions.
  • ✅A community space (chat/forum) for peer accountability between sessions — see Cohort Prep for the weekly install-list/content-preview loop this actually runs.
  • ✅Cohort enrollment intake form capturing name, email, payment, and self-reported exam target date.

Should Have

  • 🔷Recorded session archive so students who miss a live slot can catch up.
  • 🔷Structured curriculum tracker mapped to the official exam blueprint.

Could Have

  • 🔶Dedicated cohort-management software instead of spreadsheets + calendar invites (deferred until Hire 2, see Company Building).

Won't Have (yet)

  • ⛔A custom-built LMS. Off-the-shelf tools are sufficient at current scale.

💳 Payment & Certification-Progress Tracking

Requirement Priority Notes
Accept one-time cohort payments ($250–$500) and $10/mo subscriptions Must Off-the-shelf processor (e.g. Stripe/YouTube Memberships); no custom billing engine needed yet.
Log cumulative cohort revenue toward the $10k Stage 2 exit gate Must Feeds the Quality Gates dashboard directly.
Track self-reported exam-pass outcomes for the >80% internal pass-rate metric Should The company never issues the certification itself — Anthropic does, via Pearson VUE. This is an internal quality signal only.
Refund/dispute handling workflow Should Manual at current volume (20–40 students/cohort); delegated to Hire 2 at scale.
