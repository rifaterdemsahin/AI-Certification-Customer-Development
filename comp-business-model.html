Validation Strategy

To validate our pricing structure, we apply Steve Blank's recommendation: "Test pricing early using landing pages before committing to heavy custom development."

Hypothesis 1: The YouTube Funnel converts at 1%
Out of every 1,000 views on our free YouTube course, at least 10 viewers will sign up for our $10/mo membership.
Hypothesis 2: Cohorts sell out organically
By announcing cohorts directly in our videos and free newsletters, we will attract 20 to 40 premium students per launch without running paid ads.
