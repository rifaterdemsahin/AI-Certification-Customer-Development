## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Context

In customer discovery feedback captured following her live cohort attendance, **Anna** (Ukrainian professor and founder of the UWISE community in Cambridge) delivered crucial pedagogical critique on pacing, duration, and cognitive overload:
1. **The Friction Point:** The previous 2-hour unstructured technical deep-dives are *"too complicated, too quickly, too much information at once."* A 2-hour duration on Sunday evening causes severe mental fatigue, making it impossible for learners to maintain focus.
2. **The "5-10-5" Methodology:** Anna recommended a concrete, modular 20-minute pedagogical cadence:
   - **5 Minutes:** Clear explanation and live demonstration of how to execute a single task.
   - **10 Minutes:** Attendees execute the task on their own machines, with real-time instructor assistance and debugging.
   - **5 Minutes:** Targeted Q&A and clarification.
3. **Session Length & Scope Cap:** Exactly **3 tasks per session** (3 &times; 20 min = 60 minutes max total duration). Capping sessions at 40–60 minutes ensures high retention, eliminates burnout, and leaves attendees feeling successful.
