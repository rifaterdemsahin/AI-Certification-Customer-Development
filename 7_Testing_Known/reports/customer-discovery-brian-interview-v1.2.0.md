## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Context

Brian, an active multi-session community member, surfaced two related points about the audience and the public message:

1. **Not everyone wants to be a builder.** Brian's own analogy: *"Not everyone, even engineers, wants to build their own house — people are lazy, they would get a mortgage and delegate."* Applied to AI certification and skills-building, a meaningful share of the audience — including technical people — would rather pay for a done-for-you outcome than grind through self-directed learning. This is not a new persona; it reinforces Brian's own earlier "Service vs. Learning" framing from `3_Simulation/Interviews/interview_brian_2026-08-20.md`: *"Service is 'Pay me and I will build it for you.' Learning is 'We go through the gears together step-by-step so I learn how to build it myself.'"*
2. **The public message needs to be hopeful.** Brian's explicit ask: *"The core message should give hope and possibility."* Framing the product primarily around exam difficulty, technical grind, or fear of falling behind risks alienating the delegation-minded segment described above. The public-facing line should lead with aspiration.
