🎓 The PMF Program: Claude AI Architect Professional Prep

Program Value Proposition

We validate Product-Market Fit when our student cohort program successfully trains professionals to pass demanding AI certifications.

The flagship exam blueprint is the Claude AI Architect Professional. This certification checks a student's ability to orchestrate Claude model calls, configure latency parameters, design multi-agent pipelines, enforce prompt caching, and design enterprise security.

📦 Curriculum Deliverables:
We don't just provide practice questions. The cohort program includes:
  • 💻Interactive code templates demonstrating advanced system prompt caching.
  • 🗂️Visual flashcards detailing Claude API parameters (temperature, top_p, top_k).
  • 🔴Live-streamed weekly sessions where the community solves real-world prompt/architecture scenarios together in real time, building study discipline and a peer network — not a promised exam outcome.

🚦 PMF Validation Gates

🎯 >80% Pass Rate of cohort students who attempt the official exam.
💬 NPS score >50 indicating students would actively recommend the program.
🏅 LinkedIn Badges Organic social sharing of certification badges tagging Rifat's program.

These are internal metrics we track to measure our own program quality — not a guarantee made to students. The certification itself is granted solely by Anthropic via its Pearson VUE-proctored exam.
