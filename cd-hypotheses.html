📋 Why State Hypotheses First

Steve Blank's Customer Discovery starts indoors: before a single customer conversation, the founder writes down every assumption the business depends on as a falsifiable hypothesis. This turns "I think this will work" into something that can actually be proven wrong — which is the entire point. For AI Certification Helper, these six hypotheses cover what the product is, who it's for, how it reaches them, how it's priced, how demand compounds, what kind of market this is, and who else is competing for the same attention.

Each hypothesis below is cross-referenced against the live Hypothesis Tracker (H1–H13) so nothing here contradicts the site's single source of truth.
