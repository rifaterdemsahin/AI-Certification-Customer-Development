Validation Experiments & Financial Model

Sales & Model Repeatability

Customer Validation proves that there is a repeatable, scalable sales model and that customers will actually purchase our products. Rifat Erdem Sahin's model operates on a hybrid freemium and premium cohort strategy.

1. The Free Funnel (Acquisition)
High-quality animated technical tutorials are made available on YouTube. Using the YouTube courses feature, structured tracks are presented clearly to convert casual search traffic into regular students.
2. Monthly Preparation Membership
For $10/month, users gain access to exclusive practice exam simulations, detailed explanations, cheatsheets, and live study groups to prepare for certifications such as the Claude AI Architect Professional.
3. Premium 4-8 Week Cohorts (High Ticket)
Structured, fast-paced cohorts that feature live 1-on-1 support, hands-on architectural design sessions led by Rifat, and group accountability projects. The target is $10,000 in gross sales per cohort course created.
