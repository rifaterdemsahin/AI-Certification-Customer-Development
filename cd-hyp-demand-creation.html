🔎 What This Is

The Demand Creation Hypothesis states how awareness and demand compound over time — the mechanism that turns one satisfied customer into the next ten prospects, rather than restarting from zero for every video.

🎯 Applied to AI Certification Helper

Hypothesis: Each published video should move the channel meaningfully toward 1,000x its starting subscriber base, compounding through YouTube's own recommendation/search surfacing plus a repeatable content → community → content flywheel.

  • 🔁Mechanism visualized in Flywheel.
  • 📈This is H13 in the Hypothesis Tracker: 1,000x subscriber growth per video, baseline ~30 subscribers.
  • 🖱️Funnel click-through assumptions in Funnel & Channels Spec (H7).
🚦 What Would Falsify This
Flat or declining subscriber growth across several consecutive videos would mean demand isn't compounding — the channel is starting from zero every time instead of building an audience.
