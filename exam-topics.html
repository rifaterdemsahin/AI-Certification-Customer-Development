🎓 Associate (Foundations) — 19 Topics

Mastery percentages below are real data from one candidate's actual exam result — see Candidate Exam Performance Evidence. They studied official materials only, not our content, so these numbers describe the exam's difficulty distribution, not our curriculum's effectiveness.

💬 Prompting & Iteration

  • 67%Create effective prompts for business and technical tasks
  • 100%Apply task decomposition techniques to structure complex requests
  • 100%Iterate prompts to improve output quality
  • 100%Adapt prompting strategies based on task type (analysis, research, drafting, brainstorming)

✅ Evaluation & Validation

  • 75%Evaluate Claude-generated outputs for accuracy and completeness
  • 100%Identify hallucinations, inconsistencies, and biases in responses
  • 100%Apply fact-checking and validation techniques
  • 100%Determine when human review or additional verification is required

🗂️ Output Formats & Product Features

  • 0%Organize and curate information for optimal outputs; select appropriate output formats (artifacts for code/documents, inline for conversational responses, structured formats for data) — the confirmed curriculum gap, see below.
  • 100%Select appropriate Claude product features (projects, research mode, chat, artifacts)

🧠 Model Selection & Context Management

  • 100%Differentiate between Claude model types (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus)
  • 100%Align model selection with task requirements (cost, speed, quality)
  • 100%Understand and manage context limitations/memory: when to restart, summarize, or persist

💼 Business Application & Workflow

  • 67%Apply Claude to analyze requirements and use cases
  • 100%Use Claude to support solution design, development, and iteration
  • 100%Integrate Claude into existing workflows to augment or redesign them
  • 100%Communicate Claude's value and limitations to stakeholders

📁 Projects & Knowledge Configuration

  • 100%Configure Claude Projects with instructions and knowledge sources
  • 100%Manage uploaded knowledge and connectors (Google Drive, Gmail)

🏛️ Architect (Professional) — 30 Topics

This is the primary flagship exam this business is built around (see Focus). No mastery data exists yet — no real candidate result has been logged against this specific list. Grouped here into content-planning clusters for curriculum sequencing.

🏗️ Solution Design & Architecture

  • ▫️Translate business problems into Claude-based AI solutions
  • ▫️Design end-to-end architectures (input → processing → output → feedback loops)
  • ▫️Select appropriate architectural patterns (workflow, agentic, augmented LLM)
  • ▫️Apply decomposition techniques for complex problem solving
  • ▫️Align solutions to business value pillars (efficiency, transformation, productivity, cost, SLAs)

🧩 Model & Prompt Engineering

  • ▫️Select appropriate Claude models based on trade-offs
  • ▫️Design system prompts, templates, and guardrails
  • ▫️Apply prompt engineering techniques (zero-shot, few-shot, chain-of-thought, etc.)
  • ▫️Evaluate tool/agent configuration for capability bloat

🔐 Security, Compliance & Ethics

  • ▫️Analyze authentication and authorization requirements to identify security gaps
  • ▫️Ensure compliance with regulations (GDPR, HIPAA, FedRAMP)
  • ▫️Address ethical AI considerations (bias, fairness, transparency)

⚡ Performance, Retrieval & Integration

  • ▫️Evaluate accuracy-latency tradeoffs and justify configuration decisions
  • ▫️Analyze observability challenges and select monitoring strategies at scale
  • ▫️Apply retrieval strategies matched to data shape and query pattern
  • ▫️Evaluate connection protocols and select the appropriate integration mechanism
  • ▫️Optimize token usage, latency, and cost-performance trade-offs

🧪 Evaluation & Testing

  • ▫️Define evaluation metrics (accuracy, latency, cost, safety, security)
  • ▫️Design evaluation datasets and test frameworks using a mix of testing methodologies
  • ▫️Conduct A/B testing and iterative improvements
  • ▫️Implement guardrails and safety controls
  • ▫️Apply human-in-the-loop validation strategies

🤝 Stakeholder & Lifecycle Management

  • ▫️Conduct structured discovery and requirement gathering
  • ▫️Communicate architectural decisions and trade-offs
  • ▫️Manage stakeholder feedback loops and expectation alignment (including SLAs)
  • ▫️Document architectures and provide implementation guidance
  • ▫️Support lifecycle phases (discovery, design, handoff, monitoring, iteration)

🛠️ Team Enablement & Developer Tooling

  • ▫️Configure Claude tools and environments for teams (e.g., Claude Code)
  • ▫️Improve developer workflows using AI-assisted tooling
  • ▫️Support debugging and operational issue resolution

🎯 How This Feeds Curriculum Priority

1. The confirmed 0% gap first
Output-format/UI-feature routing (Associate) is the one topic with real evidence of being under-covered by official materials — the clearest, most defensible first video/memory-card topic to produce.
2. Architect clusters, in MVP order
Solution Design & Architecture and Model/Prompt Engineering are the largest clusters (5 and 4 topics) and the most directly testable against MVP's "does it work" bar — natural first targets once Associate-topic content is seeded.

This blueprint doesn't change H10's >40% MVP retention target or H13's Stage 3 metric — it's a content-sequencing tool, not a new hypothesis. See Requirements for the technical spec these topics get built into, and Self-Assessment for how a prospect gets routed toward the right track.
