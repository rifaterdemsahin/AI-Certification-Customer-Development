🔎 What This Is

The Customer & Problem Hypothesis names exactly who has the problem, and states why it's painful and urgent enough that they'll change behavior (or pay) to fix it — as opposed to a problem people merely acknowledge exists.

🎯 Applied to AI Certification Helper

Hypothesis: Working professionals and engineers who feel pressure from rapidly rising AI/cloud skill expectations are the customer, and the current cert-prep alternatives (500-page manuals, 40-hour dry video courses) are painful enough to cause real drop-off.

  • 👥Customer profile detailed in Target Audience.
  • 😖Problem framing detailed in Problem-Solution Fit Spec.
  • 📊Corroborated externally by H1 (Claude Partner Network practitioner-tier quotas, WEF 2025 skills data) in the Hypothesis Tracker.
🚦 What Would Falsify This
If problem interviews (Step 2) surface polite agreement instead of visceral frustration — "yeah that's annoying" rather than "I've been looking for exactly this" — the problem isn't urgent enough to build a business on.
