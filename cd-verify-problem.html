🔎 What This Is

Verifying the problem means confirming the underlying pain point is real and widely shared across the target audience, not a preference voiced by one or two vocal early contacts.

🎯 Applied to AI Certification Helper

Applied: Consistent qualitative signal across problem interviews (Step 2) corroborated by external research already cited for H1 — Claude Partner Network practitioner-tier quotas and WEF 2025 skills-gap data.

  • 📚Full sourcing: Problem-Solution Fit Spec and Hypothesis Tracker (H1).
🚦 Pass / Fail
Pass: prospects across multiple, independent conversations describe the same pain unprompted. Fail: the problem only resonates with a narrow slice — revisit the Customer & Problem Hypothesis.
