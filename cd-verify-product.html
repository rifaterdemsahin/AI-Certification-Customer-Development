🔎 What This Is

Verifying the product means confirming the MVP format itself is validated with real, repeated usage data — not a single good result, but a consistent pattern across multiple published pieces of content.

🎯 Applied to AI Certification Helper

Applied: >40% average watch retention held across multiple videos in the catalog, not just the first one or two (H10, confirmed as the MVP-stage quality floor).

  • 🎬Full metric definition: MVP Spec.
  • 🚦Restated with the other three stage gates in Quality Gates.
🚦 Pass / Fail
Pass: retention holds >40% across the catalog. Fail: retention is inconsistent or trending down — revisit the Product Hypothesis (Step 1) before proceeding.
