🎬 Show, Don't Describe

Step 2 tested whether the problem is real. Step 3 tests whether this specific product solves it — by actually showing the MVP (a published animated video, a real pricing page) to prospects and observing what they do, not just what they say. Opinions are cheap; watch-time, click-through, and signups are not.

For AI Certification Helper this means the first published lessons under MVP Spec and the pricing details behind Value Proposition become the actual test instruments.
